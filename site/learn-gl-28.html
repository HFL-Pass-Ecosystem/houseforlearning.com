about this course

What you will be able to do

Business Coaching Practice: Coach two peers through a goal using a structured method.

  • Understand Explain who needs Business Coaching Practice and name one real first user, with evidence from your own market scan.
  • Apply Build and run the practice task: Coach two peers through a goal using a structured method.
  • Analyse Record every step, cost and problem, then compare the plan with what really happened.
  • Evaluate Judge your result against your goal using numbers, and write three lessons learned.
  • Create Present your evidence to a review room and give two peers written feedback.
  • Create Plan your next 90 days and contribute one template or worked example back to learners.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Coach two peers through a goal using a structured method.

Condition

About 60 hours over 1-3 months. Own tools, free spreadsheet and form tools, and a real practice partner. Mentor and peer circle available. Currency written on every number.

Standard

Evidence reviewed by a mentor; at least 70% on the 10-question check; two written peer reviews given; every number carries its currency and unit.

Who it is for, requirements and materials

  • Who it is for: learners aged 16 and over, small-business starters, workers moving to a new field, and teams (see B2B, B2C, D2C).
  • Requirements: finish P2P Lending Platform Concepts first, or show equivalent work.
  • Materials included: lesson text, worked examples, case exercise, 10-question check, templates in My page.
  • Teaching team: a trainer from our network, a mentor who reviews your evidence, and a peer circle. Names are given at enrolment.

lesson by lesson

9 modules, in the order you do them

Module 1 Who needs this, and who is your first user IdeaStudy · 2 hUnderstand

Goal: name one real first user: two peers who each have one real business goal.

  1. Market scan: Interview 3 people who run small businesses. Ask what they would want help with and what would make them trust a coach. Note the words they use.
  2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
  3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.

Evidence you keep: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

Module 2 Kick-off circle IdeaShare and mentoring · 3 hUnderstand
  1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
  2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
  3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).

Evidence you keep: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

Module 3 Start the practice task ActionPractice · 14 hApply

Task steps:

  1. Write a one-page coaching agreement: purpose, number of sessions, confidentiality, what coaching is not (advice, therapy, legal help).
  2. Agree a goal with each of two peers in plain words, with a measure and a date.
  3. Run session 1 with each using a simple structure: goal, reality, options, way forward.
  4. Write private session notes (initials only) and two follow-up actions each.

Evidence you keep: Coaching agreement and session 1 notes.

Module 4 Short modules ActionStudy · 2 hApply
  • Coaching models: a coach asks questions and the client finds the answers. Use a simple four-step flow: goal, reality, options, way forward. Ask open questions that start with what or how.
  • Ethics: keep what you hear confidential, tell the client the limits, never give legal, medical or financial advice, and stop and refer if someone is in distress.
  • Contracting: agree session length, number of sessions, fee (if any, with currency), cancellation, and how you will know it worked.

Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

Module 5 Continue and log ActionPractice · 14 hApply

Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.

  1. Run sessions 2 and 3. Begin with the actions from last time.
  2. Ask each peer to rate the session 1 to 5 and say one thing to change.
  3. Record what you did well and what you want to improve.

Evidence you keep: Session notes and feedback.

Module 6 Review circle ValueShare and mentoring · 6 hEvaluate
  1. Share one moment you got stuck, with no names.
  2. Peers suggest a better question you could have asked.
  3. Write one habit to practise next week.

Evidence you keep: Reflection page.

Module 7 Finish and measure ValuePractice · 14 hEvaluate
  1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
  2. Measure the result against your goal: goal progress reported by the client, number of actions completed, client rating, share of questions that were open.
  3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.

Evidence you keep: a before and after table and the decision note.

Module 8 Review room EmpowermentShare and mentoring · 3 hCreate
  1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
  2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
  3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.

Evidence you keep: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

Module 9 Check and next step RegenerationStudy · 2 hCreate
  1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
  2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
  3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).

Evidence you keep: check result and the 90-day plan.

practice material and assessment

Worked examples, case, check and local law pointers

Worked examples
  1. Goal in plain words: 'Get 5 paying customers for my tailoring by 30 November.' Measure: signed orders.
  2. Four-step flow: Goal: what exactly by when? Reality: what have you tried, what happened? Options: what else could you do? Way forward: which one, starting when?
  3. Open question rewrite: 'Don't you think you should advertise?' becomes 'What could you do to reach more people?'
  4. Action log: peer A did 3 of 4 actions; blocker: no photos of work; next step: one photo session.
Case exercise

A peer says: 'I want to grow but I'm scared of losing my current clients.' Write the next four questions you would ask, spot one moment where advice would be wrong, say what you would put in the contract about limits, and describe when you would suggest the peer speak to another professional.

Check questions (draft set, 10)
  1. What is the difference between coaching and advice? 2. Name the four steps of the flow. 3. Give two open questions. 4. What belongs in a coaching agreement? 5. When must you stop and refer? 6. Why use initials only in notes? 7. What does confidentiality cover and not cover? 8. How do you know coaching worked? 9. What would you change in your second session? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

Rules for this course

No clinical or therapy work. No personal data of clients in shared files. Peers take part freely and can stop at any time. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
