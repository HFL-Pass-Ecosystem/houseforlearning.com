flagship course

Marketing Copywriting

Write clear, honest copy for ads, pages, emails and posts that moves readers to act.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L4 Practitioner8 modules · 32 h lessons plus practice

Write clear, honest copy for ads, pages, emails and posts that moves readers to act.

  • Define audience and goal for a text.
  • Apply headline and structure rules.
  • Analyse a rival text.
  • Evaluate claims for truth.
  • Create a copy portfolio.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Produce five pieces of marketing copy with tests and a short portfolio.

Condition

About 8 weeks part-time. A computer, sample products, a mentor.

Standard

Copy is true, clear, edited, and each piece has a goal and a test.

Who it is for: Writers, marketers, students. Requirements: Good command of the language. Materials: lesson text, templates, rubric, check with answers.

lesson by lesson

8 modules with worked examples

Module 1 Know the reader 3 hDefine
  • Reader, need and fear.
  • Words they use.
  • Goal for the text.
Worked example. Reader: parent of a teenager worried about exams.

Activity. Write a reader sheet.

Standard to meet. Sheet has need and words.

Module 2 Headlines 4 hApply
  • Clear before clever.
  • Benefit and number.
  • Test variants.
Worked example. Learn maths in 30 days.

Activity. Write ten headlines.

Standard to meet. Headlines are clear.

Module 3 Structure 4 hApply
  • Problem, solution, proof, action.
  • Short sentences.
  • Scannable headings.
Worked example. Lead with the problem.

Activity. Draft a page.

Standard to meet. Structure followed.

Module 4 Voice and tone 4 hCreate
  • Plain words.
  • Active voice.
  • Respectful.
Worked example. We help you save time.

Activity. Rewrite a text.

Standard to meet. Plain and active.

Module 5 Ads and emails 5 hApply
  • Short ads.
  • Subject lines.
  • Calls to action.
Worked example. Subject: Your free guide is ready.

Activity. Write an email.

Standard to meet. Single call to action.

Module 6 Truth and law 4 hEvaluate
  • No false claims.
  • Disclose offers.
  • Respect rules on health and money.
Worked example. Replace guaranteed results with typical results.

Activity. Audit a text.

Standard to meet. Claims are provable.

Module 7 Edit and test 4 hAnalyse
  • Cut words.
  • Read aloud.
  • A/B test.
Worked example. Version B gets more clicks.

Activity. Edit and test.

Standard to meet. Changes recorded.

Module 8 Capstone: portfolio 4 hCreate
  • Five pieces.
  • Notes.
  • Present.
  • Revise.
Worked example. A portfolio of five.

Activity. Present.

Standard to meet. Each piece has goal and test.

assessment

How you are checked

Evidence: the work product from each module, kept in one folder with dates. A mentor and two peers mark it with this rubric. Pass: no criterion at 'Not yet'.

Marking rubric
CriterionNot yetMeetsExceeds
ClarityMissing or unclearMeets the stated checkClear and short
TruthfulnessGuessingEvidence keptProven claims
RecordsGapsCompleteComplete and reviewed by a peer
Safety and ethicsOverlookedFollowedFollowed and taught to others

Ten-question check, with model answers

Q1. Why use reader words?

Model answer: Trust.

Q2. Name a headline rule.

Model answer: Clear before clever.

Q3. What is proof?

Model answer: Evidence such as results.

Q4. Why active voice?

Model answer: Direct and clear.

Q5. What is a call to action?

Model answer: The action you ask for.

Q6. Why avoid guarantees?

Model answer: They may mislead.

Q7. What is an A/B test?

Model answer: Comparing two versions.

Q8. What belongs in a portfolio?

Model answer: Samples with goals.

Q9. Why read aloud?

Model answer: To catch awkward lines.

Q10. Why one call to action?

Model answer: Clarity.

seven levels

Your ladder, L1 to L7

This course sits at L4 Practitioner. Each level adds to the one below.

  1. L1 Basic. Know basic writing.
  2. L2 Awareness. Explain audience.
  3. L3 Foundation. Write short copy.
  4. L4 Practitioner. Write campaigns.
  5. L5 Specialist. Lead content.
  6. L6 Advanced. Lead a copy team.
  7. L7 Super expert. Set voice standards.

ten locations and global

Your place

Local rules change licences, data handling, pricing words and names of bodies. Check them for your place before you act.

India (Bengaluru)

Language: English, Hindi, Kannada. Currency: INR. Data rule for what you publish: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Skills frame to cite: National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) and the national credit framework.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Language: Dutch, English. Currency: EUR. Data rule for what you publish: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Skills frame to cite: the Dutch qualification framework (NLQF) linked to the European Qualifications Framework.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Language: Arabic, English. Currency: AED. Data rule for what you publish: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection). Skills frame to cite: the emirate education and training regulators (for example KHDA in Dubai) and the national qualifications framework.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Language: Portuguese (Spanish and English support). Currency: BRL. Data rule for what you publish: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados). Skills frame to cite: the national technical-education catalogue and the S-system training bodies (SENAI, SENAC).

Canada (Toronto)

Language: English, French. Currency: CAD. Data rule for what you publish: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply. Skills frame to cite: the provincial colleges framework and the national credential-recognition guidance.

United States

Language: English, Spanish. Currency: USD. Data rule for what you publish: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13. Skills frame to cite: state and community-college credit systems.

Singapore

Language: English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil. Currency: SGD. Data rule for what you publish: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Skills frame to cite: SkillsFuture Singapore frameworks and the national credit system.

Australia

Language: English. Currency: AUD. Data rule for what you publish: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Skills frame to cite: the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) levels.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Language: Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English. Currency: HKD. Data rule for what you publish: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Skills frame to cite: the Hong Kong Qualifications Framework.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)

Language: Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic, English. Currency: GEL or ILS. Data rule for what you publish: the personal data law of the country where the learner lives. Skills frame to cite: the national qualifications framework of that country.

Cayman Islands (Caribbean)

Language: English. Currency: KYD. Data rule for what you publish: the Data Protection Act. Skills frame to cite: the local education and training framework.

Mexico (Central America)

Language: Spanish, English. Currency: MXN. Data rule for what you publish: the federal law on personal data held by private parties. Skills frame to cite: the national competency certification system and the education ministry levels.

Russian Federation

Russian Federation: HELD. A sanctions and licensing check by our lawyer must finish before any course is offered there. No localised version is published.
