the room where people learn by doing

Training zone

One room, built the same way on every campus: team tables that move, a wall you can write on from end to end, daylight, and a screen that simply works.

All campus spacesCampus builds

the room

Eight rules for the room

Training zone, room rules
PartRule
TeamsThree to six people at one team table. Four to five tables in a room.
Wall spaceSix to eight square metres of wall for each team, for posters, templates and sticky notes.
TablesAbout 1.0 m by 0.8 m, foldable, on wheels. Two make one team table. No heavy fixed tables.
ChairsLight, with a mesh back. Half on wheels, half that stack.
StandingOne high table in each break-out so a team can stand.
DaylightWindows, always. Blinds that darken the room for the screen.
PlenaryAn open area with movable chairs facing the main screen.
MoodNot formal. The room should invite people to move, talk and try.

fit-out

What the room is made of

No maker is named. Each part is ordered by its code group from the element library.

Training zone, fit-out
PartWhat we ask forCode group
Writable wallOne long wall, floor to ceiling, about 16 m by 2.6 m, finished so that it can be written on and wiped.CS-IN
Writable glassWhite back-painted glass, about 4 m by 2.6 m, magnetic, behind the main screen.CS-IN
Glass frontFull-height toughened glass, 12 mm, with a double glass door and the room name on a frosted band.CS-SH
Ceiling600 by 600 mm tiles on a standard grid, so services can be reached.CS-IN
LightDimmable 600 by 600 LED panels giving 500 lux on the tables.CS-SV
BlindsMotorised blackout blinds with one remote.CS-IN
Media wallA full-height white cabinet, about 8 m long, that holds the motorised screen, the equipment and storage.CS-FU
Refreshment cabinetA low white cabinet with a worktop, about 2.5 m long.CS-FU
Mobile cabinetsFive cabinets on wheels, 1.0 m wide, with a top that works as a bench for hands-on sessions.CS-FU
FloorCarpet tiles, with spare tiles kept for repairs.CS-IN
Skirting and paintWhite skirting and white matt paint on every other wall, floor to ceiling.CS-IN

technology

Screen, sound, power and network

Training zone, technology
PartWhat we ask for
Main screenA large 4K display on a stand with wheels, or a projector with a motorised screen about 2.4 m wide.
ConnectionA cable connection first, because it always works. Wireless sharing as the second way.
SoundCeiling speakers across the room and a sound bar at the screen, loud enough for video.
Team screensOne screen and one whiteboard or flip chart for each break-out.
PowerA socket box with power and data on every table, floor boxes under the team areas and double sockets along the walls.
NetworkWireless internet for the facilitator and every learner, and about forty wired data points.
CoolingSized by a heat-load calculation for a full room, with the thermostat inside the room.
ControlOne simple panel for light scenes, blinds and screen.

build order

From drawing to hand-over

A training zone inside an existing building is fitted out in about seven working weeks. Several steps run side by side.

Training zone, fit-out order
StepWorking days
Design and as-built drawings5
Approvals3
Site set-up and strip-out4
Ceiling grid, partitions and first-fix power and cooling7
Boarding, filling and second fix7
Glass measured, made and fitted10, alongside other work
Joinery: media wall and cabinets7
Paint, writable finish, carpet and skirting7
Lights, final fix, testing3
Snagging and hand-over3

Planning values for teaching and early design. A licensed local engineer and the local authority confirm every real project.

your task

Try it

Model this room at LOD 200 in the BIM academy. Place the four team areas, the plenary and the media wall, then count the tables, chairs and sockets from your model and compare them with the rules above.

Learning material, not a design or safety instruction. Real buildings are designed and signed off by licensed professionals under local rules.
