school course

Grade 03: Arabic

4 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L2Primary4 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Remember, Understand, Apply
  • Remember Read and answer questions on a short text.
  • Understand Tell nouns from verbs and use them in sentences.
  • Understand Describe a day and a trip.
  • Apply Write three connected sentences.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 03 Arabic, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 4 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

4 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Reading simple passages Weeks 1 to 9Remember

In this unit you work on 'Reading simple passages', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: read and answer questions on a short text. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Reading simple passages' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: read and answer questions on a short text, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: الولد يلعب في الحديقة. أين يلعب الولد؟ في الحديقة. (The boy plays in the garden. Where does the boy play? In the garden.) Method: Model run for 'Reading simple passages'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Read this short passage I wrote. أَنَا أُحِبُّ الْمَدْرَسَةَ. فِي صَفِّي عِشْرُونَ طَالِبًا. مُعَلِّمَتِي تَقْرَأُ لَنَا قِصَّةً كُلَّ يَوْمٍ. [I love school. In my class there are twenty students. My teacher reads us a story every day.] Read it twice, then underline the numbers.
  2. Step 2, Find the key words. الْمَدْرَسَةُ [school], صَفٌّ [class], طَالِبٌ [student], قِصَّةٌ [story], كُلَّ يَوْمٍ [every day]. Say what each sentence is about before answering. The first is about liking school, the second about the class, the third about the story.
  3. Step 3, Answer questions with a full sentence. كَمْ طَالِبًا فِي الصَّفِّ؟ [How many students are in the class?] فِي الصَّفِّ عِشْرُونَ طَالِبًا. [In the class there are twenty students.] مَاذَا تَقْرَأُ الْمُعَلِّمَةُ؟ [What does the teacher read?] تَقْرَأُ قِصَّةً. [She reads a story.]
  4. Step 4, Ask a when-question. مَتَى تَقْرَأُ الْمُعَلِّمَةُ الْقِصَّةَ؟ [When does the teacher read the story?] تَقْرَأُهَا كُلَّ يَوْمٍ. [She reads it every day.] Notice how the answer repeats words from the text, which proves you found it in the passage.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student answers the question كَمْ طَالِبًا فِي الصَّفِّ؟ with the single word كَثِيرٌ [many]. Correction: go back to the text; the number is عِشْرُونَ, so the full answer is عِشْرُونَ طَالِبًا. Guessing is not reading.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: answer who reads us a story and where is the class. Model answer: مُعَلِّمَتِي تَقْرَأُ لَنَا قِصَّةً. [My teacher reads us a story.] صَفِّي فِي الْمَدْرَسَةِ. [My class is at school.] Check you copied the spelling.

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Read and answer questions on a short text: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Unit 2 Nouns and verbs Weeks 10 to 18Understand

In this unit you work on 'Nouns and verbs', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: tell nouns from verbs and use them in sentences. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Nouns and verbs' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: tell nouns from verbs and use them in sentences, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: In 'Mira has a dog called Bruno', 'dog' is a common noun, 'Mira' and 'Bruno' are proper nouns and need capital letters. Method: Model run for 'Nouns and verbs'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Know what a noun is. الِاسْمُ [the noun] names a person, thing or place: وَلَدٌ [boy], كِتَابٌ [book], مَدْرَسَةٌ [school]. A quick test: you can put الْ before a noun, as in الْكِتَابُ [the book]. Collect five nouns from your bag.
  2. Step 2, Know what a verb is. الْفِعْلُ [the verb] shows an action. Past: كَتَبَ [he wrote], ذَهَبَ [he went], جَلَسَ [he sat]. Present: يَكْتُبُ [he writes], يَذْهَبُ [he goes], يَجْلِسُ [he sits]. You cannot say الْ before a verb.
  3. Step 3, Put them together. الْوَلَدُ يَقْرَأُ الْكِتَابَ. [The boy reads the book.] ذَهَبَتِ الْبِنْتُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ. [The girl went to school.] In the first sentence الْوَلَدُ and الْكِتَابَ are nouns and يَقْرَأُ is the verb.
  4. Step 4, Sort a list. قَلَمٌ، يَرْسُمُ، بِنْتٌ، أَكَلَ، شَجَرَةٌ. [pen, he draws, girl, he ate, tree] Nouns: قَلَمٌ، بِنْتٌ، شَجَرَةٌ. Verbs: يَرْسُمُ، أَكَلَ. Each noun can take الْ; the verbs cannot.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A child labels يَلْعَبُ [he plays] as a noun. Correction: it is a verb, because it shows an action and الْيَلْعَبُ is impossible. Another error is calling كُرَةٌ [ball] a verb; كُرَةٌ names a thing, so it is a noun.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write a sentence with the noun بِنْتٌ and the verb تَرْسُمُ [she draws], then name noun and verb. Model answer: الْبِنْتُ تَرْسُمُ شَجَرَةً. [The girl draws a tree.] Nouns: الْبِنْتُ، شَجَرَةً. Verb: تَرْسُمُ.

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Tell nouns from verbs and use them in sentences: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Unit 3 Daily life and the beach Weeks 19 to 27Understand

In this unit you work on 'Daily life and the beach', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: describe a day and a trip. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Daily life and the beach' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: describe a day and a trip, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: في الصيف الجو حار وفي الشتاء الجو بارد. أذهب إلى الشاطئ في الصيف. (In summer it is hot, in winter cold. I go to the beach in summer.) Method: Model run for 'Daily life and the beach'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Describe your morning in the present. أَسْتَيْقِظُ فِي السَّاعَةِ السَّابِعَةِ. [I wake up at seven o'clock.] أَغْسِلُ وَجْهِي. [I wash my face.] أَتَنَاوَلُ الْفُطُورَ. [I have breakfast.] أَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ. [I go to school.] Say them in order.
  2. Step 2, Describe the rest of the day. أَعُودُ إِلَى الْبَيْتِ. [I return home.] أَكْتُبُ وَاجِبِي. [I do my homework.] أَلْعَبُ مَعَ أَخِي. [I play with my brother.] أَنَامُ فِي السَّاعَةِ التَّاسِعَةِ. [I sleep at nine o'clock.]
  3. Step 3, Describe a trip to the beach in the past. ذَهَبْنَا إِلَى الشَّاطِئِ. [We went to the beach.] لَعِبْتُ بِالرَّمْلِ. [I played with the sand.] سَبَحَ أَخِي فِي الْبَحْرِ. [My brother swam in the sea.] كَانَ الْجَوُّ جَمِيلًا. [The weather was beautiful.]
  4. Step 4, Compare present and past. الْيَوْمَ أَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ. [Today I go to school.] أَمْسِ ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى الشَّاطِئِ. [Yesterday I went to the beach.] The present verb begins with أَ for I; the past verb ends with تُ for I.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A child says أَمْسِ أَذْهَبُ إِلَى الشَّاطِئِ. Correction: أَمْسِ [yesterday] needs the past, so say أَمْسِ ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى الشَّاطِئِ. Match the time word with the right verb form every time.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write about your morning and yesterday's trip, one sentence each. Model answer: أَسْتَيْقِظُ مُبَكِّرًا وَأَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ. أَمْسِ ذَهَبْنَا إِلَى الشَّاطِئِ وَلَعِبْتُ بِالرَّمْلِ. [I wake up early and go to school. Yesterday we went to the beach and I played in the sand.]

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Describe a day and a trip: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Unit 4 Writing short sentences Weeks 28 to 36Apply

In this unit you work on 'Writing short sentences', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: write three connected sentences. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Writing short sentences' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: write three connected sentences, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: هذا كتاب. هذه قطة. أنا أحب مدرستي. (This is a book. This is a cat. I love my school.) Method: Model run for 'Writing short sentences'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, A good short text has three connected sentences: what, then what, and why or how I felt. Plan before you write. Topic: a visit to the park. Sentence one says where, sentence two says what you did, sentence three says how you felt.
  2. Step 2, Write sentence one. ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى الْحَدِيقَةِ مَعَ أُسْرَتِي. [I went to the park with my family.] Begin with a clear verb and add a place. Put a full stop at the end of the sentence.
  3. Step 3, Write sentence two with a linking word. ثُمَّ لَعِبْتُ مَعَ صَدِيقِي بِالْكُرَةِ. [Then I played with my friend with the ball.] ثُمَّ means then. Other linking words are وَ [and] and بَعْدَ ذَلِكَ [after that].
  4. Step 4, Write sentence three with a reason. كُنْتُ سَعِيدًا لِأَنَّ الْجَوَّ كَانَ جَمِيلًا. [I was happy because the weather was beautiful.] لِأَنَّ means because. Now read all three together: ذَهَبْتُ... ثُمَّ... كُنْتُ سَعِيدًا...
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A child writes ذَهَبْتُ الْحَدِيقَةِ لَعِبْتُ سَعِيدًا. Correction: add إِلَى [to], a linking word and a reason, so the text reads clearly: ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى الْحَدِيقَةِ. ثُمَّ لَعِبْتُ. كُنْتُ سَعِيدًا لِأَنَّ الْجَوَّ جَمِيلٌ.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write three connected sentences about a trip to the market. Model answer: ذَهَبْتُ إِلَى السُّوقِ مَعَ أُمِّي. ثُمَّ اشْتَرَيْنَا الْفَاكِهَةَ. كُنْتُ سَعِيدًا لِأَنِّي أُحِبُّ التُّفَّاحَ. [I went to the market with my mother. Then we bought fruit. I was happy because I love apples.]

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Write three connected sentences: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)

Use CAD in money problems; deliver in English, French; personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

United States

Use USD in money problems; deliver in English, Spanish; personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Singapore

Use SGD in money problems; deliver in English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil; personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Australia

Use AUD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Use HKD in money problems; deliver in Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English; personal data rule: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)

Use GEL or ILS in money problems; deliver in Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic, English; personal data rule: the personal data law of the country where the learner lives; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Cayman Islands (Caribbean)

Use KYD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Data Protection Act; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Mexico (Central America)

Use MXN in money problems; deliver in Spanish, English; personal data rule: the federal law on personal data held by private parties; guardian contact for every learner under 18.
