Negotiate the package

Understand the whole package and use more than one offer wisely.

Presented in House for Learning's own words and format.

What to hold on to

  • A package is more than salary: bonus, benefits, growth, flexibility and notice period.
  • Research a fair range before you talk numbers.
  • More than one offer gives you choice and a calm voice, but always stay honest.
  • Agree in writing and thank everyone, including those you decline.

Words to own

Turn a card slowly with a tap, the Enter key, or by resting your pointer for a moment.

Do this now

List every part of your ideal package and mark which are must-haves and which are nice-to-haves.
