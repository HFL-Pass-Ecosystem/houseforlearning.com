school course

Grade 10: Hindi

4 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L4Secondary4 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Apply
  • Apply Write both.
  • Apply Write an article.
  • Apply Write a notice.
  • Apply Present on a topic.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 10 Hindi, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 4 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

4 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Formal and informal letters Weeks 1 to 9Apply

In this unit you work on 'Formal and informal letters', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: write both. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Formal and informal letters' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: write both, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: अनौपचारिक पत्र: दिनांक, संबोधन (प्रिय मित्र रवि), कुशल-क्षेम, मुख्य बात, समापन (तुम्हारा मित्र, अमन)। औपचारिक: सेवा में, प्रधानाचार्य महोदय; विषय: अवकाश हेतु प्रार्थना-पत्र; मुख्य भाग दो छोटे अनुच्छेदों में; आपका आज्ञाकारी शिष्य, नाम, कक्षा। Method: Model run for 'Formal and informal letters'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Compare the two kinds of letter. Informal: to a friend, greeting प्रिय [Dear], pronoun तुम, ending तुम्हारा मित्र [Your friend]। Formal: to an official, starts सेवा में [To], has a विषय [subject] line, greeting महोदय [Sir], pronoun आप, ending भवदीय [Yours faithfully]। Make a two-column chart.
  2. Step 2, Write an informal letter. Place and date on the right: दिल्ली, 5 नवंबर 2026. Then प्रिय सखी नेहा, सप्रेम नमस्कार [Dear friend Neha, warm greetings]। परीक्षा में प्रथम आने की बहुत बधाई [Many congratulations on coming first in the exam]।
  3. Step 3, Complete the informal body and ending. मुझे तुम पर गर्व है [I am proud of you]। अगले रविवार को हमारे घर मिठाई खाने आना [Come to our house next Sunday to eat sweets]। तुम्हारी सखी, ऋचा [Your friend, Richa]। The tone is warm and the sentences are short.
  4. Step 4, Write the formal letter. सेवा में, नगर आयुक्त महोदय [To, the Municipal Commissioner, Sir]। विषय: सड़क पर गड्ढों की मरम्मत हेतु [Subject: for repair of potholes on the road]। महोदय, सविनय निवेदन है कि हमारी गली में बड़े गड्ढे हैं [Sir, I humbly state that there are large potholes in our street]।
  5. Step 5, Finish the formal letter. गड्ढों से रोज़ दुर्घटनाएँ होती हैं [Potholes cause accidents every day]। अतः आपसे अनुरोध है कि शीघ्र मरम्मत कराएँ [So I request you to have them repaired quickly]। भवदीय, [your name], with date. Check you did not mix तुम into this letter.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write the subject line and closing for a formal letter asking the principal for new library books. Model answer: विषय: पुस्तकालय में नई पुस्तकें मँगवाने हेतु [Subject: for ordering new books for the library]। अतः कृपया नई पुस्तकें मँगवाएँ। धन्यवाद। आपका आज्ञाकारी शिष्य।

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Write both: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 2 Essay and article Weeks 10 to 18Apply

In this unit you work on 'Essay and article', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: write an article. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Essay and article' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: write an article, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: निबंध 'पर्यावरण की रक्षा': भूमिका (पेड़ और स्वच्छ हवा जीवन के लिए ज़रूरी हैं), मुख्य भाग (पेड़ लगाना, प्लास्टिक कम करना, पानी बचाना), उपसंहार (यह हम सबकी ज़िम्मेदारी है)। Method: Model run for 'Essay and article'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Separate an article from an essay. An article is written for readers of a newspaper or magazine and has a headline and byline. It argues or informs about a current topic in about 120 to 150 words. Choose the topic ऑनलाइन पढ़ाई: लाभ और सीमाएँ [online study: benefits and limits].
  2. Step 2, Write the headline and byline. Headline: ऑनलाइन पढ़ाई: सुविधा भी, चुनौती भी [Online study: convenience and challenge]। Byline: लेखक: आपका नाम [Writer: your name]। A headline is short, has a colon or a pair of ideas, and makes the reader want to continue.
  3. Step 3, Write a hook introduction. आज लाखों विद्यार्थी घर बैठे पढ़ाई कर रहे हैं [Today lakhs of students are studying sitting at home]। प्रश्न यह है कि क्या यह तरीका हर बच्चे के लिए उतना ही अच्छा है [The question is whether this method is equally good for every child]?
  4. Step 4, Write two body paragraphs. Benefits: समय की बचत और घर बैठे सुविधा [saving time and comfort at home]। Limits: खेल और मित्रों से मिलना कम हो जाता है, और इंटरनेट हर जगह नहीं मिलता [meeting friends and playing decrease, and internet is not available everywhere]। Give one example per paragraph.
  5. Step 5, Write the conclusion with a suggestion. संतुलन ही समाधान है [Balance is the solution]। कक्षा में पढ़ाई और ऑनलाइन साधन साथ-साथ चलें तो विद्यार्थियों को सबसे अधिक लाभ होगा [If class study and online tools go together, students will gain the most]। Keep the tone formal.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write a headline and an opening line for an article on सड़क सुरक्षा [road safety]। Model answer: Headline: सड़क पर सावधानी, जीवन की सुरक्षा [Care on the road, safety of life]। Opening: हर दिन सड़क पर छोटी-सी लापरवाही बड़ी दुर्घटना बन सकती है [Every day a small carelessness on the road can become a big accident]।

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Write an article: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 3 Advertisement and notice Weeks 19 to 27Apply

In this unit you work on 'Advertisement and notice', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: write a notice. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Advertisement and notice' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: write a notice, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: सूचना: विद्यालय का नाम, दिनांक, शीर्षक 'वार्षिक खेल दिवस', 40 शब्दों में समय और स्थान, नीचे नाम और पद। विज्ञापन: 'बिकाऊ साइकिल, लगभग नई, मूल्य 2,000 रुपये, संपर्क: ...' Method: Model run for 'Advertisement and notice'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Learn the notice format. Write the school name at the top (use [विद्यालय का नाम]), then the word सूचना [Notice], then the date, then a short heading, then the message, and finally the writer's post. A notice should stay within about 50 words and state who, what, when and where.
  2. Step 2, Write the top part. [विद्यालय का नाम] / सूचना / 10 दिसंबर 2026 / वार्षिक खेल दिवस [Annual Sports Day]। The date goes on the left, the heading in the middle. Check that 10 December 2026 is a Thursday, so the notice is given before the weekend.
  3. Step 3, Write the body. सभी छात्रों को सूचित किया जाता है कि वार्षिक खेल दिवस 18 दिसंबर 2026 को प्रातः 9 बजे विद्यालय मैदान में होगा [All students are informed that the Annual Sports Day will be on 18 December 2026 at 9 a.m. in the school ground]। इच्छुक छात्र 14 दिसंबर तक खेल शिक्षक को नाम दें [Interested students should give names to the games teacher by 14 December]।
  4. Step 4, Sign the notice with the post, not a chatty line: हस्ताक्षर: खेल प्रभारी [Signed: Games in-charge]। Count your words, since a notice that runs too long loses marks. The language is passive and formal, using किया जाता है [is done] and होगा [will be].
  5. Step 5, Compare an advertisement. An advertisement tries to attract readers, so it has a catchy heading, short lines and contact details. Example: पुस्तक मेला! [Book Fair!] अपनी पसंद की पुस्तकें पाइए [Find your favourite books]। 20 से 22 दिसंबर, नगर हॉल। संपर्क: XXXXX [contact number].
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write a notice that the school library will be closed on 25 November 2026 for stock-checking. Model answer: सूचना / 20 नवंबर 2026 / पुस्तकालय बंद रहेगा। सूचित किया जाता है कि 25 नवंबर 2026 को पुस्तकालय गिनती के कारण बंद रहेगा। हस्ताक्षर: पुस्तकालय प्रभारी।

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Write a notice: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 4 Oral presentation Weeks 28 to 36Apply

In this unit you work on 'Oral presentation', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: present on a topic. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Oral presentation' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: present on a topic, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: भाषण: 'आदरणीय अध्यापकगण और मेरे साथियो, आज मैं पुस्तकें पढ़ने के लाभ पर बोलूँगा।' फिर तीन बिंदु, एक उदाहरण और अंत में धन्यवाद। एक मिनट में बोलने का अभ्यास करें। Method: Model run for 'Oral presentation'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Choose a topic and a purpose. For स्वच्छ जल [clean water], the purpose is to inform. Plan three minutes, three key points and three or four slides or cards. List the points: why clean water matters, how we waste it, and what we can do.
  2. Step 2, Write a hook opening. क्या आप जानते हैं कि हम रोज़ कितना पानी बहा देते हैं [Do you know how much water we let run away every day]? सभी को नमस्कार [Hello, everyone]। आज मैं स्वच्छ जल पर बोलूँगा [Today I will speak about clean water]। A question draws attention at once.
  3. Step 3, Signpost your points so listeners can follow. पहला बिंदु, जल का महत्त्व [first point, importance of water]। दूसरा बिंदु, जल की बर्बादी [second point, waste of water]। तीसरा बिंदु, हमारे उपाय [third point, our actions]। Signpost words work like road signs for the ear.
  4. Step 4, Add evidence. एक बूँद प्रति सेकंड टपकने वाला नल लगभग 4 लीटर पानी रोज़ बहा देता है [A tap dripping one drop per second wastes about 4 litres a day]। Check: 86,400 seconds x 0.05 mL a drop = 4,320 mL, about 4.3 litres. That is about 1,577 litres a year.
  5. Step 5, Close and handle questions. तो आइए हम सब मिलकर पानी बचाएँ [So let us all save water together]। धन्यवाद [Thank you]। आपके कोई प्रश्न हों तो पूछिए [If you have any questions, please ask]। If you do not know an answer, say: मैं पता करके बताऊँगा [I will find out and tell you].
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write an opening question and one evidence point for a talk on पेड़ और पर्यावरण [trees and the environment]। Model answer: क्या आपने कभी पेड़ के नीचे ठंडक महसूस की है [Have you ever felt coolness under a tree]? पेड़ कार्बन डाइऑक्साइड लेते हैं और ऑक्सीजन छोड़ते हैं [Trees take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen]।

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Present on a topic: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

levels L1 to L7

Where this course sits on the ladder

Seven slides, one for each level. Step in at your level and move up; credits stack. A level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age.

Slide 1. L1 Basic: Greetings and first words

On the slide: L1 Basic · Greetings and first words

Notes: Greet people politely, say your name and name five family members in Hindi. Notes: teach नमस्ते (namaste, hello) and मेरा नाम ... है (mera naam ... hai, my name is ...) by ear; learners greet a partner and point to family photos while saying each word.

Slide 2. L2 Awareness: The Devanagari script

On the slide: L2 Awareness · The Devanagari script

Notes: Recognise the vowels and first consonants of Devanagari and copy them neatly under the top line. Notes: show how letters hang from a horizontal line, then write क (ka) with correct stroke order; learners find it in three printed words and read each one aloud.

Slide 3. L3 Foundation: Simple sentences and postpositions

On the slide: L3 Foundation · Simple sentences and postpositions

Notes: Read and write short sentences with verb at the end and use postpositions such as में (in) and पर (on). Notes: model किताब मेज़ पर है (kitaab mez par hai, the book is on the table); learners make five sentences about a classroom and draw each one.

Slide 4. L4 Practitioner: Daily conversation and tenses (this course)

On the slide: L4 Practitioner · Daily conversation and tenses

Notes: Hold a short conversation about daily life using present, past and future tenses with correct gender agreement. Notes: contrast वह जाता है (he goes) with वह जाती है (she goes); learners ask a partner what they did yesterday, then write six sentences marking each verb ending.

This course: Grade 10 Hindi is at this level. Its units include Formal and informal letters; Essay and article; Advertisement and notice; Oral presentation.

Slide 5. L5 Specialist: Reading, writing and register

On the slide: L5 Specialist · Reading, writing and register

Notes: Read a news article and write a formal letter of about 200 words, choosing between polite and casual forms. Notes: compare आप, तुम and तू (you, three levels of respect) in one dialogue; learners rewrite it for a teacher, a friend and a child.

Slide 6. L6 Advanced (degree level): Literature and linguistics

On the slide: L6 Advanced · Literature and linguistics

Notes: Analyse a Hindi poem or story for theme, form and vocabulary origins, and write a critical essay in Hindi. Notes: use a short poem; learners sort ten words into Sanskrit-derived, Persian-derived and English-derived, then argue in 500 words how word choice shapes tone.

Slide 7. L7 Super expert (postgraduate/expert): Research, translation and teaching

On the slide: L7 Super expert · Research, translation and teaching

Notes: Design a research or translation project on Hindi language or literature, justify the method and present findings to peers. Notes: pick a topic such as code-switching in advertising; collect forty examples, classify them, discuss limits of the sample, and have a fluent reviewer check every claim.

Notes: a level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age. Learners may move up or down a level by subject.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)
