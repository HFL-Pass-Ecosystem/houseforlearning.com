Where learning becomes work

The workplace joins people to real tasks. Every task has a clear condition and standard, so you know what good looks like and are recognised when you reach it.

Career pathwaysFor organisations

task, condition, standard

How work is organised

  1. Task

    A clear piece of work with a defined outcome.

  2. Condition

    The tools, time, safety and support available.

  3. Standard

    How good the result must be, so it can be checked fairly.

  4. Record

    Work done to standard is added to your record.

  • people

    Individuals

    Find tasks that fit your skills and grow your record.

  • teams

    Teams and organisations

    Build learning around real roles and see team-level insights only.

  • mentors

    Mentors and facilitators

    Guide others and be recognised for it.
