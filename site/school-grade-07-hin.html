Grade 07: Hindi

4 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L3Secondary4 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Remember, Apply
  • Remember Read and summarise.
  • Apply Use these in sentences.
  • Remember Read and explain.
  • Apply Write formal letters.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 07 Hindi, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 4 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

4 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Prose reading Weeks 1 to 9Remember

In this unit you work on 'Prose reading', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: read and summarise. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Prose reading' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: read and summarise, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: गद्यांश पढ़ें; मुख्य भाव एक वाक्य में लिखें; प्रश्नों के उत्तर गद्यांश के शब्दों में दें; एक उचित शीर्षक सुझाएँ। Method: Model run for 'Prose reading'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Read the passage I wrote. हमारे स्कूल के पीछे एक छोटा-सा बगीचा है। हर सप्ताह सातवीं कक्षा के विद्यार्थी वहाँ पौधों को पानी देते हैं और सूखे पत्ते हटाते हैं। [Behind our school there is a small garden. Every week seventh-grade students water the plants there and remove dry leaves.]
  2. Step 2, Read the rest. पिछले महीने लगाए गए टमाटर अब पकने लगे हैं। इस काम से विद्यार्थियों ने मेहनत और ज़िम्मेदारी का महत्त्व सीखा। [Tomatoes planted last month have now started ripening. Through this work the students learned the value of hard work and responsibility.]
  3. Step 3, Find the main idea and key points. Main idea: बगीचे की देखभाल से बच्चे ज़िम्मेदारी सीखते हैं [Caring for the garden teaches children responsibility]. Key points: पानी देना, पत्ते हटाना, टमाटर पकना. Mark these in the passage.
  4. Step 4, Write a summary. स्कूल के बगीचे की देखभाल सातवीं कक्षा के विद्यार्थी करते हैं। टमाटर पकने लगे हैं और बच्चों ने मेहनत व ज़िम्मेदारी सीखी। [Seventh-grade students look after the school garden. The tomatoes are ripening and the children learned hard work and responsibility.]
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student writes a summary as long as the passage and adds मुझे बगीचा बहुत अच्छा लगता है [I like the garden very much]. Correction: a summary is about one third as long, in your own words, with no opinion.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: answer what the students do every week and what they learned. Model answer: विद्यार्थी हर सप्ताह पौधों को पानी देते हैं और सूखे पत्ते हटाते हैं। उन्होंने मेहनत और ज़िम्मेदारी का महत्त्व सीखा। [Students water the plants and remove dry leaves every week. They learned the value of hard work and responsibility.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Read and summarise: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 2 Grammar: case, sandhi, and sentence parts Weeks 10 to 18Apply

In this unit you work on 'Grammar: case, sandhi, and sentence parts', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: use these in sentences. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Grammar: case, sandhi, and sentence parts' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: use these in sentences, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: संधि: विद्या + आलय = विद्यालय; सूर्य + उदय = सूर्योदय। कारक: 'राम ने फल खाया' में 'ने' कर्ता कारक का चिह्न है। Method: Model run for 'Grammar: case, sandhi, and sentence parts'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Case (कारक) shows the role of a noun using small words: ने, को, से, के लिए, का/की/के, में, पर. Examples: राम ने आम खाया। [Ram ate a mango.] माँ ने बच्चे को दूध दिया। [Mother gave milk to the child.] मैं पेंसिल से लिखता हूँ। [I write with a pencil.]
  2. Step 2, More cases. पेड़ से पत्ता गिरा। [A leaf fell from the tree.] सीता का भाई स्कूल में है। [Seeta's brother is in school.] मेज़ पर किताब है। [There is a book on the table.] Name each word and its role: का shows relation, में shows place.
  3. Step 3, Learn sandhi (joining of sounds). विद्या + आलय = विद्यालय [school], पुस्तक + आलय = पुस्तकालय [library], सूर्य + उदय = सूर्योदय [sunrise], महा + उत्सव = महोत्सव [grand festival]. Say each pair, then the joined word.
  4. Step 4, A consonant sandhi: सत् + जन = सज्जन [good person], उत् + नति = उन्नति [progress]. Break the word the other way too: विद्यालय = विद्या + आलय. Then find parts of a sentence: राम (doer) आम (object) खाता है (action).
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student writes मोहन ने रोटी खाया। Correction: मोहन ने रोटी खाई। because with ने the verb agrees with the object रोटी, which is feminine. Also write पुस्तक + आलय as पुस्तकालय, not पुस्तकआलय.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write a sentence with ने and का, then join देव + आलय. Model answer: सीता ने भाई का बस्ता उठाया। देव + आलय = देवालय [temple]. [Seeta lifted her brother's bag.] Check ने with the past verb.

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Use these in sentences: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 3 Poetry Weeks 19 to 27Remember

In this unit you work on 'Poetry', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: read and explain. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Poetry' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: read and explain, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: कविता की पंक्तियों में तुक खोजें (जैसे आम और शाम), कवि का भाव बताएँ और भावार्थ अपने शब्दों में लिखें। Method: Model run for 'Poetry'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Read the lines I wrote. दीपक जला है अँधेरी रात में, कहता है जग से, मैं अकेला नहीं हूँ। हर किरण मेरी दूसरों की राह है, जो बाँटता है उजाला, वही महान है। [A lamp burns in the dark night, saying, I am not alone. Each ray is another's path; one who shares light is great.]
  2. Step 2, Understand the words. दीपक [lamp], जग [world], किरण [ray], राह [path], उजाला [light], महान [great]. Write each meaning and read again. Notice the poem has no strict rhyme, so it is read by its meaning and rhythm.
  3. Step 3, Find the symbols. The lamp stands for a helpful person; the dark night stands for difficulty or ignorance; light stands for kindness and knowledge. Write: दीपक उस व्यक्ति का प्रतीक है जो दूसरों की मदद करता है। [The lamp is a symbol of a person who helps others.]
  4. Step 4, Explain the message. कवि कहता है कि सच्ची महानता अपना सुख बाँटने में है। [The poet says true greatness lies in sharing one's happiness.] Point to the last line as the key line. Identify personification: the lamp speaks like a person.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student explains only that a lamp burns at night. Correction: that is the literal meaning; add the deeper meaning: the lamp shows a person who gives help, so the poem is about sharing and kindness.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: say what the dark night stands for and what the poem teaches. Model answer: अँधेरी रात मुश्किल समय का प्रतीक है। कविता सिखाती है कि हमें दूसरों के जीवन में उजाला बाँटना चाहिए। [The dark night stands for a difficult time. The poem teaches that we should share light in the lives of others.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Read and explain: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 4 Letters and applications Weeks 28 to 36Apply

In this unit you work on 'Letters and applications', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: write formal letters. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Letters and applications' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: write formal letters, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: अनौपचारिक पत्र: दिनांक, संबोधन (प्रिय मित्र रवि), कुशल-क्षेम, मुख्य बात, समापन (तुम्हारा मित्र, अमन)। औपचारिक: सेवा में, प्रधानाचार्य महोदय; विषय: अवकाश हेतु प्रार्थना-पत्र; मुख्य भाग दो छोटे अनुच्छेदों में; आपका आज्ञाकारी शिष्य, नाम, कक्षा। Method: Model run for 'Letters and applications'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Learn the layout of a formal application. Start with सेवा में [To], then प्रधानाचार्य महोदय [The Principal, Sir], then the school and town. Next comes the subject line, विषय:, and then the greeting महोदय [Sir]। Formal letters have no personal chatting, so stay brief and respectful.
  2. Step 2, Write the subject line so the reader knows the point at once. विषय: तीन दिन के अवकाश हेतु प्रार्थना-पत्र [Subject: application for three days' leave]। Underline it. The subject line names the request in one phrase, never a full story.
  3. Step 3, Write the body. सविनय निवेदन है कि मुझे तेज़ बुखार है, इसलिए मैं 12 नवंबर से 14 नवंबर 2026 तक विद्यालय नहीं आ सकूँगा [I humbly submit that I have a high fever, so I will not be able to attend school from 12 to 14 November 2026]। That is three days, counting both dates.
  4. Step 4, Close properly. अतः आपसे प्रार्थना है कि मुझे तीन दिन का अवकाश प्रदान करें [So I request you to grant me three days' leave]। आपका आज्ञाकारी शिष्य, अमित, कक्षा सात, दिनांक: 11 नवंबर 2026 [Your obedient student, Amit, class seven, dated 11 November 2026]।
  5. Step 5, Compare a second formal letter, to an editor: सेवा में, संपादक महोदय, विषय: गली में कूड़े की समस्या [Subject: the problem of rubbish in our street]। Close with भवदीय [Yours faithfully]। Common errors: using तुम instead of आप, casual greetings, and a missing subject line.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write the subject line, body and closing for two days' leave, 20 to 21 November 2026, for a family function. Model answer: विषय: दो दिन के अवकाश हेतु प्रार्थना-पत्र। सविनय निवेदन है कि पारिवारिक समारोह के कारण मैं 20 और 21 नवंबर को नहीं आ सकूँगा। कृपया अवकाश दें। आपका शिष्य।

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Write formal letters: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

levels L1 to L7

Where this course sits on the ladder

Seven slides, one for each level. Step in at your level and move up; credits stack. A level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age.

Slide 1. L1 Basic: Greetings and first words

On the slide: L1 Basic · Greetings and first words

Notes: Greet people politely, say your name and name five family members in Hindi. Notes: teach नमस्ते (namaste, hello) and मेरा नाम ... है (mera naam ... hai, my name is ...) by ear; learners greet a partner and point to family photos while saying each word.

Slide 2. L2 Awareness: The Devanagari script

On the slide: L2 Awareness · The Devanagari script

Notes: Recognise the vowels and first consonants of Devanagari and copy them neatly under the top line. Notes: show how letters hang from a horizontal line, then write क (ka) with correct stroke order; learners find it in three printed words and read each one aloud.

Slide 3. L3 Foundation: Simple sentences and postpositions (this course)

On the slide: L3 Foundation · Simple sentences and postpositions

Notes: Read and write short sentences with verb at the end and use postpositions such as में (in) and पर (on). Notes: model किताब मेज़ पर है (kitaab mez par hai, the book is on the table); learners make five sentences about a classroom and draw each one.

This course: Grade 07 Hindi is at this level. Its units include Prose reading; Grammar: case, sandhi, and sentence parts; Poetry; Letters and applications.

Slide 4. L4 Practitioner: Daily conversation and tenses

On the slide: L4 Practitioner · Daily conversation and tenses

Notes: Hold a short conversation about daily life using present, past and future tenses with correct gender agreement. Notes: contrast वह जाता है (he goes) with वह जाती है (she goes); learners ask a partner what they did yesterday, then write six sentences marking each verb ending.

Slide 5. L5 Specialist: Reading, writing and register

On the slide: L5 Specialist · Reading, writing and register

Notes: Read a news article and write a formal letter of about 200 words, choosing between polite and casual forms. Notes: compare आप, तुम and तू (you, three levels of respect) in one dialogue; learners rewrite it for a teacher, a friend and a child.

Slide 6. L6 Advanced (degree level): Literature and linguistics

On the slide: L6 Advanced · Literature and linguistics

Notes: Analyse a Hindi poem or story for theme, form and vocabulary origins, and write a critical essay in Hindi. Notes: use a short poem; learners sort ten words into Sanskrit-derived, Persian-derived and English-derived, then argue in 500 words how word choice shapes tone.

Slide 7. L7 Super expert (postgraduate/expert): Research, translation and teaching

On the slide: L7 Super expert · Research, translation and teaching

Notes: Design a research or translation project on Hindi language or literature, justify the method and present findings to peers. Notes: pick a topic such as code-switching in advertising; collect forty examples, classify them, discuss limits of the sample, and have a fluent reviewer check every claim.

Notes: a level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age. Learners may move up or down a level by subject.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)

Use CAD in money problems; deliver in English, French; personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

United States

Use USD in money problems; deliver in English, Spanish; personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Singapore

Use SGD in money problems; deliver in English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil; personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Australia

Use AUD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Use HKD in money problems; deliver in Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English; personal data rule: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)

Use GEL or ILS in money problems; deliver in Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic, English; personal data rule: the personal data law of the country where the learner lives; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Cayman Islands (Caribbean)

Use KYD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Data Protection Act; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Mexico

Use MXN in money problems; deliver in Spanish, English; personal data rule: the federal law on personal data held by private parties; guardian contact for every learner under 18.
