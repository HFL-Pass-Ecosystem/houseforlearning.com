your choices

Every data choice starts switched off

When you join, you choose what we may learn about you. All four choices start off, and saying no to all of them still gives you a full account.

  • Remember my interests (kept until you remove them)
  • Learn from how I use the site (12 months)
  • Let Loka and Vidya remember my questions (6 months)
  • Share learning insights with my organisation (organisation accounts only, team level)

You can see, download or delete your record at any time. Read the privacy notice.

paying us

Bank details are never shown on this website

Membership is paid only by UPI or bank transfer. To protect you from fake invoices, our bank details are not published here. Ask for them and our accounts team sends them to you by email from hfl.ceo@hfl-pass.com.

  • Check that the email comes from hfl.ceo@hfl-pass.com and names your membership or invoice.
  • If anyone sends you different bank details, by email, phone or chat, do not pay. Tell us first.
  • We never ask for card numbers, passwords or one-time codes.

questions

Membership questions

Who is eligible for the $5 membership?

Eligibility is checked when you join. If you are not sure, write to us and we will tell you before you sign up.

Does membership renew by itself?

No. Membership is paid one year in advance and does not renew unless you switch renewal on. You see the price and end date before paying, get a reminder before any renewal, and can cancel in one click. See paying and renewing.

Can a young person join?

Yes, with a parent or guardian’s consent for learners under 16, or the age set by local law.

Is sign-up open now?

Online sign-up opens with the members area. Until then, the Members login button takes you to the contact form.
