2. Choose how you join

Prices are shown in full before you pay. Full refund within 14 days, then unused months less 10%. No exit fee, ever.

3. Your sign-in

You choose your password on the members app, not on this page. After you press Create my account, the members app asks for a password of at least 12 characters, then shows you how to set up a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone.

Two-step sign-in. You will use your password and the 6-digit code every time you sign in. This replaces the old security question, which is easy to guess.

4. Check you are a person

This simple check keeps robots out. It uses no third-party service and sets no cookie.

5. Agree and create your account

Every data choice starts switched off. You choose what we may learn about you after you sign in, and you can change it any time.

your safety

How we keep your account safe

  • Your password is typed only on the members app, never by email or chat
  • A 6-digit code from an authenticator app is needed every time you sign in
  • We never ask for identity documents, card numbers or bank details on this page
  • We use sufficient guardrails, and your information is safe with us
