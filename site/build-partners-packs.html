Partner packs

One pack for each kind of partner: what you receive from us, what you hand back and the CSI divisions that are yours.

pack 1 of 6

Consultants

Architects, engineers, cost and project managers. You turn the standard into a design for one site, coordinate the model and sign what the law asks you to sign.

You receive

  • Design brief and campus type
  • Space schedule and room data
  • The whole specification, all divisions
  • LOD stage plan and check sheet

You hand back

  • Drawings and models at each stage
  • Specification sections, edited for the site
  • Clash and check reports
  • Approvals from the authorities

Your divisions

  • Division 00, Procurement and Contracting Requirements
  • Division 01, General Requirements

pack 2 of 6

Contractors

Main and fit-out contractors. You build what is drawn, in the order agreed, and hand over a campus that matches the model.

You receive

  • Issued-for-construction drawings and models
  • Specification sections for your scope
  • Programme and sequence
  • Quality and safety rules

You hand back

  • Shop drawings and samples
  • Method statements
  • Programme updates
  • As-built drawings and manuals

Your divisions

  • Division 01, General Requirements
  • Division 02, Existing Conditions
  • Division 03, Concrete
  • Division 05, Metals
  • Division 06, Wood, Plastics, and Composites
  • Division 07, Thermal and Moisture Protection
  • Division 08, Openings
  • Division 09, Finishes
  • Division 10, Specialties
  • Division 13, Special Construction

pack 3 of 6

Developers

Land owners, landlords and development partners. You bring the site or the shell. We agree what the building gives us and what we fit ourselves.

You receive

  • Site rules and campus tiers
  • What we need from the base building
  • Our fit-out sequence
  • Hand-over checklist

You hand back

  • Site and base-building information
  • Service capacities
  • Access and working rules
  • Permits that sit with the owner

Your divisions

  • Division 00, Procurement and Contracting Requirements
  • Division 01, General Requirements
  • Division 31, Earthwork
  • Division 32, Exterior Improvements
  • Division 33, Utilities

pack 4 of 6

Installers

Specialist trades: glass, joinery, ceilings, floors, furniture, signs. You make and fit one part, exactly to its section, and prove it with a sample first.

You receive

  • The sections for your part
  • Setting-out drawings
  • Interface notes with other trades
  • Sample and mock-up rules

You hand back

  • Shop drawings
  • Samples and mock-ups
  • Test and inspection records
  • Care and maintenance sheets

Your divisions

  • Division 06, Wood, Plastics, and Composites
  • Division 08, Openings
  • Division 09, Finishes
  • Division 10, Specialties
  • Division 12, Furnishings

pack 5 of 6

Integrators

Audio-visual, network, security and building-control specialists. You make the technology work as one system that a teacher can use without a manual.

You receive

  • Room-by-room technology schedule
  • Sections for communications, safety and controls
  • Network and naming rules
  • Commissioning scripts

You hand back

  • Schematics and rack layouts
  • Programming and configuration files
  • Commissioning results
  • Training for campus staff

Your divisions

  • Division 25, Integrated Automation
  • Division 26, Electrical
  • Division 27, Communications
  • Division 28, Electronic Safety and Security

pack 6 of 6

Service providers

Facility management, cleaning, maintenance and support firms. You keep the campus safe, clean and working from the first day of use.

You receive

  • As-built model and asset list
  • Operation and maintenance manuals
  • Service levels and inspection plan
  • Fault reporting route

You hand back

  • Planned maintenance programme
  • Inspection and test records
  • Monthly service report
  • Improvement proposals

Your divisions

  • Division 01, General Requirements
  • Division 21, Fire Suppression
  • Division 22, Plumbing
  • Division 23, Heating, Ventilating, and Air Conditioning
  • Division 26, Electrical

every pack

What every pack shares

  • One place. The current drawings, models, sections and schedules are here. A file sent by email or chat is not current until it is here.
  • One number for everything. Each item carries its CSI section number and our element code. Offers, submittals, questions and invoices quote both.
  • One file name rule. Campus, building, level, discipline and code, as in the BIM and LOD rules.
  • One revision at a time. Each document shows its revision and date. Old revisions are kept, marked superseded, never deleted.

Learning material, not a design or safety instruction. Real buildings are designed and signed off by licensed professionals under local rules.
