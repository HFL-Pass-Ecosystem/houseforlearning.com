The pathway template

Every course pathway is described the same way, so a learner, a partner or an employer can read any of them in minutes.

The seven parts

  • Sector anchor

    The part of the economy the pathway belongs to.

  • Course pathway

    The units and series, in order.

  • Career roles

    The jobs it leads to.

  • Employment models

    Employed, self-employed, partnership or project work.

  • Market channels

    Where the work or product is sold.

  • PAVER overlay

    Where each step sits on the journey above.

  • 5W1H

    Who, what, when, where, why and how, in one page.

Three axes

  • X: Subjects

    What is taught.

  • Y: Topics

    The topics inside each subject.

  • Z: What is in it for me

    The benefit the learner sees, in plain words.

how it is taught

Practice, digital, mentoring

  • 70% practice

    Learning by doing, on real or simulated work.

  • 20% digital

    Short online units at your own pace.

  • 10% mentoring

    A person who guides and checks your progress.

Learning is case-based, adapts to how each person learns, and stacks into small credentials.

  • Short module

    1 hour to 1 month

  • Long module

    1 to 3 months

  • Learning course

    3 to 6 months

  • Diploma

    12 months

  • Advanced diploma

    24 months

  • Undergraduate

    36 to 48 months

  • Postgraduate

    12 or 24 months

  • Doctorate

    36 to 60 months

  • Lifelong learning

    No fixed end; stacks into all of the above
