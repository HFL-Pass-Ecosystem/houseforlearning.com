flagship course

Podcasting

Plan, record, edit, publish and grow a podcast with good audio, honest rights, and a simple way to earn.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L3 Foundation29:00 h of lessons plus practice and sharingSelf-paced with peer circle
  • Understand Explain formats, audiences and the production chain.
  • Apply Record clean audio and edit with free tools.
  • Analyse Read listener data.
  • Evaluate Judge rights, ethics and sponsors.
  • Create Publish a three-episode season.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Plan and publish a three-episode podcast season with show plan, scripts, clean audio, show notes, rights check and listener review.

Condition

A phone or mic, free editing software, a quiet room, two peers and a mentor.

Standard

Audio is clear with steady levels; every episode has notes and a rights check; episodes publish on a free host; listener data reviewed; peers rate Meets or better.

Who it is for: Creators, teachers, small businesses, community groups. Requirements: none. Materials: lesson text, worked examples, templates, rubric, quiz, tracker.
