HFL Saathi: one app from first subscription to daily life

Working name "Saathi" (companion). Free for people, in the language they choose, for India and the Global South. It opens with exam prep and job alerts, then grows into chat, learning, careers, enterprise, news and entertainment. Tap through the phone on the left; the build plan is on the right.

Sample data. Try: open the AI Doubt Tutor chat, answer today's practice question, toggle Lite, tap the language chip to cycle languages.

Eight modules, one login, one phone number

Sign-in is a mobile number with OTP, so nobody needs an email. Every module shares one identity, one wallet and one notification inbox. The launch build ships modules 1 to 4; the rest switch on by phase.

1 · Chats Launch

  • 1:1 and group chat, end-to-end encrypted
  • Study circles of 5 to 50 people
  • Voice notes in 16 kbps audio
  • Read receipts that can be turned off
The daily habit. Replaces outside messengers for HFL learners first.

2 · Channels Launch

  • One-way broadcast: job alerts by state, exam and qualification
  • Daily solved-question drop
  • Comments in a linked discussion group
  • Free channels plus paid premium ones
The first thing people subscribe to. It brings them in.

3 · Learn (70-20-10) Launch

  • 70 Practice: daily sets, mock tests, offline packs
  • 20 Lessons: recorded 2 to 6 minute videos
  • 10 Facilitator: live doubt rooms, expert review
  • AI Doubt Tutor inside chat
Uses the same course objects as the HFL learning platform, so progress counts toward credentials.

4 · Watch Launch

  • Short vertical solved-question videos
  • Audio plus slides mode for weak signal
  • Download on Wi-Fi or at a truck hub
  • Plays on phone, tablet, PC and TV cast
Covers the "medium of their choice" ask across devices.

5 · Careers Phase 1

  • Eligibility check against the learner's profile
  • Deadline and admit-card reminders
  • Application tracker
  • Employer and apprenticeship listings later
Turns alerts into a record of outcomes HFL can show.

6 · Explore Phase 2

  • All HFL programmes: govt exams, IT and Digital, EMBA, academies
  • Creation Studio and Open Library
  • Communities by city, college and employer
  • Partner mini-apps
The doorway from one subscription into the full ecosystem.

7 · Daily Phase 2–3

  • Engagement-team feed: quizzes, contests, streak games
  • Licensed news and current affairs
  • Infotainment and entertainment shows
  • Audio and video calls
What makes it a daily app beyond study time.

8 · Wallet Launch

  • Core app is free; pay only for optional certified programmes
  • UPI in India; Pix, M-Pesa and local wallets elsewhere
  • Receipts, scholarships, referral credits
  • Certificates and badges in one place
  • Family member can pay for a learner
All sales and payment stay digital, inside HFL.
70Practice. Daily sets, mocks, projects and on-the-job tasks, auto-marked where possible.
20Digital lessons. Recorded short videos and readings, cached for offline use.

How a doubt becomes content for everyone

This is the loop the person described: AI answers at scale, and experts in the cognitive, affective and psychomotor domains fill gaps that then go back to every learner.

Free for people, in their own language, across the Global South

India and the Global South together are about 4 to 5 billion people. Saathi reaches them through its own app and its own public web pages, with no paid ads and no outside social platforms.

Free forever

  • Chat, channels, job alerts
  • Practice, lessons and Watch
  • AI Doubt Tutor with a daily allowance
  • Rights and safety lessons
Nobody pays to join or to talk.

Optional, paid

  • Certified programmes and credentials
  • Live facilitator cohorts
  • Enterprise and academy licences
  • Government and partner contracts
Pays for the free tier. No ads, no selling of data.

Own reach

  • Every solved question, alert and lesson summary gets a fast public web page in each language that search engines index
  • Share links open without the app
  • Referral credits
  • Village champions from train-the-trainer
Organic growth that HFL owns.

Community spaces

  • Communities by village, town, college and employer
  • Local notice boards and events
  • Small-business and enterprise circles
  • Local news and entertainment in Phase 3
Engagement inside people's own communities.

How each language is madeMachine translation drafts every screen and lesson; a paid native-speaker reviewer signs off learning content before it goes live. The person picks a language at sign-up and can change it any time; voice doubts work in that language too.

  • Images load on tap; small previews first

Offline first

  • Practice works offline and syncs answers later
  • Weekly packs of lessons and questions

Truck and campus hubs

  • Each truck unit carries a local cache server
  • Phones pull packs over local Wi-Fi
  • Hub syncs with the cloud when it has a link

No smartphone

  • Job alerts and results by SMS
  • IVR line for daily questions in local language
  • Shared family phone with separate profiles

Rules HFL must meet before scale

These are known Indian requirements for a messaging and payments app. This is a checklist for counsel, not legal advice. Each country's data-protection law, and HFL's public statement on it, lives on the shared HFL privacy and data rights page. Saathi links to it from sign-up, Settings and Explore › Your rights, in the user's language.

AreaWhat appliesWhat the design does
Personal dataDigital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and its 2025 RulesConsent per purpose, data export and delete in settings, a named grievance contact
ChildrenVerifiable parent consent for users under 18 under the same ActGuardian link at sign-up; no targeted ads or tracking for minors; chats with adults limited to HFL staff and study circles
Intermediary dutiesIT Rules 2021. Above 50 lakh users in India, extra duties for a significant social media intermediaryPlan the compliance officer, nodal contact and resident grievance officer before Phase 1 ends
Message traceabilityThe same Rules ask large messaging apps to identify a first sender on a court order. This rule is under court challenge.Needs a legal decision before Phase 1, because it affects how encryption is built
PaymentsRBI rules on payment aggregators and recurring payment mandatesHFL does not hold money; a licensed aggregator does
NewsDigital news publisher code under the IT Rules 2021Licence news from registered publishers in Phase 3 rather than write it in-house at first
SafetyDuty to remove unlawful content quickly once notifiedReport button on every message, moderation team, AI screening of public channels only
