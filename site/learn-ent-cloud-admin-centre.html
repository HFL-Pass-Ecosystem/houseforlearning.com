Cloud Office Admin Centre: Users, Security and Compliance

Administer a cloud office suite: people, licences, mail, files, devices, security and reports.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L1 to L7 ladder23:00 h of lessons plus practice and sharingSelf-paced with peer circleUSD 39 per learner per month, or USD 79 with mentor review
  • Remember Remember : the admin centre tour.
  • Apply Apply : users, groups and licences.
  • Apply Apply : mail and calendars.
  • Understand Understand : files, sites and teams.
  • Analyse Analyse : sign-in and device security.
  • Evaluate Evaluate : compliance, data protection and audit.
  • Create Create : tenant governance and automation.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Administer a cloud office suite: people, licences, mail, files, devices, security and reports. Finish all 7 modules on a practice set-up.

Condition

A free trial or practice account, practice data only (never real customer data), a peer circle and a mentor for the final review.

Standard

Every module standard is met; records are kept; a peer and a mentor rate the work 'Meets' or better on the rubric.

Who it is for: IT support staff, office managers, small-business owners, junior administrators. Requirements: a computer and internet. Materials: slides with notes, worked examples, activities, quiz, rubric.

How the ladder works: modules climb from L1 (remember) to L7 (create). Take them in order; the first three suit new learners, 4 and 5 suit practitioners, 6 and 7 suit advanced staff.

lesson by lesson

7 modules, slide by slide, with notes

Module 1 The admin centre tour 2 hRememberL1 Basic

One portal controls the whole office suite.

  • Home, users, groups, billing, health.
  • Roles: global admin should be few.
  • Service health and message centre.
Worked example. A new admin finds a user, a licence and the service health page in 5 minutes.

Activity. Take the tour and list ten menu items with purpose.

Standard to meet. Ten correct.

Slides with notes

  1. Slide 1: The map. Speaker note: Annotated screenshot.
  2. Slide 2: Few global admins. Speaker note: Why two, not ten.
Module 2 Users, groups and licences 3 hApplyL2 Awareness

Joiners, movers and leavers done the same way every time.

  • Create user, assign licence.
  • Groups for teams and mail.
  • Leaver: block sign-in, convert mailbox, remove licence.
Worked example. A leaver is blocked in minutes and mailbox kept for 30 days.

Activity. Process a joiner, a mover and a leaver.

Standard to meet. All three complete with checklist.

Slides with notes

  1. Slide 1: J-M-L. Speaker note: Three checklists.
  2. Slide 2: Licence cost. Speaker note: Saved by removing.
Module 3 Mail and calendars 3 hApplyL3 Foundation

Mail needs correct records and rules.

  • Shared mailbox and alias.
  • Mail flow rules.
  • Domain records: mx, spf, dkim, dmarc (never edit without a plan).
Worked example. A shared inbox is used by four agents without a shared password.

Activity. Create a shared mailbox and one rule.

Standard to meet. Works; permissions minimal.

Slides with notes

  1. Slide 1: Mail records. Speaker note: Explain each in one line.
  2. Slide 2: Shared mailbox. Speaker note: No shared password.
Module 4 Files, sites and teams 3 hUnderstandL4 Practitioner

Where files live decides who can see them.

  • Sites, libraries, sharing links.
  • Guest access rules.
  • Retention basics.
Worked example. External sharing is limited to named guests with expiry.

Activity. Set sharing limits for one site.

Standard to meet. Guests expire in 30 days.

Slides with notes

  1. Slide 1: Link types. Speaker note: Anyone, org, named.
  2. Slide 2: Expiry. Speaker note: Why.
Module 5 Sign-in and device security 4 hAnalyseL5 Specialist

Most attacks start with a stolen password.

  • Multi-factor sign-in.
  • Conditional access.
  • Device compliance.
  • Break-glass account stored safely.
Worked example. Sign-in from a new country requires a second proof.

Activity. Design a baseline policy set.

Standard to meet. Policy tested on a pilot group first.

Slides with notes

  1. Slide 1: Second proof. Speaker note: Why.
  2. Slide 2: Pilot first. Speaker note: Never everyone at once.
Module 6 Compliance, data protection and audit 4 hEvaluateL6 Advanced

Know where sensitive data is and prove who did what.

  • Labels and data-loss rules.
  • Audit log search.
  • Retention and legal hold.
Worked example. A label stops a file with personal data being shared outside.

Activity. Create a label and test a rule.

Standard to meet. Rule blocks the test file.

Slides with notes

  1. Slide 1: Label. Speaker note: Levels.
  2. Slide 2: Audit search. Speaker note: Steps.
Module 7 Tenant governance and automation 4 hCreateL7 Super expert

Run the tenant like a product.

  • Admin roles by task.
  • Scripted bulk changes with review.
  • Monthly health report.
  • Disaster plan.
Worked example. A script creates 200 users from a checked file with a rollback list.

Activity. Write a governance pack.

Standard to meet. Roles, review, report, recovery described.

Slides with notes

  1. Slide 1: Admin by task. Speaker note: Least roles.
  2. Slide 2: Monthly report. Speaker note: Five numbers.

ten locations and global

Your place

Local law changes how you store customer data and issue invoices. Check it for your place first.

India (Bengaluru)

Language: English, Hindi, Kannada. Use INR for amounts. Personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Skills frame to cite: National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) and the national credit framework.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Language: Dutch, English. Use EUR for amounts. Personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Skills frame to cite: the Dutch qualification framework (NLQF) linked to the European Qualifications Framework.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Language: Arabic, English. Use AED for amounts. Personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection). Skills frame to cite: the emirate education and training regulators (for example KHDA in Dubai) and the national qualifications framework.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Language: Portuguese (Spanish and English support). Use BRL for amounts. Personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados). Skills frame to cite: the national technical-education catalogue and the S-system training bodies (SENAI, SENAC).

Canada (Toronto)

Language: English, French. Use CAD for amounts. Personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply. Skills frame to cite: the provincial colleges framework and the national credential-recognition guidance.

United States

Language: English, Spanish. Use USD for amounts. Personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13. Skills frame to cite: state and community-college credit systems.

Singapore

Language: English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil. Use SGD for amounts. Personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Skills frame to cite: SkillsFuture Singapore frameworks and the national credit system.

Australia

Language: English. Use AUD for amounts. Personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Skills frame to cite: the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) levels.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Language: Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English. Use HKD for amounts. Personal data rule: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Skills frame to cite: the Hong Kong Qualifications Framework.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)

Language: Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic, English. Use GEL or ILS for amounts. Personal data rule: the personal data law of the country where the learner lives. Skills frame to cite: the national qualifications framework of that country.

Cayman Islands (Caribbean)

Language: English. Use KYD for amounts. Personal data rule: the Data Protection Act. Skills frame to cite: the local education and training framework.

Mexico (Central America)

Language: Spanish, English. Use MXN for amounts. Personal data rule: the federal law on personal data held by private parties. Skills frame to cite: the national competency certification system and the education ministry levels.

Russian Federation

Russian Federation: HELD. A sanctions and licensing check by our lawyer must finish before any course is offered there. No localised version is published.
