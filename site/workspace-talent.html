Talent network intake

Partners ask for interns, apprentices or temporary staff with one short form. We match them with learners whose course record shows the skills.

Fields in the intake form
FieldWhat it holds
Role and levelJob title and grade.
Kind of placeIntern, apprentice, temporary or on probation.
Batch sizeOne person, or a batch of up to 15 with the same terms.
Start and end datesWhen the placement begins and ends.
ReplacementIs this role replacing someone? Yes or no.
BudgetIs it inside the agreed budget? Yes or no.
Cost centreWhich budget pays for it.
PlacesWhere the person will work: city or remote.
Skills wantedSkills and courses that suit the role.
ApprovalWho approves, and the date.

Every request needs a clear owner on the hiring side, and every placement is cleared with the HFL owner before anyone is contacted.

what happens next

From request to placement

  • 1 Request

    The partner fills the form.

  • 2 Match

    We list learners whose records fit.

  • 3 Approve

    The HFL owner clears the shortlist.

  • 4 Place

    Terms are agreed and the placement starts.

  • 5 Review

    The learner journal and partner feedback are saved.
