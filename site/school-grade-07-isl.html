school course

Grade 07: Islamic studies

6 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L3Secondary6 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Remember, Understand, Apply
  • Remember Recite and understand the selected passages for this grade.
  • Understand Explain the pillars of faith in age-appropriate words.
  • Understand Describe the acts of worship and how to carry them out.
  • Understand Tell and explain lessons from the stories studied.
  • Apply Apply the values of honesty, respect and kindness in daily life.
  • Understand Describe how learning and culture grew in Islamic history.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 07 Islamic studies, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 6 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

6 units with worked examples

Unit 1 The Holy Qur'an: recitation and memorisation Weeks 1 to 6Remember

In this unit you work on 'The Holy Qur'an: recitation and memorisation', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: recite and understand the selected passages for this grade. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen and talk: hear a short story, text or example linked to 'The Holy Qur'an: recitation and memorisation' and say what it means in your own words.
  • Connect: name one place in your own day where it applies.
  • Use it: recite and understand the selected passages for this grade. Practise with care and respect, in class and at home.
  • Reflect: what went well, what was hard, and one thing you will do next time.
Worked example. Model run for 'The Holy Qur'an: recitation and memorisation'. A short text or story is read aloud. Learners say the main lesson in one sentence. In pairs they find one moment in school or home where this lesson would help. Each learner plans one small action for the week and tells it to a partner. At the end of the week they report back. What a good answer looks like: the lesson is in the learner's own words, the example is real and local, and the action is small enough to do. Content follows the approved local syllabus and is checked by a qualified teacher.

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Recite and understand the selected passages for this grade: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)

Use CAD in money problems; deliver in English, French; personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

United States

Use USD in money problems; deliver in English, Spanish; personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Singapore

Use SGD in money problems; deliver in English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil; personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Australia

Use AUD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles; guardian contact for every learner under 18.
