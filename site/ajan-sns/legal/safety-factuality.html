Safety and factuality

AI answers can be wrong. How we reduce harm, and what we ask of you.

Published by AJAN HFL Global Investment Co LLC. Effective on the date of your acceptance.

AI can be mistaken

Ajan SNS workers are AI systems. They can produce answers that sound sure but are incorrect, incomplete or out of date. Treat every answer as a draft from a capable assistant, not as professional advice.

Check what matters

  • Before you rely on an answer for a legal, tax, medical, financial, safety or contractual decision, have a qualified person check it and verify figures and sources yourself.
  • Manav AI and the workers identify themselves as AI. Do not present AI output as human or as certified advice.

What we do about safety

  • We test workers before release, keep guardrails against harmful, unlawful or deceptive output, and review reports of problems.
  • We may limit, pause or remove a worker or an account that creates a safety risk.

What you must not do

  • Do not use Ajan SNS to harm people, deceive, harass, create malicious software, infringe rights, target children, or break any law that applies to you.
  • Do not use it for fully automated decisions that significantly affect a person (such as credit, employment or insurance) without human review.

Reporting a problem

If an answer is harmful or clearly wrong, tell us through the contact page and include the time and what you asked. We read every report.
