How Ajan SNS uses your data

What we keep when you use Ajan SNS, why, for how long, and the choices you have.

Published by AJAN HFL Global Investment Co LLC. Effective on the date of your acceptance.

The short version

When you ask an Ajan SNS worker something, we receive your request and the answer. We use them to run the service, keep it safe, fix problems and, in anonymised form, improve it. Please do not type sensitive or personal details into a request unless you need to. We never sell your content.

What we receive

  • Your requests, files you add, and the answers produced.
  • Your account details: name, email, organisation, and the choices you make on the welcome page.
  • Technical details: time, device and browser type, approximate region, and which workers and features you used.

Why we use it

  • To deliver the service you asked for and to bill and support you.
  • To keep Ajan SNS secure, prevent misuse and meet legal duties.
  • To measure quality and fix mistakes. Where we use content to improve workers, we first remove names and other identifiers, and a small number of trained staff may read samples under confidentiality duties.

Outside AI providers

Ajan SNS routes some requests to outside AI providers that we select. They receive only what is needed to answer the request, under written terms that limit their use of it. Which providers handle a worker is shown in the worker description.

How long we keep it

  • Requests and answers: up to 12 months, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Account details: while your account is active and up to 6 years afterwards where the law requires records.
  • Consent records (time, version, choices): for as long as the consent is relied on, plus the legal limitation period.

Where it is stored

Data is kept in secured cloud systems. It may be processed outside the country where you live. Where we transfer personal data internationally we use safeguards recognised by the UAE PDPL, the India DPDP Act 2023 and, where it applies, the GDPR (for example contractual clauses and equivalent protection).

Your choices

  • Do not enter sensitive data. Business users can ask us to switch off training use of their content in writing.
  • You can withdraw marketing consent at any time using the link in any email or by writing to us.
  • Rights to access, correct, delete and port your data are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Questions

Write to AJAN HFL through the contact page at houseforlearning.com/contact.html and mark the message "Data".
