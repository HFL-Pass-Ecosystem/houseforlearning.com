AJAN HFL Privacy Policy

How AJAN HFL handles personal data in Ajan SNS.

Published by AJAN HFL Global Investment Co LLC. Effective on the date of your acceptance.

1. Who we are

The controller is AJAN HFL Global Investment Co LLC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("AJAN HFL"). Contact: the contact page at houseforlearning.com/contact.html, marked "Privacy".

2. Laws we follow

We handle personal data in line with the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection (PDPL), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), and, for people in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, the GDPR.

3. What we collect

Account and contact details; the choices you make on the welcome page (time, terms version, both tick boxes, interests); your requests, files and answers; technical logs; messages you send us.

4. Why, and on what basis

To provide the service you asked for (contract); to keep it safe and fix it (legitimate interests); to meet legal duties; and, only for marketing emails and optional research invitations, with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Where the DPDP Act applies, we rely on your consent or on the legitimate uses it allows.

5. Who receives it

Our own staff who need it; service providers who host, secure, email, and process on our behalf under written terms; outside AI providers as described on the Data use page; authorities where the law requires. We do not sell personal data.

6. International transfers

Your data may be processed in other countries. We use contractual and technical safeguards so that it stays protected to the standard these laws require.

7. How long we keep it

As set out on the Data use page. We delete or anonymise data we no longer need.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, limit or object to use, move it, or withdraw consent, and you may complain to the data-protection authority of your country. India users may also name a nominee and use our grievance route. We answer within the time the law sets, normally within 30 days.

9. Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit, restricted keys and staff confidentiality duties. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

10. Children

Ajan SNS is for adults using it professionally. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Cookies and storage

The welcome page stores one random identifier in your browser so we can keep your choices together. It is not used for advertising.

12. Changes

We will post changes here and, for material changes, ask you to confirm them again.
