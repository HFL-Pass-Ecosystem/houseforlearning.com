Starter Tier Additional Terms

Extra terms for the free Ajan SNS Starter Tier.

Published by AJAN HFL Global Investment Co LLC. Effective on the date of your acceptance.

1. What the Starter Tier is

The Starter Tier gives limited free access to Ajan SNS so you can try it and build early prototypes. It is offered at our discretion.

2. Limits

Daily and monthly request, size and speed limits apply and are shown in your account. We can change them. Starter keys are not for production use serving large numbers of end users.

3. Content on the Starter Tier

Because the Starter Tier is free, we may use anonymised samples of requests and answers to test and improve workers, as set out on the Data use page. Do not enter confidential or personal information on the Starter Tier. Move to a paid plan if you need stricter handling.

4. No support promise

Support for the Starter Tier is best-effort. We give no service level, uptime or response-time commitment.

5. Ending and upgrading

We may change, pause or end the Starter Tier or your access to it with reasonable notice where practical. You may upgrade when paid plans are available; upgrading does not carry back any rights to past data beyond what the plan states.

6. Everything else

The API Additional Terms, Safety and factuality page, and Privacy Policy also apply.
