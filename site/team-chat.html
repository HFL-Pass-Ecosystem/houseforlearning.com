Team chat and team spaces

Test build. This page shows how contacts and spaces will be organised. Not connected Live messaging waits for the help and chat service.

Contact folders

This site has its own chat community. Contacts sit in folders by role and unit, and every folder is marked Internal or External. Nobody imports a contact list: people are added by click from our own directory.

  • Programme leads Internal ● Available
  • Safety officers Internal ◐ Busy
  • Learning team Internal ◑ Away
  • Partner contacts External ○ Offline
  • Customer contacts External ○ Offline

Sample folders only. Presence shows as a word and a shape, never colour alone. External contacts need clearance before they are added.

Team spaces

A space keeps the whole conversation with a partner, a project, a department or a learning cohort in one traceable place. What is not in the space does not exist on the system.

Kinds of space

  • Partner space (one per channel partner)
  • Project space
  • Department space
  • Learning cohort space

What a space holds

  • Document library
  • Surveys and feedback forms
  • Discussion board
  • Shared calendar
  • Task list
  • Contact cards
  • Workflows and forms (approvals)
  • Assessments

Partners see only their own space. We start with a small pilot of small or new partners. Ask about a team space
