marketplace and workplace

A fully equipped suite

At HFL, we believe in the early adoption of new technologies to solve the challenges facing modern humanity in healthcare, climate change, sustainability and the like. We offer a conducive marketplace and workplace with a fully equipped suite of:

  • IoT services and people
  • ICT services and people
  • ICTSP and IOTSP full stack with ERP, CRM, MIS, MS365, MSD, MSA and more: a no-code ecosystem powered by custom APIs and apps
  • An end-to-end open standards DEM and DAAP ecosystem with ACS, ATLE, PAVER, LAB and LEAP course and career pathways in a connected marketplace and workplace
  • Consultancy, channel, GBOS, support and full-service ecosystem
  • An L1 to L4 partner, affiliate and value provider hub-and-spoke ecosystem
  • AI and machine learning
  • Big data
  • Analytics
