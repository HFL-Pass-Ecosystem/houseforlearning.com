Skills matrix

See who in the team can do what. Each person has a level for each skill. Levels 3 and 4 need a sign-off by someone who can teach the skill. Safety skills have an expiry date. Find gaps and plan training.

Team and skills

Levels: 0 not trained, 1 learning, 2 can do with help, 3 can do alone, 4 can teach. Here you can set levels 0, 1 and 2, or lower a level. To raise someone to 3 or 4, use the Sign off tab.

    Sign off a level 3 or 4

    The person who signs must be at level 4 in that skill (and not expired). They cannot sign for themselves. For a safety skill, give the date the skill runs out.
