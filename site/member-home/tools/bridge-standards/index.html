Bridge programme standards

A bridge programme prepares a learner to enter a named next course. These ten standards are our own checklist for building one. They apply to every bridge programme we run or host.

Written fresh in our own words, based on India's national education policy approach to stackable credits and multiple entry and exit points. The word 'bridge' is used so it is never confused with the national policy's own stage names. Not an accredited qualification; no guarantee of entry anywhere.

Tasks, conditions and standards

For each practical unit, write a Task (what the learner must do), the Conditions (time, tools, help allowed) and the Standard (what good looks like, with a measure).
