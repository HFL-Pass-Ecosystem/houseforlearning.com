    How to make two pictures that match
    1. Take one photo of the room. Keep it. This is the BEFORE picture.
    2. Give that same photo to an image tool. Ask it to redesign the room without moving the walls, the windows or the camera.
    3. Save the new picture. This is the AFTER picture. Do not crop it.
    4. Come back here and choose the two pictures with the buttons above.
    5. If the tool says the shapes are different, make the AFTER picture again from the same photo.
