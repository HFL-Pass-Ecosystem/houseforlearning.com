Meeting notes and actions

Plan a meeting on site, online or mixed. Write notes, decisions and actions for each agenda point. Follow up open actions, and let the chair approve the minutes.

Pick your own name. The screen uses it to check who may approve minutes and confirm actions.

Set up a meeting

The chair and the note-taker must be two different people. The chair later approves the minutes the note-taker writes.

    Actions

    The action owner reports an action as done. Then the chair of that meeting confirms it. The chair cannot confirm an action the chair owns: then the note-taker confirms it.

      Meetings

      Only the chair can approve minutes. Approved minutes are locked: notes, decisions and actions can no longer be changed. Actions can still be marked done.
