Workplace help desk

Ask for help with IT, the building, supplies or people matters. Each request gets a ticket number and a target time. Helpers move tickets along, and the person who asked confirms the fix.

Ask for help

Target times: Stop-work 1 hour, High 4 hours, Normal 1 day, Low 3 days. Please do not type phone numbers, email addresses or money amounts.

Queue

Steps: New, Taken, Waiting, Fixed, Closed. Taking a ticket needs a helper. Only the person who asked can close it, and a helper cannot close their own fix.
