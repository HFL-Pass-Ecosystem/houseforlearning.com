Karma points

Karma points are a mirror of good work, in the spirit of the Bhagavad Gita: do your own duty well, serve others, stay steady, and let go of the reward. Points are thanks, not money. They cannot be bought, sold, swapped or cashed out.

"Your part is the work itself, never the fruit of it. Do not work for the reward, and do not give up the work." Bhagavad Gita 2.47, in our own words.

My resolve (Sankalpa)

Before the great battle at Kurukshetra, Arjuna, on Krishna's advice, prayed to Maa Durga for strength and made his resolve to do what is right (Mahabharata, Bhishma Parva). In the same spirit, start with a promise of your own: strength for honest effort, not for gain.

    My good deeds

      Log a good deed

      Thank someone for a good deed, or write down your own. A different person checks it before the points count. The same kind of deed counts at most 3 times a day for one person. Please do not type phone numbers, email addresses or money amounts.

      Check good deeds (maker-checker)

      A checker confirms that the deed really happened. The checker must be a different person from the one who did it and the one who wrote it. Check with an even mind: the same care for everyone (Gita 2.48).
