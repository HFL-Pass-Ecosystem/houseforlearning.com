Stock desk

Keep count of the goods in a small store or marketplace shop. Book goods in and out, move them between places, count the shelves, and make a reorder list. Quantities only, no prices.

Stock on hand

An item marked "Reorder" has a quantity at or below its reorder level. Quantities change only through Move stock or an approved count.

Move stock

Goods in adds to a place. Goods out takes from a place for an order or a job. Transfer moves goods from one place to another. You cannot take out more than is on hand in that place.
