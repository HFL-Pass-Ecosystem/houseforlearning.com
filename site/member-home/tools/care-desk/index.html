Customer care desk

Log after-sales questions, complaints, returns and warranty claims from marketplace orders. Every case gets a number and a reply-by time. Move each case step by step until it is closed.

Log a new case

Reply-by time: Urgent cases must get a first reply within 2 hours. Normal cases within 24 hours. The first reply counts when the case is marked "Looked into".

Do not type prices, money amounts, phone numbers or email addresses. The screen will refuse them.

Cases

Steps: Received, Looked into, Remedy offered, Customer answer, Closed. A remedy offer must be approved by a different person from the case handler. Every step is logged with the time and who did it.
