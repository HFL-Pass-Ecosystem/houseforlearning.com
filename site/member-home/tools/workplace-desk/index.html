Workplace desk

Daily work screens for staff and partners: a task board where one person does and another checks, team and leave approvals, shift handover on site, where you work this week, and a work log with a timer.

Task board

Each task has one person who does it (R), one who answers for it (A), and people to ask (C) or to tell (I). A task can only move to Done when a different person from the doer has checked it.
