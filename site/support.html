Learner support and accessibility

Learning works best when help is easy to find. Here is who supports you, how we make learning accessible, and how to ask for what you need.

Ask for helpQuestions

who helps you

People and guides on your side

  • support desk

    Learner support

    Help with sign-up, your learning record, access needs and well-being.

  • faculty

    Faculty

    Teach live classes, answer questions about a unit and give feedback.

  • coach

    Coach or mentor

    Helps you choose your next unit and keep going when it gets hard.

  • labs

    Lab supervisor

    A qualified person present for every hands-on unit on a training rig.

  • coming soon

    Loka and Vidya

    Our AI guides: Loka for finding a path, Vidya for study help. Both will say they are AI.

accessibility

Learning built for everyone

  • Captions on every live session
  • Plain words, short sentences and one idea at a time
  • Pages that work with a keyboard and a screen reader, and are checked for contrast and clear headings
  • Text you can enlarge without losing anything
  • Short units you can pause and pick up later
  • Each language checked by a native speaker before it is shown
  • Transcripts and low-data versions of units (planned)
  • A read-along voice in your language (planned)

adjustments

Asking for an adjustment

  1. Tell us

    Use the contact form and choose “Learner support or an adjustment”. You do not need to share medical details.

  2. Agree it

    Together we agree what helps, such as extra time, another format, a quiet room or a support person.

  3. Keep it private

    The adjustment is noted privately on your record so you do not have to ask again.

  4. Review it

    We check each term that it still works for you.

where you study

Study stations: campus or home

  • at home

    Home study station

    A quiet corner, a device, headphones and a steady connection. A 10-minute break is enough for many units.

  • on campus

    Campus study station

    A booked desk with a device and headphones, close to learner support.

  • hands-on

    Practice station

    For hands-on units only: a campus training rig, de-energised, with a supervisor present.

safety and well-being first

Looking after you while you learn

  • Safety units always come before hands-on work
  • Training rigs are de-energised, and a qualified supervisor is present
  • Breaks are built into longer units
  • Anyone can stop a task they think is unsafe
  • You can raise a concern with learner support at any time
  • A safeguarding role looks after learners under 18

for parents and guardians

If your child learns with us

  • Learners under 16, or the age set by local law, need your consent to join
  • We never show advertising and never sell anyone’s data
  • Hands-on units are always supervised, and safety units come first
  • Every data choice starts switched off, and you can ask to see or delete the record
  • A safeguarding role is responsible for young learners

Questions? Use the contact form and choose “A question from a parent or guardian”. A person reads every message.

your next step

Need help now?

Tell us what you need. A person reads every message and writes back.
