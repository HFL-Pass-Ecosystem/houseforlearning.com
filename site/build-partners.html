single source of truth

Seven rules everyone works by

  • One place. The current drawings, models, sections and schedules are here. A file sent by email or chat is not current until it is here.
  • One number for everything. Each item carries its CSI section number and our element code. Offers, submittals, questions and invoices quote both.
  • One file name rule. Campus, building, level, discipline and code, as in the BIM and LOD rules.
  • One revision at a time. Each document shows its revision and date. Old revisions are kept, marked superseded, never deleted.
  • One route for questions. A question is raised against a section number, answered once and visible to every partner it touches.
  • One change log. Every change says what changed, why, who approved it and which sections it touches.
  • No maker is named in our standard. A partner proposes products that meet the section; we approve by sample and data.

how a build runs

From invitation to a campus in use

  1. We issue the campus type, climate annex and campus number.
  2. The consultant edits the sections for the site and coordinates the model.
  3. Partners price by section number on one form.
  4. Submittals and samples are approved here; one room is built first as a mock-up.
  5. Work follows the build order; the programme is updated every week.
  6. Every system is commissioned with the room full.
  7. Hand-over: as-built model, manuals, asset list and training. The service provider takes over.

Learning material, not a design or safety instruction. Real buildings are designed and signed off by licensed professionals under local rules.
