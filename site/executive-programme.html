    How you learn

    See it, hear it, read it or do it

    Every unit and class is offered in four ways. Use one or all of them and switch whenever you like; nobody is tested or labelled with one style.

    Learn, earn, author, train and collaborate

    One loop runs through every edition: you learn a unit, earn by using it in your work, author what your work taught you, train others, and work with others in seven shared spaces.

      What stays with you

      AI helps at every step: it can explain, draft, check and summarise, and you learn to use it well. What carries meaning and responsibility stays with you, and the time AI saves goes back into people.
