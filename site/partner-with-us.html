What you receive

The tools of a real business, without a subscription

  • Free

    Finance and operations

    Accounts, invoices, orders, stock and records are included for every partner. No subscription fee and no set-up fee.

  • Fair

    One simple fee

    We take a share of what you earn through the platform. It is about what other finance and operations suites charge you anyway, so you do not pay twice. If you earn nothing, you owe us nothing.

  • Looked after

    Tax and licences

    We help with your tax filings and with the licences your work needs, so you can spend your time on your craft. (Draft wording, subject to review by our lawyer.)

  • Trusted

    Your member ID

    You receive a member ID that customers can check, and a record of your training and accreditation.

  • Backed

    Our brand stands behind you

    Customers come to a known name. If a mistake happens, we work with you to put it right, subject to our terms. (Draft wording, subject to review by our lawyer.)

Trust for everyone

Safe for customers and for you

People trust a marketplace when they know who they are dealing with. So we ask everyone who joins to complete a simple identity check (KYC), and we ask partners, with their consent, to complete a background or police clearance check through authorised agencies. We repeat these checks regularly for everyone on the marketplace and workplace, and we issue a clearance record you can show your customers. We handle your details with care and use them only for these checks. (Draft wording, subject to review by our lawyer.)

How it starts

Four easy steps

  1. Choose the sectors and layers you would like to serve.
  2. Join with your details and complete the identity check.
  3. Set up your free finance and operations desk, with our help.
  4. Earn as customers find you, and grow at your own pace.

Partners work at levels L1 to L4 in a hub-and-spoke ecosystem of partners, affiliates and value providers. No promises are made about income; what you earn depends on your work.
