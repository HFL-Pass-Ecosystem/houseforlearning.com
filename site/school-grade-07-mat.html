Grade 07: Mathematics

14 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L3Secondary14 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Apply, Analyse, Create
  • Apply Add, subtract, multiply and divide integers and use properties.
  • Apply Multiply and divide fractions and decimals and solve problems.
  • Apply Find mean, median, mode and range and read bar graphs.
  • Apply Solve and form equations in one variable.
  • Apply Use angle pairs, parallel lines and transversals.
  • Apply Use the angle-sum and exterior-angle properties and the Pythagoras property.
  • Apply Test congruence with SSS, SAS, ASA and RHS.
  • Apply Solve percentage, profit and loss, and simple interest problems.
  • Analyse Compare and operate on rational numbers.
  • Create Construct lines, angles and triangles.
  • Apply Find area of parallelograms, triangles and circles.
  • Apply Add and subtract expressions and find values.
  • Apply Use laws of exponents and standard form.
  • Apply Draw nets and views of solids and find lines of symmetry.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 07 Mathematics, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 14 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

14 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Integers Weeks 1 to 3Apply

In this unit you work on 'Integers', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: add, subtract, multiply and divide integers and use properties. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Show it with objects or a picture: build or draw the idea in 'Integers' before using symbols.
  • Say the rule in your own words, then write one worked line, naming each step.
  • Use it: add, subtract, multiply and divide integers and use properties. Start with one easy question, then one with a real-life story.
  • Check it: use a second method, estimate, or work backwards to test your answer.
Worked example. Model run for 'Integers'. Read the question and underline what is asked. Draw or write what is known. Choose the method named in the unit. Work it line by line, one idea per line, with units where they apply. Estimate first so the answer can be tested for sense. Check by working backwards. What a good answer looks like: correct method, every step visible, units stated, and a one-line check.

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Add, subtract, multiply and divide integers and use properties: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

How you are checked

Evidence for each unit is marked with this rubric by a teacher, and by a peer where the unit allows. Pass: no criterion at 'Not yet' after one retry.

Marking rubric (Not yet, Meets, Exceeds)
CriterionNot yetMeetsExceeds
AccuracyFrequent errorsMostly correctCorrect, checked
Method shownAnswer onlySteps shownSteps shown with reasons
Problem solvingNeeds the method toldChooses the methodChooses and explains why
CommunicationHard to followClear workingClear working with correct terms
Practice habitRarely practisesPractises weeklyPractises most days with an error log

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)

Use CAD in money problems; deliver in English, French; personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

United States

Use USD in money problems; deliver in English, Spanish; personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Singapore

Use SGD in money problems; deliver in English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil; personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Australia

Use AUD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles; guardian contact for every learner under 18.
