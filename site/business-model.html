Business model

From first order to lasting service: simple to start, fair to continue. Choose any step to go deeper.

  • 1. Order

    You choose a plan and sign up.

    • Membership first
    • Then only what you use

  • 2. Delivery

    Content, tools and services are set up for your first piece of work.

    • Set up around one workstream
    • Clear hand-over

  • 3. Service

    Support and updates continue after delivery.

    • Ongoing support
    • Updates
    • Warranty where it applies

What it costs

Membership is $5 a year. After that you pay only for what you use, at cost plus a small administration charge and taxes. No stored cards and no automatic renewal.
