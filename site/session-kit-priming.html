Pre-read shape

  1. Aim in one line.
  2. Five key words, one plain line each.
  3. One picture.
  4. One small task, 10 minutes.

Post-read shape

  1. What we did, in steps.
  2. Links and files.
  3. Follow-up task with a due day.
  4. Where to get help.
