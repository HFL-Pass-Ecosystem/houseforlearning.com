choosing

How to choose where to start

  1. Pick a family

    Start from the work you do now, or the work you want to do.

  2. Take the first unit

    Most families start with a short unit of 10 to 15 minutes.

  3. Check the fit

    A coach or our learner support desk can suggest your next unit.

  4. Follow a pathway

    Units stack in order, so each one builds on the last.

take them in order

Series: Learning how to learn

Six short units. Each one builds on the one before, so the next unit opens when you finish this one.

  1. What is a learning outcome? (10 min)
  2. Writing your own study goal (15 min)
  3. Planning a 10-minute study burst (10 min)
  4. Learning by doing: practice that sticks (20 min)
  5. Asking good questions (15 min)
  6. Showing what you can do (20 min)

Take it online at your own pace, or blended, with one homework task per unit and a live class with a faculty member.
