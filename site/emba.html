Executive MBA subjects

19 subjects from first steps to the action project. Start free, listen or read, and answer by speaking or typing.

free entry

Start free, learn your way

  • Start free. The three pre-programme subjects, Managerial Skills, Individual Skills and Team Skills, are free for everyone. They suit young people starting out and adults who are new to study or to computers.
  • Try before you join. Part 1 of every other subject is free to read, listen to and answer.
  • Talk or type. Every part has a Listen button, a Speak button and a typing box. You never have to type if you prefer to talk.
  • Learn your way. Every part offers See, Hear, Do and Read-and-write routes. Take the learning-style check to find yours.
  • Pay as you go. Join the whole programme, or take one subject at a time. Fees are shown at the join step.
