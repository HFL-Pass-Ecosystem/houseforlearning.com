school course

Grade 07: Arabic

4 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L3Secondary4 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Understand, Apply
  • Understand Summarise a short story.
  • Apply Use verbs in present and past.
  • Apply Hold a short phone conversation.
  • Understand Describe a person or place.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 07 Arabic, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 4 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

4 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Reading short stories Weeks 1 to 9Understand

In this unit you work on 'Reading short stories', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: summarise a short story. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Reading short stories' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: summarise a short story, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: الولد يلعب في الحديقة. أين يلعب الولد؟ في الحديقة. (The boy plays in the garden. Where does the boy play? In the garden.) Method: Model run for 'Reading short stories'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Read the story I wrote. كَانَ فِي قَرْيَةٍ صَغِيرَةٍ صَبِيٌّ اسْمُهُ يُوسُفُ يَخَافُ مِنَ الظَّلَامِ. فِي لَيْلَةٍ انْقَطَعَ النُّورُ، فَأَشْعَلَ شَمْعَةً وَسَاعَدَ جَارَتَهُ الْعَجُوزَ. [In a small village there was a boy named Yusuf who feared the dark. One night the electricity went out, so he lit a candle and helped his old neighbour.]
  2. Step 2, Read the ending. عِنْدَهَا عَرَفَ أَنَّ الشَّجَاعَةَ أَنْ نَفْعَلَ الْخَيْرَ رَغْمَ الْخَوْفِ. [Then he knew that courage is to do good in spite of fear.] Underline the lesson sentence, which tells you what the story teaches.
  3. Step 3, List the story elements. الشَّخْصِيَّاتُ [characters]: يُوسُفُ وَالْجَارَةُ. الْمَكَانُ [place]: قَرْيَةٌ صَغِيرَةٌ. الْمُشْكِلَةُ [problem]: الْخَوْفُ مِنَ الظَّلَامِ. الْحَلُّ [solution]: شَمْعَةٌ وَمُسَاعَدَةٌ. الْعِبْرَةُ [lesson]: الشَّجَاعَةُ فِعْلُ الْخَيْرِ. Fill in each from the text.
  4. Step 4, Write a three-sentence summary. يُحْكَى عَنْ صَبِيٍّ يَخَافُ مِنَ الظَّلَامِ. فِي لَيْلَةٍ انْقَطَعَ النُّورُ فَسَاعَدَ جَارَتَهُ بِشَمْعَةٍ. تَعَلَّمَ أَنَّ الشَّجَاعَةَ فِعْلُ الْخَيْرِ. [It is told of a boy who feared the dark. One night, when the light went out, he helped his neighbour with a candle. He learned that courage is doing good.]
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student retells every detail, including the village size, and adds an opinion. Correction: a summary keeps only the main events in your own words, in the past tense, and leaves out opinions. Aim for about a third of the text.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write two sentences on why the neighbour needed Yusuf and what he learned. Model answer: كَانَتِ الْجَارَةُ الْعَجُوزُ تَحْتَاجُ إِلَى النُّورِ. تَعَلَّمَ يُوسُفُ أَنْ يَتَغَلَّبَ عَلَى خَوْفِهِ. [The old neighbour needed light. Yusuf learned to overcome his fear.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Summarise a short story: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 2 Present and past tenses Weeks 10 to 18Apply

In this unit you work on 'Present and past tenses', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: use verbs in present and past. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Present and past tenses' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: use verbs in present and past, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: أكتب الدرس (present) ثم كتبتُ الدرس (past). يذهب تصبح ذهب. (writes becomes wrote; goes becomes went.) Method: Model run for 'Present and past tenses'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Learn the past forms of كَتَبَ [to write]. كَتَبْتُ [I wrote], كَتَبْتَ [you wrote, m], كَتَبْتِ [you wrote, f], كَتَبَ [he wrote], كَتَبَتْ [she wrote], كَتَبْنَا [we wrote]. The endings are added to the stem كَتَب.
  2. Step 2, Learn the present forms. أَكْتُبُ [I write], تَكْتُبُ [you write, m], تَكْتُبِينَ [you write, f], يَكْتُبُ [he writes], تَكْتُبُ [she writes], نَكْتُبُ [we write]. The prefixes أ ت ي ن show the person.
  3. Step 3, Use time words. أَمْسِ كَتَبْتُ رِسَالَةً. [Yesterday I wrote a letter.] الْآنَ أَكْتُبُ وَاجِبِي. [Now I am writing my homework.] غَدًا سَأَكْتُبُ مَقَالًا. [Tomorrow I will write an essay.] Add سَـ before the present verb to make it future.
  4. Step 4, Try another verb, ذَهَبَ [to go]. ذَهَبْنَا إِلَى الْمَكْتَبَةِ أَمْسِ. [We went to the library yesterday.] هِيَ تَذْهَبُ إِلَى الْمَدْرَسَةِ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ. [She goes to school every day.] Say the past and present of both verbs for three persons.
  5. Step 5, Fix errors. A student says هِيَ كَتَبَ الدَّرْسَ. Correction: هِيَ كَتَبَتِ الدَّرْسَ, because she needs the feminine ending ـَتْ. Another says نَحْنُ ذَهَبْتُ; correction: نَحْنُ ذَهَبْنَا, because we ends with ـنَا.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write one past and one present sentence with the verb قَرَأَ [to read]. Model answer: أَمْسِ قَرَأْتُ قِصَّةً. الْآنَ أَقْرَأُ كِتَابًا. [Yesterday I read a story. Now I am reading a book.] Check that قَرَأْتُ ends in تُ for I.

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Use verbs in present and past: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 3 Telephone and daily conversation Weeks 19 to 27Apply

In this unit you work on 'Telephone and daily conversation', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: hold a short phone conversation. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Telephone and daily conversation' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: hold a short phone conversation, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: — السلام عليكم، هل أستطيع التحدث إلى أحمد؟ — وعليكم السلام، لحظة من فضلك. (Hello, may I speak to Ahmed? One moment please.) Method: Model run for 'Telephone and daily conversation'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Open a call politely. أَلُو، السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ. [Hello, peace be upon you.] هَلْ أَسْتَطِيعُ أَنْ أَتَكَلَّمَ مَعَ سَلْمَى؟ [May I speak with Salma?] The reply is وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلَامُ. مَنْ يَتَكَلَّمُ؟ [And upon you peace. Who is speaking?]
  2. Step 2, Say who you are. أَنَا خَالِدٌ، زَمِيلُهَا فِي الصَّفِّ. [I am Khalid, her classmate.] نَعَمْ، لَحْظَةً مِنْ فَضْلِكَ. [Yes, one moment please.] عُذْرًا، سَلْمَى غَيْرُ مَوْجُودَةٍ الْآنَ. [Sorry, Salma is not here now.]
  3. Step 3, Leave a message. هَلْ تُرِيدُ أَنْ تَتْرُكَ رِسَالَةً؟ [Do you want to leave a message?] نَعَمْ، أَخْبِرْهَا أَنَّ الِاجْتِمَاعَ غَدًا. [Yes, tell her the meeting is tomorrow.] حَسَنًا، سَأُخْبِرُهَا. [Fine, I will tell her.]
  4. Step 4, Close the call. شُكْرًا جَزِيلًا. مَعَ السَّلَامَةِ. [Thank you very much. Goodbye.] If you dialled wrongly: عُذْرًا، الرَّقْمُ خَطَأٌ. [Sorry, wrong number.] Act out the full call with a partner, then swap roles.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A caller says هَلْ تُرِيدُ أَنْ تَتْرُكُ رِسَالَةً؟ with damma. Correction: هَلْ تُرِيدُ أَنْ تَتْرُكَ رِسَالَةً؟ because after أَنْ the verb takes fatha. Also never hang up without saying مَعَ السَّلَامَةِ.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: phone a friend to ask about homework. Model answer: السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ. أَنَا نُورُ. هَلْ أَسْتَطِيعُ أَنْ أَسْأَلَ عَنِ الْوَاجِبِ؟ شُكْرًا. مَعَ السَّلَامَةِ. [Peace be upon you. I am Nour. May I ask about the homework? Thank you. Goodbye.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Hold a short phone conversation: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 4 Descriptive writing Weeks 28 to 36Understand

In this unit you work on 'Descriptive writing', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: describe a person or place. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Descriptive writing' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: describe a person or place, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: فقرة: القراءة مفيدة. فهي تزيد معلوماتنا. وتنمّي لغتنا. لذلك أقرأ كل يوم. (topic sentence, two details, closing) Method: Model run for 'Descriptive writing'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, A description has three parts: appearance, character, example. For a person write: جَدِّي رَجُلٌ طَوِيلُ الْقَامَةِ، أَبْيَضُ الشَّعْرِ. [My grandfather is a tall man with white hair.] Notice طَوِيلُ الْقَامَةِ [tall of stature] is a common phrase.
  2. Step 2, Add character. هُوَ هَادِئٌ وَكَرِيمٌ. [He is calm and generous.] يَبْتَسِمُ دَائِمًا. [He always smiles.] Choose adjectives that agree: for a woman say جَدَّتِي امْرَأَةٌ طَيِّبَةٌ [My grandmother is a kind woman].
  3. Step 3, Give an example that proves the character. فِي كُلِّ جُمْعَةٍ يُوزِّعُ الْحَلْوَى عَلَى الْأَطْفَالِ. [Every Friday he gives out sweets to the children.] Connect with لِذَلِكَ [therefore]: لِذَلِكَ يُحِبُّهُ الْجَمِيعُ. [Therefore everyone loves him.]
  4. Step 4, Describe a place. مَدِينَتِي سَاحِلِيَّةٌ صَغِيرَةٌ. شَوَارِعُهَا نَظِيفَةٌ وَبُيُوتُهَا بَيْضَاءُ. يَبْدُو الْبَحْرُ كَأَنَّهُ مِرْآةٌ كَبِيرَةٌ. [My town is a small coastal town. Its streets are clean and its houses white. The sea looks like a big mirror.] The word كَأَنَّ makes a simile.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student writes a list: طَوِيلٌ، كَرِيمٌ، هَادِئٌ. Correction: join the ideas in sentences and give an example, as in جَدِّي كَرِيمٌ لِأَنَّهُ يُسَاعِدُ الْفُقَرَاءَ [My grandfather is generous because he helps the poor]. Also agree adjectives with the noun.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: describe your mother in three sentences. Model answer: أُمِّي امْرَأَةٌ جَمِيلَةٌ وَهَادِئَةٌ. تَعْمَلُ بِجِدٍّ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ. لِذَلِكَ أُحِبُّهَا كَثِيرًا. [My mother is a beautiful, calm woman. She works hard every day. Therefore I love her very much.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Describe a person or place: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

levels L1 to L7

Where this course sits on the ladder

Seven slides, one for each level. Step in at your level and move up; credits stack. A level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age.

Slide 1. L1 Basic: First sounds and greetings

On the slide: L1 Basic · First sounds and greetings

Notes: Say hello, give your name and name three everyday objects in Arabic. Notes: teach مرحبا (marhaba, hello) and كتاب (kitab, book) aloud before any writing; learners greet a partner and point to a book, a pen and a door while saying each word.

Slide 2. L2 Awareness: Letters and the right-to-left page

On the slide: L2 Awareness · Letters and the right-to-left page

Notes: Recognise that Arabic is written right to left and copy the shapes of the first eight letters. Notes: show that letters change form at the start, middle and end of a word; learners trace the letter for b, then find it in three printed words.

Slide 3. L3 Foundation: Simple sentences and a first reading (this course)

On the slide: L3 Foundation · Simple sentences and a first reading

Notes: Read and write short present-tense sentences about yourself, family and daily life with short vowels marked. Notes: use هذا كتاب (hadha kitab, this is a book) as the model; learners build five new sentences, swapping the noun each time, and read them aloud.

This course: Grade 07 Arabic is at this level. Its units include Reading short stories; Present and past tenses; Telephone and daily conversation; Descriptive writing.

Slide 4. L4 Practitioner: Everyday conversation and roots

On the slide: L4 Practitioner · Everyday conversation and roots

Notes: Hold a two-minute conversation on school, food and travel and use the three-letter root idea to guess new words. Notes: show k-t-b giving kitab (book) and katib (writer); learners find the root in four given words, then order a meal in a role-play.

Slide 5. L5 Specialist: Formal Arabic and dialect awareness

On the slide: L5 Specialist · Formal Arabic and dialect awareness

Notes: Compare Modern Standard Arabic with one spoken dialect and write a formal letter of about 150 words. Notes: take one phrase, such as how to say 'how are you', in both registers; learners list three differences and choose the right register for a news item and a chat.

Slide 6. L6 Advanced (degree level): Literary analysis and linguistics

On the slide: L6 Advanced · Literary analysis and linguistics

Notes: Analyse a classical or modern text for grammar, rhetoric and register, and write a reasoned essay in Arabic. Notes: choose a short poem of eight lines; learners mark case endings, name one image used, and explain in a 400-word essay how the form supports the meaning.

Slide 7. L7 Super expert (postgraduate/expert): Translation, research and teaching

On the slide: L7 Super expert · Translation, research and teaching

Notes: Design and defend a translation or research project that handles dialect variation, with a documented method. Notes: pick a 500-word article and translate it for two audiences; justify every choice about register, note untranslatable terms, and have a native reader check the result.

Notes: a level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age. Learners may move up or down a level by subject.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)

Use CAD in money problems; deliver in English, French; personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

United States

Use USD in money problems; deliver in English, Spanish; personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Singapore

Use SGD in money problems; deliver in English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil; personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Australia

Use AUD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Use HKD in money problems; deliver in Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English; personal data rule: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)
