Electric vehicles pathway

The model pathway: eight subjects, their topics and what is in it for you.

Workspace homePathway templatePathway listModel: EV pathway

model pathway

Automobile engineering and electric vehicles

This pathway is the model: every other pathway is filled in the same way (see the pathway template). Each subject shows its topics (the Y axis) and what is in it for the learner (the Z axis).

eight subjects

Subjects, topics and benefits

  • 1. Vehicle systems

    • How an electric vehicle is put together
    • Drive layouts and energy flow
    • Thermal management
    • Reading a vehicle diagram

    What is in it for me: You can explain how the whole vehicle works and talk with any specialist.

  • 2. Battery chemistry and packs

    • Cell types and how they differ
    • Pack design, modules and cooling
    • Battery management and balancing
    • Ageing, health and second life

    What is in it for me: You can judge a battery, spot early faults and know what it is worth.

  • 3. Power electronics and motor drives

    • Converters, inverters and chargers
    • Electric motor types
    • Efficiency and heat
    • Fault finding on drives

    What is in it for me: You can repair and tune the parts that turn stored energy into motion.

  • 4. Embedded controls and safety

    • Control units and vehicle networks
    • Software updates and data
    • Functional safety basics
    • Cyber safety of vehicles

    What is in it for me: You can work on the vehicle software side and keep people safe.

  • 5. Materials and manufacturing

    • Light materials and joining
    • Assembly lines and quality checks
    • Battery and motor manufacturing basics
    • Supply chains and sourcing

    What is in it for me: You can take a job in a plant and understand why parts cost what they cost.

  • 6. Test and type approval

    • Test methods and rigs
    • Standards and approval steps
    • Records and traceability
    • Reading a test report

    What is in it for me: You can show a product is safe and ready for the market.

  • 7. Charging and vehicle-to-grid

    • Charger types and connectors
    • Home, work and public charging
    • Grid links and smart charging
    • Planning a charging site

    What is in it for me: You can plan and run charging for homes, fleets and businesses.

  • 8. Lifecycle and circularity

    • Repair, reuse and recycling
    • Handling used batteries safely
    • Cost of ownership
    • Rules on waste and take-back

    What is in it for me: You can reduce waste and build a business around reuse.

first 90 days

Quick wins

  • Day 30

    Safety unit passed and first vehicle diagram read.

  • Day 60

    First bench task done with a mentor.

  • Day 90

    Battery or charging mini-project shown to a partner; first micro-credential in reach.

before opening

Labs and partners checklist

  • An anchor partner such as a workshop, fleet or maker
  • A safe bench area with protective equipment and a trained supervisor
  • Training batteries and a charger that are safe for learners
  • Test tools and a place to keep records
  • A mentor for every small group
  • Written safety rules read and signed by every learner

Safety units come first. Learners work on high-voltage parts only under trained supervision, with the rules above.
