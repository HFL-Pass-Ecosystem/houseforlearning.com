Weather and the sky

Science · Grade 3 · weeks 32 to 36 (5 weeks) · unit G03-SCI-07

By the end, the learner can: link the sun, moon and weather to daily and seasonal change

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US NGSS grade band for grade 3

Key words

sun moon star day night season weather temperature rainfall thermometer

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Day and night

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShine a torch on a globe and spin it.
TeachTeach Earth spins once every day, giving day and night.
PractiseDraw sun and Earth diagrams.
Check and closeExplain in a sentence.

Lesson 2: The moon

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow moon phase pictures.
TeachTeach the moon changes shape because we see different parts lit by the sun.
PractiseOrder phases on a chart.
Check and closeDraw the moon for one week.

Lesson 3: Weather

PhaseWhat happens
EngageLook outside and describe today's weather.
TeachTeach describing weather: sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy, hot, cool; reading a thermometer.
PractiseRecord a weather chart for a week.
Check and closeMake a weather forecast.

Lesson 4: Seasons and clothes

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk what we wear in hot and cool weather.
TeachTeach that there are four seasons in many places and the UAE has a hot summer and a mild winter.
PractiseMatch clothes and activities to seasons.
Check and closeQuick quiz.
