Forces and motion

Science · Grade 3 · weeks 27 to 31 (5 weeks) · unit G03-SCI-06

By the end, the learner can: describe pushes and pulls and the effect of friction

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US NGSS grade band for grade 3

Key words

force push pull friction gravity magnet attract repel speed

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Pushes and pulls

PhaseWhat happens
EngageOpen and close a door.
TeachTeach forces as pushes and pulls that start, stop or change a motion.
PractiseSort actions into pushes and pulls.
Check and closeAdd arrows to pictures.

Lesson 2: Friction

PhaseWhat happens
EngageSlide a block across carpet, wood and a tray.
TeachTeach friction between surfaces and how rough surfaces cause more.
PractiseTest and rank surfaces.
Check and closeExplain one result.

Lesson 3: Gravity

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDrop a ball and a sheet of paper.
TeachTeach gravity as the pull toward the Earth that makes things fall.
PractiseDraw arrows for gravity.
Check and closeDiscuss why we do not float away.

Lesson 4: Magnets

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTest which objects a magnet picks up.
TeachTeach magnets attract iron and steel and have poles that attract or repel.
PractiseSort magnetic and non-magnetic objects.
Check and closeExplain with a diagram.
