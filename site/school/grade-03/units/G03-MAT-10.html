Geometry

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 30 to 31 (2 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-10

By the end, the learner can: name angles, lines and 2D and 3D shapes by their properties

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

angle right angle side corner triangle square rectangle pentagon hexagon cube cone

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Flat shapes

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShape hunt in the room.
TeachTeach sides and corners of triangles, squares, rectangles, pentagons and hexagons.
PractiseSort and name shapes.
Check and closeDraw each shape.

Lesson 2: Angles

PhaseWhat happens
EngageOpen a door; where is the right angle?
TeachTeach right, smaller and bigger angles using a corner of paper.
PractiseCheck six angles.
Check and closeFind angles in the classroom.

Lesson 3: Solid shapes

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePass around a cube, cone, sphere and cylinder.
TeachTeach faces, edges and corners.
PractiseComplete a table.
Check and closeName solids at home.
