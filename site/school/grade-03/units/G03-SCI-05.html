Light and shadows

Science · Grade 3 · weeks 22 to 26 (5 weeks) · unit G03-SCI-05

By the end, the learner can: explain how shadows form and how they change in length

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US NGSS grade band for grade 3

Key words

light source transparent translucent opaque shadow reflect torch sun

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Sources of light

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTurn off the lights and show a torch.
TeachTeach natural and made light sources and that the moon reflects the sun.
PractiseSort pictures.
Check and closeQuick quiz.

Lesson 2: Which materials let light through?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow glass, tissue paper and wood.
TeachTeach transparent, translucent and opaque.
PractiseTest materials with a torch and record.
Check and closeExplain one result.

Lesson 3: Shadows

PhaseWhat happens
EngageMake hand shadows on the wall.
TeachTeach shadows form when an opaque thing blocks light.
PractiseDraw where a shadow falls when the light comes from the left.
Check and closePredict and test.

Lesson 4: Shadows and the sun

PhaseWhat happens
EngageMeasure a shadow stick at 9, 12 and 3.
TeachTeach that shadows are long in the morning, short at noon and long again.
PractiseRecord and graph the lengths.
Check and closeExplain the pattern.
