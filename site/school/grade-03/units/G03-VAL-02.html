Kindness in action

Moral and Value Education · Grade 3 · weeks 10 to 18 (9 weeks) · unit G03-VAL-02

By the end, the learner can: plan a small act of service

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core / NGSS grade 3 band

Key words

kindness help share gentle thankful generous

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Kindness at school

PhaseWhat happens
EngageList kind acts you saw today.
TeachTeach the difference between small and big acts of kindness.
PractisePlan three kind acts for the week.
Check and closeShare.

Lesson 2: Kindness at home

PhaseWhat happens
EngageInterview a partner about home helping.
TeachTeach helping family without being asked.
PractiseDraw a chore chart.
Check and closeReflect.

Lesson 3: Kindness to those in need

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow pictures of a food drive.
TeachTeach caring for others, including people we do not know.
PractiseMake cards for a collection drive.
Check and closeReflect.

Lesson 4: Thankful hearts

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePass a gratitude stone.
TeachTeach saying thank you and writing thank-you notes.
PractiseWrite three thank-yous.
Check and closeShare.
