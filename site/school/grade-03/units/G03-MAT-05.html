Multiplication

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 14 to 17 (4 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-05

By the end, the learner can: use tables to 10, multiply a two-digit number by one digit

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

multiply product times array equal groups times table

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Equal groups and arrays

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShare counters into rows.
TeachTeach multiplication as equal groups and rows and columns.
PractiseDraw arrays and write the sentence.
Check and closeMatch arrays to sentences.

Lesson 2: The tables of 2, 5 and 10

PhaseWhat happens
EngageSkip count aloud.
TeachTeach patterns in the 2, 5 and 10 tables.
PractiseFill table grids.
Check and closeFast-fact game.

Lesson 3: The tables of 3, 4, 6 and 8

PhaseWhat happens
EngageCount and chant.
TeachTeach tricks: double for 4 and 8, 3 plus 3 for 6.
PractisePractise facts.
Check and closeMini quiz.

Lesson 4: The tables of 7 and 9

PhaseWhat happens
EngageFinger trick for 9.
TeachTeach 7 using known facts.
PractiseComplete a full multiplication square.
Check and closeFast quiz.

Lesson 5: Two-digit times one-digit

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow 14 x 3 with blocks.
TeachTeach splitting tens and ones: 14 x 3 = 10 x 3 + 4 x 3.
PractiseSolve ten questions.
Check and closeShare strategies.
