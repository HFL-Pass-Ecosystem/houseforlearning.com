Data handling

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 35 to 36 (2 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-13

By the end, the learner can: read and make tally charts, pictographs and bar graphs

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Tally charts

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTally votes for a favourite fruit.
TeachTeach tally marks in fives.
PractiseMake a tally chart.
Check and closeCount the tallies.

Lesson 2: Reading bar graphs

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a bar graph about lunches.
TeachTeach the title, labels and scale.
PractiseAnswer questions on graphs.
Check and closeWrite two facts about a graph.
