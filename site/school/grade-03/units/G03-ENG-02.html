Subject and predicate

English · Grade 3 · weeks 6 to 10 (5 weeks) · unit G03-ENG-02

By the end, the learner can: find who or what a sentence is about and what it says about it

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core English language arts grade 3

Key words

sentence subject predicate capital letter full stop question mark

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: What makes a sentence?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageWrite "ran the dog" on the board; ask if it makes sense and how to fix it.
TeachTeach: a sentence tells a complete idea, starts with a capital letter and ends with . ? or !.
PractiseLearners sort cards into sentences and non-sentences and fix three.
Check and closeWrite one sentence about today's weather.

Lesson 2: The subject

PhaseWhat happens
EngageSay "The cat sat on the mat"; ask who or what the sentence is about.
TeachTeach: the subject is who or what the sentence is about. Underline the subject in five model sentences.
PractiseLearners underline the subject in ten sentences.
Check and closeQuick poll: each learner shares one subject.

Lesson 3: The predicate

PhaseWhat happens
EngageCover the subject in a sentence; ask what is left.
TeachTeach: the predicate tells what the subject does or is. Draw a line between subject and predicate in model sentences.
PractiseLearners draw the line in ten sentences and colour the subject blue and the predicate green.
Check and closeExit ticket: split one sentence of their own.

Lesson 4: Build and join

PhaseWhat happens
EngageGive each learner half a sentence on a card; they walk to find their match.
TeachTeach: every sentence needs both parts; show how adding detail grows each part.
PractiseLearners build six full sentences from a subject bank and a predicate bank, then write two of their own.
Check and closeRead two sentences aloud; the class decides which part is the subject.

Lesson 5: Practice and review

PhaseWhat happens
EngageMini-whiteboard quiz: subject or predicate?
TeachReview with a game of "sentence builders".
PractiseLearners complete the worksheet.
Check and closeSelf-check with the answer key.
