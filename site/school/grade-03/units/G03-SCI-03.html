Food and digestion

Science · Grade 3 · weeks 12 to 16 (5 weeks) · unit G03-SCI-03

By the end, the learner can: group nutrients and describe the journey of food

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US NGSS grade band for grade 3

Key words

protein carbohydrate fat vitamin mineral fibre digestion stomach intestine teeth

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Food groups

PhaseWhat happens
EngageBring a lunchbox sort.
TeachTeach the groups: energy foods, body-building foods, protective foods, and water.
PractiseSort foods in a table.
Check and closePlan a balanced plate.

Lesson 2: A healthy plate

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow an unhealthy lunch and ask what to change.
TeachTeach portions: half fruit and vegetables, a quarter grains, a quarter protein.
PractiseDraw a plate for breakfast, lunch and dinner.
Check and closeShare and compare.

Lesson 3: The journey of food

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRoll a ball through a long tube as a model.
TeachTeach mouth, food pipe, stomach, small intestine, large intestine.
PractiseLabel the digestive path and write what happens at each part.
Check and closeExplain the journey in three sentences.

Lesson 4: Teeth and care

PhaseWhat happens
EngageLook at your teeth in mirrors and count them.
TeachTeach tooth types (incisors cut, canines tear, molars grind) and caring for teeth.
PractiseDraw a tooth map and make a brushing plan.
Check and closePledge one habit.
