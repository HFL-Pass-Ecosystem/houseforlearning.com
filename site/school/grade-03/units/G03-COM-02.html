Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Hands on the keys

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a keyboard map.
TeachTeach posture and the home row.
PractisePractise home row letters.
Check and closeTyping game.

Lesson 2: Typing words and sentences

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRace to type a short word.
TeachTeach capitals with shift and punctuation.
PractiseType five sentences.
Check and closeCheck accuracy.

Lesson 3: Making text look good

PhaseWhat happens
EngageCompare plain and formatted text.
TeachTeach font, size, bold and colour.
PractiseFormat a title and body.
Check and closeSave.

Lesson 4: Save and print

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a printed page.
TeachTeach save as, folders and print preview.
PractiseSave a document in a folder.
Check and closeQuick check.
