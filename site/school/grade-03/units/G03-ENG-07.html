Dictionary and spelling skills

English · Grade 3 · weeks 31 to 33 (3 weeks) · unit G03-ENG-07

By the end, the learner can: use alphabetical order and a dictionary to find and check words

Key words

alphabetical order dictionary guide word meaning spelling syllable

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Alphabetical order

PhaseWhat happens
EngageLine up by first letter of first name.
TeachTeach ordering by first letter, then second letter when the first matches.
PractiseOrder twenty words.
Check and closeRace: order the class word wall.

Lesson 2: Using a dictionary

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a dictionary and the guide words at the top of the page.
TeachTeach finding a word with the guide words and reading its meaning.
PractiseLook up eight words and copy one meaning each.
Check and closeWrite a sentence with a new word.

Lesson 3: Spelling patterns

PhaseWhat happens
EngageSpell three tricky words together using sound-out and chunk.
TeachTeach look, say, cover, write, check; patterns -ing, -ed, -tion.
PractisePractise ten spelling words.
Check and closeMini quiz.
