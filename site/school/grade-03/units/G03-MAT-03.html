Addition

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 6 to 9 (4 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-03

By the end, the learner can: add up to three-digit numbers with carrying and solve word problems

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

add sum carry regroup total altogether

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Adding without carrying

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow 236 + 142 with base-ten blocks.
TeachTeach adding ones, then tens, then hundreds in a column.
PractiseSolve eight sums.
Check and closeCheck with a number line.

Lesson 2: Adding with carrying

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow 148 + 175; ask what happens when ones make more than nine.
TeachTeach carrying a ten or a hundred with blocks, then a column sum.
PractiseSolve ten sums.
Check and closeEstimate first, then check.

Lesson 3: Word problems with adding

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTell a short shop story.
TeachTeach find the numbers, find the question, choose the operation, check.
PractiseSolve four word problems.
Check and closeExplain one choice aloud.

Lesson 4: Missing number puzzles

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow 245 + ? = 400.
TeachTeach using a bar model for missing number problems.
PractiseSolve six puzzles.
Check and closeReview.
