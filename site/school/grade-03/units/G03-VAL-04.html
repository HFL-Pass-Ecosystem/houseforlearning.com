Caring for the Earth

Moral and Value Education · Grade 3 · weeks 28 to 36 (9 weeks) · unit G03-VAL-04

By the end, the learner can: choose reduce, reuse and recycle actions

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core / NGSS grade 3 band

Key words

reduce reuse recycle litter waste energy environment protect

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Our Earth is special

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a photo of Earth.
TeachTeach that we share one planet and must protect it.
PractiseDraw ways to take care of Earth.
Check and closeShare.

Lesson 2: Reduce, reuse, recycle

PhaseWhat happens
EngageSort rubbish into bins.
TeachTeach the three Rs with examples.
PractiseSort items into reduce, reuse and recycle.
Check and closeQuiz.

Lesson 3: Saving energy and water

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTour the school for lights and taps.
TeachTeach turning off lights and fixing leaks.
PractiseMake a poster and pledge.
Check and closePresent.

Lesson 4: A class action plan

PhaseWhat happens
EngageChoose a class project.
TeachTeach planning a clean-up or garden.
PractiseMake a plan and assign tasks.
Check and closeReflect.

Lesson 5: Plant a tree

PhaseWhat happens
EngageLook at seeds and saplings.
TeachTeach how trees help air and animals; the ghaf tree in the UAE.
PractisePlant or draw.
Check and closePledge.
