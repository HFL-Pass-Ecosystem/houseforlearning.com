Subtraction

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 10 to 13 (4 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-04

By the end, the learner can: subtract up to three-digit numbers with borrowing and check by adding

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

subtract difference borrow regroup take away check

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Subtract without borrowing

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRemove blocks from a number.
TeachTeach column subtraction when each digit on top is bigger.
PractiseSolve eight questions.
Check and closeCheck with addition.

Lesson 2: Subtract with borrowing

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow 342 - 175 and ask what is hard.
TeachTeach exchanging a ten for ten ones and a hundred for ten tens with blocks, then the column.
PractiseSolve eight questions.
Check and closeCheck by adding.

Lesson 3: Word problems with subtracting

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTell a story about a bus.
TeachTeach key words: left, fewer, how many more.
PractiseSolve four problems.
Check and closeShare one method.

Lesson 4: Checking subtraction

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a wrong answer and ask how to find the mistake.
TeachTeach inverse operations: add the answer to the smaller number.
PractiseCheck six answers.
Check and closeReview.
