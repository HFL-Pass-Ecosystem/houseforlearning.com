Verbs and simple tenses

English · Grade 3 · weeks 16 to 20 (5 weeks) · unit G03-ENG-04

By the end, the learner can: use present, past and future verbs correctly

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core English language arts grade 3

Key words

verb present past future yesterday today tomorrow

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Action words

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAct out verbs from a hat for the class to guess.
TeachTeach verbs as words for actions and states (run, is).
PractiseCircle verbs in ten sentences.
Check and closeWrite three sentences with strong verbs.

Lesson 2: Present and past

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow "today we walk" and "yesterday we walked".
TeachTeach how most verbs add -ed for the past and the common irregular verbs (go-went, see-saw, eat-ate).
PractiseChange ten present sentences to the past.
Check and closePlay a past-tense bingo.

Lesson 3: Future with will

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk learners what they will do tomorrow.
TeachTeach will + verb for the future.
PractiseWrite a plan for next weekend with five sentences.
Check and closeShare one plan.

Lesson 4: Matching the verb to the subject

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRead "She like" and "They likes"; ask which sounds wrong.
TeachTeach that singular subjects take -s (She likes) and plural do not (They like).
PractiseFix twelve sentences.
Check and closeSelf-check.
