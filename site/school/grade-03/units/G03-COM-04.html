Online safety

Computing and Digital Skills · Grade 3 · weeks 33 to 36 (4 weeks) · unit G03-COM-04

By the end, the learner can: say what to keep private and who to tell when something feels wrong

Key words

private password stranger screen time trust report kind

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Keeping private things private

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk what we do not tell strangers.
TeachTeach private things: full name, address, phone, school, passwords.
PractiseSort private and safe to share.
Check and closePledge.

Lesson 2: Strong passwords and trusted adults

PhaseWhat happens
EngageCompare strong and weak passwords.
TeachTeach passwords are secret and long; tell a trusted adult.
PractiseRate passwords.
Check and closeRole-play who to tell.

Lesson 3: Kind online

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRead a kind and an unkind message.
TeachTeach to think before you post and to report unkindness.
PractiseRewrite unkind messages.
Check and closePledge.

Lesson 4: Screen time balance

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDraw a day circle.
TeachTeach balancing play, sleep, learning and screens.
PractisePlan a day.
Check and closeShare.
