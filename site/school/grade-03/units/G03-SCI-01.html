Plant life

Science · Grade 3 · weeks 1 to 6 (6 weeks) · unit G03-SCI-01

By the end, the learner can: name parts of a plant and their jobs, and describe how seeds spread

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US NGSS grade band for grade 3

Key words

root stem leaf flower fruit seed germinate pollination sunlight nutrients

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Parts of a plant and their jobs

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a whole plant with roots. Ask what each part might do.
TeachTeach roots (hold the plant, take in water), stem (carries water, holds the plant up), leaves (make food using sunlight), flower (makes seeds), fruit (holds seeds).
PractiseLabel a plant diagram and match each part to its job.
Check and closeSay one job for each part to a partner.

Lesson 2: What plants need

PhaseWhat happens
EngageCompare a healthy plant and a wilted one.
TeachTeach that plants need light, water, air, space and warmth.
PractisePredict what happens when one need is taken away and record in a table.
Check and closeShare predictions.

Lesson 3: Seed to plant

PhaseWhat happens
EngageSoak bean seeds and open one.
TeachTeach the life cycle: seed, germination, seedling, adult plant, flower, fruit and seeds.
PractiseDraw and label the life cycle and set up a bean in a clear bag.
Check and closeRecord Day 1 observations.

Lesson 4: How seeds travel

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow winged, spiky and fluffy seeds.
TeachTeach dispersal by wind, water, animals and bursting pods.
PractiseSort real or pictured seeds by how they travel.
Check and closeDesign a seed that floats on water.

Lesson 5: Investigation: does a plant need light?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk: what do you think will happen in a dark cupboard?
TeachTeach a fair test: change one thing only (light), keep water and soil the same.
PractiseSet up two cress pots; record every two days for a week.
Check and closeShare results with a drawing and a sentence.

Lesson 6: Plants we use

PhaseWhat happens
EngageMake a class shopping list of plant things we use.
TeachTeach food, cloth, wood and medicine from plants, and why date palms matter in the UAE.
PractiseSort pictures into groups.
Check and closeChoose a plant and say three ways it helps us.
