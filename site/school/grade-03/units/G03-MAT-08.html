Time

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 26 to 27 (2 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-08

By the end, the learner can: read clocks to the minute, use a.m. and p.m. and find elapsed time

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

hour hand minute hand a.m. p.m. quarter past half past quarter to elapsed time

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Reading the hour and half hour

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a big clock and set it to 3:00 and 3:30.
TeachTeach the hands and o'clock and half past.
PractiseRead clocks.
Check and closeSet the clock for a partner.

Lesson 2: Five-minute intervals

PhaseWhat happens
EngageCount in fives around the clock.
TeachTeach the minute hand and reading to five minutes.
PractiseRead twelve clocks.
Check and closeDraw hands on blank clocks.

Lesson 3: Minute by minute and a.m. / p.m.

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk what time we start school and finish.
TeachTeach reading to the minute, and a.m. and p.m.
PractiseRead and write times.
Check and closeQuick quiz.

Lesson 4: Elapsed time

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDraw a timeline of a school morning.
TeachTeach counting on to find how long.
PractiseSolve eight problems.
Check and closeSelf-check.
