Place value

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 4 to 5 (2 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-02

By the end, the learner can: show hundreds, tens and ones and round to the nearest ten

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

place value expanded form round nearest ten digit

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Hundreds, tens, ones

PhaseWhat happens
EngageBuild 356 with blocks.
TeachTeach place value and expanded form with the place value chart.
PractiseWrite numbers in expanded form.
Check and closeCheck with blocks.

Lesson 2: Value of a digit

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow 4 in 407, 740 and 874; ask if it means the same each time.
TeachTeach that a digit's value depends on its place.
PractiseComplete a value table.
Check and closeQuick quiz.

Lesson 3: Rounding to the nearest ten

PhaseWhat happens
EngageStand on a hill number line; which way does the ball roll?
TeachTeach: look at the ones digit; 5 or more round up, 4 or less round down.
PractiseRound twelve numbers.
Check and closeSelf-check.
