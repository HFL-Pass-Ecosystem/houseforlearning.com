Rocks, soil and water

Science · Grade 3 · weeks 17 to 21 (5 weeks) · unit G03-SCI-04

By the end, the learner can: compare soils and describe how water is used and saved

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US NGSS grade band for grade 3

Key words

rock soil sand clay humus layer reservoir desalination conserve

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Rocks

PhaseWhat happens
EngageHold a smooth and a rough rock.
TeachTeach properties: hard, soft, rough, smooth, heavy, light. Rocks are used for building.
PractiseTest and sort rocks by properties.
Check and closeRecord results.

Lesson 2: Soil

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDig up a spoon of soil in a clear cup.
TeachTeach soil as bits of rock plus humus; sand, clay and loam.
PractiseShake soil with water in a jar and observe layers.
Check and closeDraw the layers.

Lesson 3: Where water comes from

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a water bottle label.
TeachTeach sources: rain, rivers, wells, sea water made drinkable by desalination in the UAE.
PractiseDraw the journey of water to our home.
Check and closeQuiz.

Lesson 4: Saving water

PhaseWhat happens
EngageCompare a dripping tap and a closed one.
TeachTeach ways to save water at home and school.
PractiseCreate a poster and a class water pledge.
Check and closeCount how many saving ideas we have.
