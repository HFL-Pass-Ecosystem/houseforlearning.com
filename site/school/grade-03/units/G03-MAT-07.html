Fractions

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 22 to 25 (4 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-07

By the end, the learner can: name, compare and add simple fractions of a whole and of a set

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

fraction numerator denominator half quarter third equal parts

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Equal parts

PhaseWhat happens
EngageFold paper into halves, quarters and eighths.
TeachTeach that fractions need equal parts; name the parts.
PractiseShade and name fractions.
Check and closeCheck by folding.

Lesson 2: Reading and writing fractions

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a pizza cut into 8.
TeachTeach numerator and denominator.
PractiseMatch pictures and fractions.
Check and closeQuick quiz.

Lesson 3: Comparing fractions

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTwo chocolate bars of the same size.
TeachTeach comparing with the same denominator and with fraction bars.
PractisePut symbols in boxes.
Check and closeChallenge cards.

Lesson 4: Fraction of a set

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShare 12 counters into 3 groups.
TeachTeach finding a fraction of a number.
PractiseSolve eight questions.
Check and closeCheck by drawing.

Lesson 5: Adding fractions

PhaseWhat happens
EngageFill a jug in parts.
TeachTeach adding fractions with the same denominator.
PractiseSolve eight sums.
Check and closeReview.
