The United Arab Emirates

Social Studies · Grade 3 · weeks 1 to 12 (12 weeks) · unit G03-SST-01

By the end, the learner can: locate the seven emirates, describe leaders, symbols, landmarks and heritage

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core / NGSS grade 3 band

Key words

emirate capital federation flag symbol ruler unity Founding Father currency

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Where are we?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a world map and find the UAE.
TeachTeach the UAE is a country in the Arabian Peninsula by the Gulf.
PractisePoint to the UAE and neighbours on a map.
Check and closeColour the map.

Lesson 2: The seven emirates

PhaseWhat happens
EngageChant the names with a rhythm.
TeachTeach the seven: Abu Dhabi, Dubai, Sharjah, Ajman, Umm Al Quwain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah; Abu Dhabi is the capital.
PractiseLabel an outline map.
Check and closeQuiz.

Lesson 3: The flag and symbols

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow the flag and ask what the colours may mean.
TeachTeach the four colours (red, green, white and black), the falcon as national bird, the Arabian oryx as national animal and the ghaf tree.
PractiseDraw the flag; match symbols to pictures.
Check and closeDescribe one symbol.

Lesson 4: How the UAE began

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a timeline from the 1960s to 1971.
TeachTeach that seven emirates joined on 2 December 1971 and the Founding Father led this union.
PractiseComplete a timeline strip.
Check and closeRetell in three sentences.

Lesson 5: People who lead

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRole-play a class council.
TeachTeach the roles of rulers and the President in simple words.
PractiseMatch roles to jobs.
Check and closeWrite one question for a leader.

Lesson 6: Famous places

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow landmark photos.
TeachTeach landmarks: Burj Khalifa, Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Louvre Abu Dhabi, Jebel Jais.
PractiseMatch landmarks to emirates.
Check and closeDraw a postcard.

Lesson 7: National Day project

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePlan a celebration.
TeachTeach how people celebrate with respect and unity.
PractiseMake a poster.
Check and closePresent in groups.
