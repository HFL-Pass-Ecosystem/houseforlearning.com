Heritage and traditions

Social Studies · Grade 3 · weeks 31 to 36 (6 weeks) · unit G03-SST-04

By the end, the learner can: describe traditional life in the Gulf and what we keep today

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core / NGSS grade 3 band

Key words

heritage tradition pearl diving dhow majlis falconry souq Bedouin date palm coffee

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Life in the past

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow old photos of the Gulf.
TeachTeach how people lived by the sea and in the desert.
PractiseMake a two-column chart of then and now.
Check and closeDiscuss what is the same.

Lesson 2: Traditions we keep

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTaste dates and Arabic coffee if allowed, else show pictures.
TeachTeach hospitality, the majlis, falconry and the souq.
PractiseWrite a postcard about a tradition.
Check and closePresent.

Lesson 3: Heritage project

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePlan a heritage corner.
TeachTeach respect for heritage places.
PractiseMake a model or poster.
Check and closeGallery walk.
