Paragraph writing

English · Grade 3 · weeks 21 to 25 (5 weeks) · unit G03-ENG-05

By the end, the learner can: write a paragraph with a topic sentence and supporting sentences

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core English language arts grade 3

Key words

paragraph topic sentence supporting sentence detail conclusion indent

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: What is a paragraph?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a jumble of sentences about one idea and one stray sentence; find the odd one out.
TeachTeach: a paragraph is a group of sentences about one idea, with a topic sentence first.
PractiseLearners mark the topic sentence and cross out the odd one in three samples.
Check and closeWrite one topic sentence about their favourite game.

Lesson 2: Supporting details

PhaseWhat happens
EngageBrainstorm three facts about camels together.
TeachTeach adding detail with who, what, where, why and with joining words (and, because, but).
PractiseAdd three supporting sentences to a given topic sentence.
Check and closeRead one paragraph aloud and spot the details.

Lesson 3: Planning with a web

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDraw a spider web on the board for "My school day".
TeachTeach planning: centre idea, three branches, then write.
PractiseLearners make their own web and write the topic sentence.
Check and closeShare webs in pairs.

Lesson 4: Write and check

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRead a model paragraph slowly.
TeachTeach a checklist: topic sentence, details, closing sentence, capitals, full stops.
PractiseWrite a paragraph of five to six sentences from the web.
Check and closeCheck against the list and fix one thing.

Lesson 5: Publish

PhaseWhat happens
EngageChoose a favourite paragraph to share.
TeachTeach neat handwriting and adding a title.
PractiseCopy the final paragraph with a title and a drawing.
Check and closeDisplay and read aloud.
