Animal life and adaptation

Science · Grade 3 · weeks 7 to 11 (5 weeks) · unit G03-SCI-02

By the end, the learner can: link body features to habitats including desert and sea

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US NGSS grade band for grade 3

Key words

mammal bird reptile fish insect habitat adaptation herbivore carnivore omnivore

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Animal groups

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow cards with animals; sort them any way.
TeachTeach five groups by body covering and how they breathe and reproduce: mammals (fur or hair, feed milk), birds (feathers, eggs), reptiles (dry scales), fish (gills, scales), insects (six legs).
PractiseSort twenty animal cards and record in a table.
Check and closeOdd one out game.

Lesson 2: Habitats

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow pictures of desert, sea, forest and polar places.
TeachTeach that a habitat gives food, water and shelter.
PractiseMatch animals to habitats and say why.
Check and closeDraw a habitat poster.

Lesson 3: Adaptation

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk why a camel can live in the desert.
TeachTeach adaptation using a camel (humps store fat, wide feet, long eyelashes), a fish (gills, fins), a bird (wings, light bones).
PractiseChoose two animals and match body parts to jobs.
Check and closeExplain one adaptation to a partner.

Lesson 4: What animals eat

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow teeth pictures of a cow and a lion.
TeachTeach herbivore, carnivore and omnivore, and teeth shapes.
PractiseSort animals by diet.
Check and closeCreate a simple food chain.
