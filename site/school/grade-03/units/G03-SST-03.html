Communities and helpers

Social Studies · Grade 3 · weeks 22 to 30 (9 weeks) · unit G03-SST-03

By the end, the learner can: describe how people work together and why services matter

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core / NGSS grade 3 band

Key words

community helper service police doctor teacher firefighter cooperate

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Our community

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDraw your neighbourhood.
TeachTeach a community as people living and working together.
PractiseList places and people in our community.
Check and closeShare.

Lesson 2: Community helpers

PhaseWhat happens
EngageMime a helper for others to guess.
TeachTeach the jobs of doctors, police, firefighters, teachers, waste collectors.
PractiseMatch helpers to jobs.
Check and closeWrite a thank-you card.

Lesson 3: Services we share

PhaseWhat happens
EngageList things we all use, such as roads and water.
TeachTeach public services and how they are paid for by all.
PractiseSort services.
Check and closeDiscuss caring for shared places.

Lesson 4: Working together

PhaseWhat happens
EngageBuild a tower in teams.
TeachTeach cooperation and fair rules.
PractisePlan a clean-up.
Check and closeReflect.
