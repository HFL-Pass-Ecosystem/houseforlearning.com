Measurement

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 28 to 29 (2 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-09

By the end, the learner can: measure length, mass and capacity and convert between metric units

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

centimetre metre kilometre gram kilogram millilitre litre capacity mass

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Length

PhaseWhat happens
EngageMeasure a desk with hand spans, then a ruler.
TeachTeach cm, m and km and choosing the right unit.
PractiseMeasure six objects.
Check and closeEstimate then measure.

Lesson 2: Mass

PhaseWhat happens
EngageHold two objects and guess which is heavier.
TeachTeach g and kg and reading a scale.
PractiseWeigh five objects.
Check and closeCompare estimates.

Lesson 3: Capacity

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePour water between jugs.
TeachTeach ml and l and reading a jug.
PractiseRead measuring jars.
Check and closeQuick quiz.

Lesson 4: Converting units

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow 1 m = 100 cm on a metre stick.
TeachTeach the conversions.
PractiseComplete a conversion table.
Check and closeCheck six answers.
