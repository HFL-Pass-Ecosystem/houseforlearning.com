Key words

mistake learn try again brave honest sorry forgive

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Everyone makes mistakes

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTell a short story about a mistake.
TeachTeach mistakes help us learn.
PractiseShare a mistake and a lesson in pairs.
Check and closeClass chart.

Lesson 2: Saying sorry and fixing it

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRole-play spilling water.
TeachTeach apologising and putting things right.
PractiseWrite a sorry note.
Check and closeShare.

Lesson 3: Trying again

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTry a tricky puzzle.
TeachTeach perseverance and kind self-talk.
PractiseMake a "yet" poster.
Check and closePledge.

Lesson 4: Forgiving others

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRole-play a friend breaking a toy.
TeachTeach forgiveness and kind words.
PractiseDraw a forgiveness card.
Check and closeReflect.
