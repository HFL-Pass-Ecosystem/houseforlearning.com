Pictures and shapes in a drawing program

Computing and Digital Skills · Grade 3 · weeks 1 to 14 (14 weeks) · unit G03-COM-01

By the end, the learner can: rotate, resize, colour and save a picture

Key words

pixel tool shape fill brush undo save file rotate resize flip

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Opening a drawing program

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a finished picture.
TeachTeach opening, naming and saving a file.
PractisePractise opening and saving.
Check and closeShow a file in the folder.

Lesson 2: Shape and line tools

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDraw shapes on paper.
TeachTeach the shape tools, line tool, colours and fill.
PractiseDraw a house with shapes.
Check and closeCheck saved file.

Lesson 3: Brush and eraser

PhaseWhat happens
EngageFree draw.
TeachTeach brush sizes and the eraser.
PractiseDraw and colour a scene.
Check and closeSave.

Lesson 4: Rotate, flip and resize

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow a picture and rotate it.
TeachTeach rotate, flip and resize.
PractiseMake a pattern with flips.
Check and closeSave.

Lesson 5: Select, copy and paste

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk how to draw ten stars quickly.
TeachTeach selecting and copying parts.
PractiseMake a repeat pattern.
Check and closeSave and share.

Lesson 6: Colour mixing and gradients

PhaseWhat happens
EngageMix paint on paper.
TeachTeach the colour picker and mixing in the program.
PractiseColour a rainbow.
Check and closeSave.

Lesson 7: Project

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePlan a picture.
TeachTeach planning with a sketch.
PractiseMake a final picture with title.
Check and closePresent in pairs.
