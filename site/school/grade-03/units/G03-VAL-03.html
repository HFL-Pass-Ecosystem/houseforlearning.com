Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: What makes a team?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageBuild a paper tower in teams.
TeachTeach team goals and roles.
PractiseAssign roles and finish a tower.
Check and closeReflect.

Lesson 2: Listening and speaking in a team

PhaseWhat happens

PractiseMake a team plan.
Check and closeReview.

Lesson 3: Fair share of jobs

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDivide a classroom task unfairly then fairly.
TeachTeach fairness.
PractiseCreate a job chart.
Check and closeReview.

PhaseWhat happens

TeachTeach compromise.
