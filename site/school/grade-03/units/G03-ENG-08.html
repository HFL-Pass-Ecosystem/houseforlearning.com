Speaking and listening

By the end, the learner can: give a one-minute talk and ask a question about a talk

listen speak volume eye contact question audience

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Good listening

PhaseWhat happens
EngageWhisper a message down a line and compare the end.
TeachTeach listening rules: eyes on the speaker, no interrupting, ask questions after.
PractiseListen to a short talk and note two facts.
Check and closeShare facts with a partner.

Lesson 2: One-minute talk

PhaseWhat happens
EngageModel a one-minute talk about a favourite place.
TeachTeach beginning, three points, ending; clear voice, eye contact.
PractisePlan and practise in pairs.
Check and closeGive one star and one wish to a partner.

Lesson 3: Asking questions

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePlay "twenty questions" with a mystery object.
TeachTeach good questions (who, what, why, how) and polite words.
PractisePrepare two questions for each speaker.
Check and closeClass talk time.
