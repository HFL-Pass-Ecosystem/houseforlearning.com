Nouns, pronouns and adjectives

English · Grade 3 · weeks 11 to 15 (5 weeks) · unit G03-ENG-03

By the end, the learner can: use them correctly and for variety

Key words

noun pronoun adjective common noun proper noun he she it they

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Naming words

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePlay "I spy" with nouns in the room.
TeachTeach nouns for people, animals, places and things. Common nouns are general; proper nouns name one and start with a capital letter.
PractiseSort twenty words into common and proper nouns.
Check and closeWrite three proper nouns about our town.

Lesson 2: Pronouns

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRead a short paragraph with the same name repeated; ask what sounds odd.
TeachTeach pronouns: he, she, it, they, we, I, you, and how they stand in for nouns.
PractiseReplace repeated nouns with pronouns in a short text.
Check and closeWrite two sentences using she and they.

Lesson 3: Adjectives

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDescribe a mystery object in a bag using only words about how it looks and feels.
TeachTeach adjectives as words that describe nouns; place before the noun and after is or are.
PractiseAdd adjectives to ten plain sentences to make them richer.
Check and closeWrite a four-line description of a desert animal.

Lesson 4: Putting it together

PhaseWhat happens
EngageWord-class sort game in teams.
TeachTeach how to spot all three in one sentence.
PractiseColour nouns blue, pronouns red and adjectives green in a short text.
Check and closeExit ticket: write one sentence with all three.
