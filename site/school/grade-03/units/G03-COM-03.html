Algorithms and loops without a computer

Computing and Digital Skills · Grade 3 · weeks 24 to 32 (9 weeks) · unit G03-COM-03

By the end, the learner can: write and test a sequence of steps with a repeat

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core / NGSS grade 3 band

Key words

algorithm step sequence loop repeat debug bug instruction

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: What is an algorithm?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageGive a robot-partner instructions to make a sandwich.
TeachTeach algorithm as a set of steps in order.
PractiseWrite steps for brushing teeth.
Check and closeTest each other's algorithms.

Lesson 2: Giving clear instructions

PhaseWhat happens
EngageGuide a partner through a maze on the floor.
TeachTeach clear short instructions with arrows.
PractiseWrite an algorithm for a grid.
Check and closeTest.

Lesson 3: Loops

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow repeating dance moves.
TeachTeach a loop as repeating steps (repeat 4 times).
PractiseRewrite long algorithms using loops.
Check and closeTest.

Lesson 4: Finding bugs

PhaseWhat happens
EngageGive a wrong algorithm.
TeachTeach debugging: test, find the error, fix.
PractiseDebug three algorithms.
Check and closeReflect.

Lesson 5: Mini challenge

PhaseWhat happens
EngageDesign a dance.
TeachTeach combining sequence and loops.
PractiseWrite and perform.
Check and closeReview.
