Reading stories and poems

English · Grade 3 · weeks 1 to 5 (5 weeks) · unit G03-ENG-01

By the end, the learner can: read a story or poem and say the main idea and favourite part

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core English language arts grade 3

Key words

character setting beginning middle end main idea rhyme rhythm

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: The parts of a story

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShow three pictures out of order; learners put them in order and say what happens first, next, last.
TeachRead the passage aloud. Name the character (Salma), the setting (a quiet beach) and the beginning, middle and end.
PractiseIn pairs, fill a three-box story map for the passage.
Check and closeEach learner says one sentence about the end of the story.

Lesson 2: Who, what, where, when, why

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk five quick questions about the classroom; learners answer in full sentences.
TeachTeach the five question words and what kind of answer each one wants. Model finding the answer in the text with a finger.
PractiseLearners answer six questions about the passage, underlining the line that proves each answer.
Check and closeSwap papers and check one answer together.

Lesson 3: Main idea and favourite part

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk: if you told a friend this story in one sentence, what would you say?
TeachTeach main idea (the big point) against details (small facts). Model one main idea sentence for the passage.
PractiseLearners write the main idea and draw their favourite part with a caption.
Check and closeShare captions; the class votes for the clearest main idea sentence.

Lesson 4: Reading a poem aloud

PhaseWhat happens
EngageClap the beat of a nursery rhyme together.
TeachRead a short poem twice: first for meaning, then for rhythm. Point out rhyming words.
PractiseLearners find rhyming pairs and practise reading in groups, then perform one verse.
Check and closeEach group performs; the class gives one kind compliment and one tip.

Lesson 5: Retell and respond

PhaseWhat happens
EngagePass a soft ball; each learner adds one sentence to a class retelling.
TeachTeach retelling with first, then, next, finally.
PractiseLearners retell the passage in their own words to a partner, then write four sentences.
Check and closeRead aloud one retelling; check it has a beginning, middle and end.

Home practice
