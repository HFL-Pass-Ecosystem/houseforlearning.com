Maps and directions

Social Studies · Grade 3 · weeks 13 to 21 (9 weeks) · unit G03-SST-02

By the end, the learner can: read a simple map using a key and compass points

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core / NGSS grade 3 band

Key words

map key symbol compass north south east west direction route

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: What is a map?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageLook at a treasure map.
TeachTeach that a map is a drawing of a place from above.
PractiseDraw a map of our classroom.
Check and closeCompare maps.

Lesson 2: Map keys and symbols

PhaseWhat happens
EngageMatch symbols to places.
TeachTeach reading a key.
PractiseComplete a key for a school map.
Check and closeQuiz.

Lesson 3: The compass

PhaseWhat happens
EngageStand in the playground and find north.
TeachTeach N, S, E and W.
PractiseLabel a compass rose and follow directions on a grid.
Check and closeGive directions to a partner.

Lesson 4: Routes and directions

PhaseWhat happens
EngageWalk the route to the library.
TeachTeach giving and following directions.
PractiseWrite steps from the gate to the classroom.
Check and closeCheck each other.
