Poetry and nonsense verse

English · Grade 3 · weeks 26 to 30 (5 weeks) · unit G03-ENG-06

By the end, the learner can: read limericks, notice rhyme and rhythm and write one

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) CBSE-style class 3 topic outline (public) England national curriculum Year 4 (age 8 to 9) US Common Core English language arts grade 3

Key words

limerick rhyme rhythm nonsense line verse public domain

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: What is a limerick?

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRead the first limerick aloud with a bouncy beat.
TeachTeach: five lines; lines 1, 2 and 5 rhyme; lines 3 and 4 are shorter and rhyme with each other (pattern AABBA).
PractiseLearners label the rhyme pattern with letters on both limericks.
Check and closeClap the rhythm of line 1.

Lesson 2: Funny pictures from words

PhaseWhat happens
EngageAsk learners to draw the Old Man and what he found.
TeachTeach that nonsense verse uses silly ideas and exaggeration on purpose.
PractiseDraw and caption one limerick.
Check and closeShare and explain the funniest idea.

Lesson 3: Hunt for rhymes

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRhyme chain game: each learner says a word that rhymes with "chin".
TeachTeach rhyme families (chin, pin, thin, win, begin) and how to check ending sounds.
PractiseBuild rhyme lists for beard, hen, sea and tree.
Check and closeChoose the best rhyme pair for a new limerick.

Lesson 4: Write a limerick

PhaseWhat happens
EngageRead a model limerick about a school camel.
TeachTeach: pick a character and place, rhyme the first two and the last line, then fit the short lines.
PractiseDraft a limerick and read it to a partner.
Check and closeImprove one line after feedback.
