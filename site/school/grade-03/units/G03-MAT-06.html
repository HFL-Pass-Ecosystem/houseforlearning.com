Division

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 18 to 21 (4 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-06

By the end, the learner can: divide as sharing and grouping, link to tables, with remainders

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

divide quotient remainder share group divisor dividend

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Sharing equally

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShare 12 counters among 3 children.
TeachTeach division as sharing and as grouping.
PractiseDraw and solve eight questions.
Check and closeMatch words to sums.

Lesson 2: Division facts

PhaseWhat happens
EngageFact family: 3 x 4 = 12, 12 / 4 = 3.
TeachTeach division as the opposite of multiplication.
PractiseComplete fact family cards.
Check and closeQuick quiz.

Lesson 3: Remainders

PhaseWhat happens
EngageShare 13 sweets among 4. What is left?
TeachTeach remainders and when they matter.
PractiseSolve ten questions with remainders.
Check and closeCheck with multiplication.

Lesson 4: Word problems with division

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTell a story about sharing dates.
TeachTeach reading the question and choosing what to share.
PractiseSolve four word problems.
Check and closeExplain an answer.
