Numbers to 1000

Mathematics · Grade 3 · weeks 1 to 3 (3 weeks) · unit G03-MAT-01

By the end, the learner can: read, write, order and compare numbers to 1000

UAE ministry curriculum framework (public outline) national-curriculum-style class 3 topic outline (public) US Common Core mathematics grade 3

Key words

hundreds tens ones digit greater than less than ascending descending

Lesson plans

Each lesson is about 45 minutes: engage (5 to 8), teach (12 to 15), practise (15), check and close (5 to 8).

Lesson 1: Counting to 1000

PhaseWhat happens
EngageCount in tens and hundreds aloud as a class.
TeachTeach hundreds, tens and ones with a number line and place value blocks.
PractiseRead and write ten numbers in digits and words.
Check and closeExit slip: write 507 in words.

Lesson 2: Ordering and comparing

PhaseWhat happens
EngageTwo learners show three-digit cards; the class says which is greater.
TeachTeach comparing hundreds first, then tens, then ones, and the symbols > and <.
PractiseOrder sets of numbers and put symbols in the boxes.
Check and closeChallenge pair game.

Lesson 3: Before, after and between

PhaseWhat happens
EngageHide a number on a number line and guess.
TeachTeach number before and after and counting on from any number.
PractiseComplete number lines and fill missing numbers.
Check and closeCheck with a partner.

Skills assessed

Home practice

Count in tens from 120 to 300 with a family member.
