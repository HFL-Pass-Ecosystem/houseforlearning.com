SA1: Science

Grade 3 · Summative assessment 1 (term 1 and 2 learning to week 18) · 25 marks · 50 minutes

Name: ____________________Class: ________Date: ____________Marks: ______ / 25
Answer every question. Show your thinking where asked. Write neatly.

Section A: short questions (1 mark each)

1.Name one fruit that has seeds you can see.
2.What is germination?
3.Why do plants need sunlight?
4.Which part of a plant takes in water from the soil?
5.Put the life cycle in order: seedling, seed, flower, adult plant.
6.Name a mammal that lives in water.
7.What is a habitat?
8.Is a lion a herbivore or a carnivore?
9.Is a snake a mammal, a bird or a reptile?
10.Complete the food chain: grass → ___ → lion.
11.Which part of the body breaks food into small pieces first?
12.How many times a day should we brush our teeth?
13.Put in order: stomach, mouth, small intestine, food pipe.
14.Why do we need water?
15.Name one body-building food.
16.Name one use of rock.
17.What is soil made of?
18.What does desalination do?
19.Why do plants grow better in soil with humus?
20.Is water a renewable or a limited resource? Explain in one sentence.

Section B: extended task (5 marks)

Plan a fair test to find which of three materials makes the best shade. Say what you change, what you keep the same, what you measure and what you expect.
