SA1: Social Studies

Grade 3 · Summative assessment 1 (term 1 and 2 learning to week 18) · 25 marks · 50 minutes

Name: ____________________Class: ________Date: ____________Marks: ______ / 25
Answer every question. Show your thinking where asked. Write neatly.

Section A: short questions (1 mark each)

1.Which emirate has the capital city of the UAE?
2.What is the UAE National Day date?
3.Name one national symbol besides the flag.
4.When did the seven emirates form one country?
5.Name the capital of the UAE.
6.Name the four colours of the UAE flag.
7.What is the currency of the UAE?
8.Why is unity important for a country?
9.What is the national bird of the UAE?
10.How many emirates make up the UAE?
11.In which emirate is the Burj Khalifa?
12.What do we call the small drawing that shows directions on a map?
13.Which direction is opposite to east?
14.Name one thing a key can show.
15.Why do maps use symbols?
16.What is a map?
17.What does a map key show?
18.If you walk south, which direction is behind you?
19.Which tool tells us where north is?
20.If you face north and turn right, which direction do you face?

Section B: extended task (5 marks)

Make a fact file about one emirate or one UAE landmark. Give its name, where it is, two facts and one reason people value it.
