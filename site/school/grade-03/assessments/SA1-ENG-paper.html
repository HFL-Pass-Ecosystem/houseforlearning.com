SA1: English

Grade 3 · Summative assessment 1 (term 1 and 2 learning to week 18) · 25 marks · 50 minutes

Name: ____________________Class: ________Date: ____________Marks: ______ / 25
Answer every question. Show your thinking where asked. Write neatly.

Section A: short questions (1 mark each)

1.Which word rhymes with "beach": reach, bend, bell?
2.What is the "main idea" of a text?
3.What colour was the kite?
4.Where did they find the kite?
5.Find two words in the story that name a place.
6.Underline the subject: Our class visited the museum.
7.Underline the predicate: Dolphins swim in the sea.
8.Which punctuation mark ends a question?
9.Underline the subject: My little brother sleeps late.
10.Is this a sentence? "Runs fast the horse." Write Yes or No and fix it.
11.Underline the pronoun: She likes dates.
12.Name two adjectives that describe the sun.
13.Which word is a pronoun? Table, she, quickly
14.Circle the proper noun: dog, Dubai, city
15.Underline the adjective: The sandy beach was warm.
16.What is the past tense of "see"?
17.Choose: He (play / plays) in the garden.
18.Change to the future: I play football.
19.Write the past tense of "go".
20.Write the past tense of "eat".

Section B: extended task (5 marks)

Write a paragraph of four or five sentences about your favourite place in the UAE.
