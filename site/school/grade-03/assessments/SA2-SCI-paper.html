SA2: Science

Grade 3 · Summative assessment 2 (second half of the year, with key skills carried forward) · 25 marks · 50 minutes

Name: ____________________Class: ________Date: ____________Marks: ______ / 25
Answer every question. Show your thinking where asked. Write neatly.

Section A: short questions (1 mark each)

1.Name one use of rock.
2.What is soil made of?
3.Name two sources of water.
4.What does desalination do?
5.Why do plants grow better in soil with humus?
6.Name an opaque object.
7.Why do we see a shadow on the ground?
8.Name one light source.
9.A torch is moved closer to a toy. What happens to the shadow?
10.When is a shadow shortest in the day?
11.Name one force.
12.Does a magnet attract wood?
13.What does friction do to a moving thing?
14.Why do we put grip on sports shoes?
15.When two north poles are brought together, do they attract or repel?
16.Name a measuring tool for rainfall.
17.How long does the Earth take to spin once?
18.Name one planet.
19.Does the sun move around the Earth or does the Earth spin?
20.What causes day and night?

Section B: extended task (5 marks)

Plan a fair test to find which surface gives the most friction for a toy car. Say what you change, what you keep the same, what you measure and what you expect.
