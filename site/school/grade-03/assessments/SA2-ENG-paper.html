SA2: English

Grade 3 · Summative assessment 2 (second half of the year, with key skills carried forward) · 25 marks · 50 minutes

Name: ____________________Class: ________Date: ____________Marks: ______ / 25
Answer every question. Show your thinking where asked. Write neatly.

Section A: short questions (1 mark each)

1.What is the past tense of "see"?
2.Choose: He (play / plays) in the garden.
3.Choose the right verb: The boys (run / runs) fast.
4.Write the past tense of "walk".
5.What goes at the start of a paragraph?
6.What does "because" show?
7.Cross out the sentence that does not belong: "I love my school. My teacher is kind. Bananas are yellow. I have many friends."
8.Which sentence is the topic sentence? (a) Camels live in the desert. (b) They have humps. (c) They can go many days without water.
9.How many sentences are in a good short paragraph? Choose: 1, 5 to 6, 20
10.How many syllables are in the word "limerick"?
11.Which word rhymes with "pin": thin, pen, pan?
12.What does nonsense verse try to be?
13.Why is the limerick funny?
14.Which animals nested in the old man's beard?
15.Which comes first: lemon or lime?
16.How many syllables in "elephant"?
17.Spell the word that means "not wet".
18.What should a speaker do with their eyes?
19.Why do we speak clearly?
20.Which question word asks for a reason? Who, why, where

Section B: extended task (5 marks)

Write a paragraph of four or five sentences about a day at school.
