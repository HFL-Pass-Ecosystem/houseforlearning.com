Speaking and listening

English · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Write two rules for good listening.
Answer: For example: look at the speaker; do not interrupt.
2.Which question word asks for a reason? Who, why, where
Answer: why
3.Write one polite way to ask a question.
Answer: For example: Excuse me, may I ask why...?
4.Plan a one-minute talk about your favourite place: write the opening line.
Answer: Any clear opening sentence.
5.List three points for your talk.
Answer: Any three relevant points.
6.Write one star and one wish for a classmate's talk.
Answer: A kind positive comment and a helpful suggestion.
