Animal life and adaptation

Science · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name an animal with feathers.
Answer: Any bird, for example falcon or parrot.
2.Is a snake a mammal, a bird or a reptile?
Answer: A reptile.
3.Give one adaptation of a camel and say how it helps.
Answer: For example: wide feet stop it sinking in sand; the hump stores fat for energy.
4.What do all habitats provide?
Answer: Food, water and shelter.
5.Match: herbivore, carnivore, omnivore with: eats only plants, eats only meat, eats both.
Answer: Herbivore eats only plants; carnivore eats only meat; omnivore eats both.
6.Name one animal that lives in the sea and one that lives in the desert.
Answer: Sea: for example shark or turtle. Desert: for example camel or oryx.
7.How many legs does an insect have?
Answer: Six.
8.Why do fish have gills?
Answer: To take oxygen from the water.
9.Complete the food chain: grass → ___ → lion.
Answer: An animal that eats grass, for example zebra.
10.Draw an animal and label two parts that help it survive.
Answer: Marked with the labelling rubric.
