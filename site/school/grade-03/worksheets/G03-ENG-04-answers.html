Verbs and simple tenses

English · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Circle the verb: The fish swims fast.
Answer: swims
2.Write the past tense of "walk".
Answer: walked
3.Write the past tense of "go".
Answer: went
4.Write the past tense of "eat".
Answer: ate
5.Change to the future: I play football.
Answer: I will play football.
6.Choose the right verb: She (like / likes) mangoes.
Answer: likes
7.Choose the right verb: The boys (run / runs) fast.
Answer: run
8.Write a sentence about yesterday using a past verb.
Answer: Any correct past tense sentence.
9.Write a sentence about tomorrow using "will".
Answer: Any correct sentence with will + verb.
10.Find two verbs: Mum cooked rice and we ate it.
Answer: cooked, ate
