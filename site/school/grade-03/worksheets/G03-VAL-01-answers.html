Learning from mistakes

Moral and Value Education · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Why can mistakes help us?
Answer: We learn what to do differently.
2.What are three steps after making a mistake?
Answer: Say sorry if needed, fix it, learn for next time.
3.Write a kind thing to say to a friend who made a mistake.
Answer: Any kind sentence.
4.Finish the sentence: "I can't do it ___."
Answer: yet
5.Draw a time you tried again.
Answer: Any relevant drawing and a caption.
6.Why is it brave to say sorry?
Answer: It takes courage to admit a mistake.
7.What can you do if you spill something?
Answer: Tell the adult, clean it, say sorry.
8.Write one lesson you learned this week.
Answer: Any honest lesson.
