Nouns, pronouns and adjectives

English · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Circle the proper noun: dog, Dubai, city
Answer: Dubai
2.Circle the common noun: Monday, teacher, Sara
Answer: teacher
3.Write the proper noun for: a country you know.
Answer: Any country name with a capital letter.
4.Replace the noun with a pronoun: Omar is tall. ___ is tall.
Answer: He
5.Replace the noun with a pronoun: The girls ran home. ___ ran home.
Answer: They
6.Underline the adjective: The sandy beach was warm.
Answer: sandy (and warm)
7.Add an adjective: I saw a ______ camel.
Answer: Any sensible adjective, for example "tall".
8.Write the sentence with capitals: sara and maryam live in abu dhabi.
Answer: Sara and Maryam live in Abu Dhabi.
9.Which word is a pronoun? Table, she, quickly
Answer: she
10.Write a sentence using the adjective "shiny".
Answer: Any correct sentence containing "shiny".
