Light and shadows

Science · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name one natural and one made source of light.
Answer: Natural: the sun. Made: a torch or a lamp.
2.Is the moon a source of light?
Answer: No. It reflects light from the sun.
3.Which is transparent: glass, wood or tissue paper?
Answer: Glass.
4.A plastic sheet lets some light through but you cannot see clearly. What word describes it?
Answer: Translucent.
5.What is needed to make a shadow?
Answer: A light source, an opaque object and a surface.
6.When is a shadow shortest in the day?
Answer: Around midday.
7.Why are curtains made of opaque cloth in a bedroom?
Answer: To block light so the room is dark.
8.A torch is moved closer to a toy. What happens to the shadow?
Answer: It gets bigger.
9.Draw the shadow of a tree when the sun is low.
Answer: A long shadow on the side away from the sun.
10.Write one safety rule when using light.
Answer: For example: never look straight at the sun.
