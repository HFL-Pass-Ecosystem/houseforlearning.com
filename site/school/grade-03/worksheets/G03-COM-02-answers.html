Typing and word processing basics

Computing and Digital Skills · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Which row has the keys A S D F?
Answer: The home row.
2.What does the space bar do?
Answer: Makes a space between words.
3.How do you make a capital letter?
Answer: Hold the shift key and press the letter.
4.What does backspace do?
Answer: Deletes the letter behind the cursor.
5.Which button makes text darker and thicker?
Answer: Bold.
6.Type your name and the date.
Answer: Observe: capital letters and a saved document.
7.Why should we sit up straight when we type?
Answer: To protect our back and wrists and type better.
8.What is a font?
Answer: The style of the letters.
9.Where do you find the print button?
Answer: In the File menu or on the toolbar.
10.Type three sentences about your school and save.
Answer: Marked with the typing checklist.
