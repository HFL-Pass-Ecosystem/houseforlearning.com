Caring for the Earth

Moral and Value Education · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.What do the three Rs stand for?
Answer: Reduce, reuse, recycle.
2.Give one example of reusing.
Answer: For example refill a water bottle.
3.Give one example of recycling.
Answer: For example put paper in a recycling bin.
4.Name two ways to save electricity.
Answer: For example turn off lights; switch off devices.
5.Why should we not litter?
Answer: It harms animals and makes places dirty.
6.Write a pledge to take care of Earth.
Answer: Any sincere pledge.
7.How do trees help us?
Answer: They give shade, clean air, food and homes for animals.
8.Draw a recycling bin and write what goes in it.
Answer: Any relevant drawing with a list.
9.What can you do to reduce plastic bags?
Answer: Use cloth bags.
10.List three things your class can do to help the environment.
Answer: Any three.
