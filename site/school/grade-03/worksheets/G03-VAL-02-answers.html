Kindness in action

Moral and Value Education · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name two kind acts you can do at school.
Answer: For example share a pencil; help a new student.
2.How can you help at home?
Answer: For example set the table; tidy toys.
3.Why do we say thank you?
Answer: To show we appreciate someone.
4.Write a thank-you note to someone.
Answer: Any note with a greeting, thanks and sign-off.
5.Finish: Kindness feels like ...
Answer: Any thoughtful answer.
6.What can you give if you have no money?
Answer: Time, a smile, help, kind words.
7.Draw a kind act.
Answer: Any relevant picture.
8.Why should we be gentle with animals?
Answer: They feel pain and need care.
