Maps and directions

Social Studies · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.What is a map?
Answer: A drawing of a place as seen from above.
2.What does a map key show?
Answer: What the symbols on the map mean.
3.Name the four main directions.
Answer: North, south, east and west.
4.If you face north and turn right, which direction do you face?
Answer: East.
5.If you walk south, which direction is behind you?
Answer: North.
6.Draw a symbol for a hospital and a school.
Answer: Any clear symbols with a key.
7.Write directions from your classroom to the school office.
Answer: Marked with the directions checklist: start point, left/right and landmarks, end point.
8.Which tool tells us where north is?
Answer: A compass.
9.Why do maps use symbols?
Answer: To show lots of information in small space.
10.Draw a simple map of your street.
Answer: Marked with the map rubric: title, key, compass.
