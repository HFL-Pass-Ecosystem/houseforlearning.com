Subject and predicate

English · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Underline the subject: The red kite flew high.
Answer: The red kite
2.Underline the subject: My little brother sleeps late.
Answer: My little brother
3.Underline the predicate: The children sang a song.
Answer: sang a song
4.Underline the predicate: Grandfather planted a palm tree.
Answer: planted a palm tree
5.Is this a sentence? "Runs fast the horse." Write Yes or No and fix it.
Answer: No. The horse runs fast.
6.Join each subject to a predicate to make two full sentences. Subjects: The baker; Birds. Predicates: fly south; bakes bread.
Answer: The baker bakes bread. Birds fly south.
7.Write the sentence correctly: the camel drank water
Answer: The camel drank water.
8.Add a subject: ______ jumped over the fence.
Answer: Any sensible subject, for example "The goat".
9.Add a predicate: The tall teacher ______ .
Answer: Any sensible predicate, for example "walked into the room".
10.Write one sentence of your own and draw a line between the subject and the predicate.
Answer: Any correct sentence with the line in the right place.
