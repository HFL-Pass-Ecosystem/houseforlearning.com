Food and digestion

Science · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name two foods that give energy.
Answer: Any two of: rice, bread, pasta, potatoes.
2.Name one body-building food.
Answer: Any of: fish, eggs, beans, meat, milk.
3.Why do we need water?
Answer: To stay healthy, move food through the body and keep us cool.
4.Where does digestion start?
Answer: In the mouth.
5.Put in order: stomach, mouth, small intestine, food pipe.
Answer: Mouth, food pipe, stomach, small intestine.
6.Which teeth are used for grinding?
Answer: Molars.
7.Write two ways to care for your teeth.
Answer: For example: brush twice a day; eat less sugar; visit the dentist.
8.Write a healthy breakfast.
Answer: Any balanced breakfast with at least two food groups.
9.What does fibre help with?
Answer: Digestion (keeping food moving).
10.Is a chocolate bar a healthy daily snack? Give a reason.
Answer: No; it has a lot of sugar and fat. Accept a reasoned answer.
