Salma's Kite

Salma lived near a quiet beach. Every Friday her grandfather helped her fly a red kite.

One windy morning the string slipped from her hand and the kite floated out over the water. Salma wanted to cry, but she took a deep breath. "Let us follow it," said Grandfather.

They walked along the shore until they saw the kite caught on a low palm branch. Salma climbed onto her grandfather's shoulders and gently freed it. "Next time," she smiled, "I will wrap the string around my wrist."

1.Where did Salma live?
Answer: Near a quiet beach.
2.Who helped Salma fly the kite?
Answer: Her grandfather.
3.What colour was the kite?
Answer: Red.
4.Why did the kite float away?
Answer: The string slipped from Salma's hand.
5.How did Salma feel when she lost the kite?
Answer: Upset; she wanted to cry (accept sad or worried).
6.Where did they find the kite?
Answer: Caught on a low palm branch.
7.Write the main idea of the story in one sentence.
Answer: A girl and her grandfather find a lost kite and she learns to hold the string more safely. (Accept any sentence with the same meaning.)
8.What will Salma do next time?
Answer: Wrap the string around her wrist.
9.Put these events in the order they happened: (a) Salma frees the kite. (b) The string slips away. (c) They follow the kite along the shore.
Answer: b, c, a (the string slips away; they follow the kite along the shore; Salma frees the kite).
10.Find two words in the story that name a place.
Answer: Beach, water, shore, palm branch (any two).
