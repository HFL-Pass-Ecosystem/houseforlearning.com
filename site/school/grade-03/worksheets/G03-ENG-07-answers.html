Dictionary and spelling skills

English · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Put in alphabetical order: pear, apple, mango, banana.
Answer: apple, banana, mango, pear
2.Put in alphabetical order: cat, car, cap.
Answer: cap, car, cat
3.Which word would you find between "dog" and "door"? dig, dot, dust
Answer: dot
4.What are guide words for?
Answer: They show the first and last word on a dictionary page so you can find your word quickly.
5.Split into syllables: banana
Answer: ba-na-na
6.Add -ing: jump
Answer: jumping
7.Add -ed: walk
Answer: walked
8.Fix the spelling: freind
Answer: friend
9.Fix the spelling: becuase
Answer: because
10.Use "enormous" in a sentence.
Answer: Any correct sentence, for example: The elephant was enormous.
