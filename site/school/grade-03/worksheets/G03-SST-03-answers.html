Communities and helpers

Social Studies · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name three community helpers.
Answer: Any three, for example doctor, police officer, teacher.
2.Who helps us when there is a fire?
Answer: Firefighters.
3.Who looks after sick people?
Answer: Doctors and nurses.
4.Why do we need rules in a community?
Answer: So that people are safe and treated fairly.
5.Name two public places we all share.
Answer: For example park, library.
6.Write a thank-you sentence to a helper.
Answer: Any polite sentence naming the helper and the thanks.
7.How can you help keep your neighbourhood clean?
Answer: For example put rubbish in bins; pick up litter.
8.Why do people cooperate?
Answer: Working together gets more done and is fairer.
9.What number do you call in an emergency in the UAE?
Answer: 999 (accept 999 for police; 998 ambulance; 997 fire).
10.Draw your favourite community helper at work.
Answer: Any relevant drawing with a label.
