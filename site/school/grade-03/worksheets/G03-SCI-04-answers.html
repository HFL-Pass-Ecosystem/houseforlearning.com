Rocks, soil and water

Science · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name two properties of rocks.
Answer: Any two, for example hard, rough.
2.Which soil holds the most water: sand or clay?
Answer: Clay.
3.Why do plants grow better in soil with humus?
Answer: Humus has nutrients that help plants grow.
4.Name two sources of water.
Answer: Any two of: rain, rivers, wells, the sea (after desalination).
5.What does desalination do?
Answer: Removes salt from sea water so we can use it.
6.Write three ways to save water at home.
Answer: For example: close the tap; take short showers; fix leaks.
7.What happens to sand in a jar of water when you shake it and wait?
Answer: It settles at the bottom first (heavier particles settle first).
8.Why are rocks used to build houses?
Answer: They are strong and hard.
9.Is water a renewable or a limited resource? Explain in one sentence.
Answer: It cycles but fresh water is limited, so we must conserve it.
10.Draw soil layers and label them.
Answer: Marked with the labelling rubric.
