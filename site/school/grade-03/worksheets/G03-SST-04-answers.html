Heritage and traditions

Social Studies · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name two traditional jobs in the Gulf.
Answer: Any two, for example pearl diver, fisher.
2.What is a dhow?
Answer: A traditional wooden boat.
3.What is a majlis?
Answer: A place where people gather to talk and welcome guests.
4.What fruit comes from the date palm?
Answer: Dates.
5.Name one tradition that people still keep.
Answer: For example hospitality, falconry.
6.Why is heritage important?
Answer: It teaches us about our past and who we are.
7.How are souqs different from malls?
Answer: Souqs are traditional markets with small shops; malls are large modern buildings. Accept a fair comparison.
8.Draw a dhow on the sea.
Answer: Any dhow drawing with sea.
9.Name one thing people used before electricity to keep cool.
Answer: For example wind towers or shade.
10.Write two sentences about your family's traditions.
Answer: Any two relevant sentences.
