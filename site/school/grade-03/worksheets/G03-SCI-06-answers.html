Forces and motion

Science · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Is kicking a ball a push or a pull?
Answer: A push.
2.Is opening a drawer by the handle a push or a pull?
Answer: A pull.
3.Which surface gives more friction: ice or sand paper?
Answer: Sand paper.
4.What force makes a dropped ball fall?
Answer: Gravity.
5.Name one material that a magnet attracts.
Answer: Iron or steel (for example a paper clip).
6.When two north poles are brought together, do they attract or repel?
Answer: They repel.
7.Why do we put grip on sports shoes?
Answer: To increase friction so we do not slip.
8.Which is faster down a slope: a car on carpet or on a smooth table?
Answer: On the smooth table because there is less friction.
9.Name two things a force can do to a moving object.
Answer: Any two of: speed it up, slow it down, stop it, change its direction.
10.Write a prediction for a ball rolling on sand and on tile.
Answer: The ball rolls farther on tile because there is less friction.
