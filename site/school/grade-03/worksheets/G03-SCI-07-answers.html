Weather and the sky

Science · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.What causes day and night?
Answer: The Earth spins on its axis.
2.Does the sun move around the Earth or does the Earth spin?
Answer: The Earth spins (it only looks as if the sun moves).
3.Name two kinds of weather.
Answer: Any two, for example sunny and windy.
4.What do we use to measure temperature?
Answer: A thermometer.
5.What clothes do we wear in hot weather?
Answer: Light, loose clothes and a hat.
6.Is the moon a star?
Answer: No. It is a natural satellite of the Earth.
7.Why do we see different shapes of the moon?
Answer: Because we see different parts of the lit side as it moves around the Earth.
8.Which season is the hottest in the UAE?
Answer: Summer.
9.Record: if it is 38 °C, is it hot or cool?
Answer: Hot.
10.Draw the weather you saw today and write a caption.
Answer: Any correct drawing and a full-sentence caption.
