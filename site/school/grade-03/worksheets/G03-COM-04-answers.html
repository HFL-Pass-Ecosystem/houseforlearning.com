Online safety

Computing and Digital Skills · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name two things you should not share online.
Answer: Any two of: home address, phone number, password, school name.
2.Who do you tell if something online makes you feel unsafe?
Answer: A trusted adult such as a parent or teacher.
3.Which password is stronger: sara or S@ra-Loves-Pears8?
Answer: The second one.
4.Should you share your password with friends? Why?
Answer: No, it is private and could let someone use your account.
5.Rewrite kindly: "Your drawing is rubbish."
Answer: Any kind version, for example "I like your colours; try adding more detail."
6.What should you do if a stranger messages you?
Answer: Do not reply; tell a trusted adult.
7.How much screen time is healthy each day? Tell your family plan.
Answer: Marked on balance: follows family and school guidance; includes breaks.
8.Write one online safety rule for your class.
Answer: Any sensible rule.
9.Draw a stop sign and write when you would use it online.
Answer: Any relevant picture and sentence.
10.Finish: I will be kind online by ...
Answer: Any kind action.
