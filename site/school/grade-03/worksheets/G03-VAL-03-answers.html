Teamwork

Moral and Value Education · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Name two roles in a team.
Answer: For example leader, recorder, timekeeper.
2.Why do teams need fair jobs?
Answer: So everyone can contribute and no one feels left out.
3.What does compromise mean?
Answer: Each person gives a little to find a solution.
4.Write one rule for listening.
Answer: For example look at the speaker.
5.Draw your team's tower.
Answer: Any relevant drawing.
6.What should you do if a teammate is upset?
Answer: Listen kindly and help find a solution.
7.Why is it good to praise teammates?
Answer: It builds confidence and a good team spirit.
8.Write one thing you did well in the team task.
Answer: Any honest strength.
