Paragraph writing

English · Grade 3 · worksheet

Name: ____________________Class: ________Date: ____________Marks: ______ / 10
Read each question carefully. Write your answers on the lines. Check your work at the end.
1.Which sentence is the topic sentence? (a) Camels live in the desert. (b) They have humps. (c) They can go many days without water.
2.Cross out the sentence that does not belong: "I love my school. My teacher is kind. Bananas are yellow. I have many friends."
3.Write a topic sentence about your favourite food.
4.Add two supporting sentences to: "Our school has a big library."
5.Use "because" to join: I wear a hat. The sun is hot.
6.Write a closing sentence for a paragraph about the beach.
7.How many sentences are in a good short paragraph? Choose: 1, 5 to 6, 20
8.Fix the sentence: my friend and i play football on friday
9.Write a four-sentence paragraph about your pet or a pet you would like.
10.Give a title to your paragraph.
