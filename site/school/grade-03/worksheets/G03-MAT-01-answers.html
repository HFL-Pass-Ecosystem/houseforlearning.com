Numbers to 1000

Mathematics · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Write 345 in words.
Answer: Three hundred forty-five (accept three hundred and forty-five).
2.Write "six hundred and nine" in digits.
Answer: 609
3.Write in order from smallest to largest: 528, 806, 750
Answer: 528, 750, 806
4.Write in order from largest to smallest: 590, 963, 321, 911
Answer: 963, 911, 590, 321
5.Write in order from smallest to largest: 927, 624, 287, 617
Answer: 287, 617, 624, 927
6.Put > , < or = in the box: 243 [ ] 244
Answer: <
7.Put > , < or = in the box: 288 [ ] 287
Answer: >
8.Put > , < or = in the box: 911 [ ] 901
Answer: >
9.What number comes just before 899?
Answer: 898
10.What number comes just after 950?
Answer: 951
