Plant life

Science · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.Which part of a plant takes in water from the soil?
Answer: The roots.
2.What do leaves make for the plant?
Answer: Food (using sunlight, air and water).
3.Name three things a plant needs to grow.
Answer: Any three of: light, water, air, space, warmth.
4.Put the life cycle in order: seedling, seed, flower, adult plant.
Answer: Seed, seedling, adult plant, flower.
5.Name two ways seeds can travel.
Answer: Any two of: wind, water, animals, bursting pods.
6.Why does the stem matter?
Answer: It holds the plant up and carries water to the leaves.
7.What is a fair test?
Answer: A test where only one thing changes and the others stay the same.
8.A plant is kept in a dark cupboard. What will probably happen? Why?
Answer: It grows weak and pale because it has no light to make food.
9.Which part of a plant makes seeds?
Answer: The flower (and fruit holds them).
10.Draw and label a plant with four parts.
Answer: Marked with the labelling rubric: roots, stem, leaves, flower correctly placed and labelled.
