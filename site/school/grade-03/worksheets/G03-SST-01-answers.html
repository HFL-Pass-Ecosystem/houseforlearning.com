The United Arab Emirates

Social Studies · Grade 3 · TEACHER ANSWER KEY

Answers are shown in green. Accept any correct answer in the learner's own words. Marking: 1 mark for each question unless stated.
1.How many emirates make up the UAE?
Answer: Seven.
2.Name the capital of the UAE.
Answer: Abu Dhabi.
3.Name three emirates.
Answer: Any three of the seven.
4.When did the seven emirates form one country?
Answer: 2 December 1971.
5.Name the four colours of the UAE flag.
Answer: Red, green, white and black.
6.What is the national bird of the UAE?
Answer: The falcon.
7.What is the currency of the UAE?
Answer: The dirham.
8.In which emirate is the Burj Khalifa?
Answer: Dubai.
9.Name one national symbol besides the flag.
Answer: Falcon, Arabian oryx or ghaf tree.
10.Why is unity important for a country?
Answer: People can work together, feel safe and share what they have.
