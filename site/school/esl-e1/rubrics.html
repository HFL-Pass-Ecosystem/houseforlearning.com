ESL level 1 (Pre-A1 to A1): skill rubrics

Our own format AFMT-SCH-06. Four levels for each strand; evidence to look for comes from the unit outcomes of this grade.

English as a Second Language bridge

StrandEmergingDevelopingSecureMastering
Listening
Look for: follows instructions and short talks		Needs a lot of help to understand spoken English at this level. Many gaps; builds on guided practice.Can understand spoken English at this level with some help. Look for: capital letters, full stops, linked ideas		Needs a lot of help to write sentences and short texts. Many gaps; builds on guided practice.Can write sentences and short texts with some help. Some errors; improving with practice.Can write sentences and short texts accurately and on their own.Can write sentences and short texts accurately, in new situations, and explains the choices made.

Evidence this year: greet people, give my name and age and spell my name; say numbers to 20 and name colours and shapes; name family members and rooms in a house; name school objects and follow classroom instructions; say what I like and ask for food politely; name animals and body parts and say what they can do; describe what I wear and the weather; match letters to sounds and read simple words; and more
