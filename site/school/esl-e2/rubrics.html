ESL level 2 (A1 to A2): skill rubrics

Our own format AFMT-SCH-06. Four levels for each strand; evidence to look for comes from the unit outcomes of this grade.

English as a Second Language bridge

Evidence this year: talk about my day using the present simple; ask for and give simple directions; say what I like and can do; ask the price and buy something; tell a simple story about yesterday using the past simple; use adjectives and comparatives; say how I feel and what is wrong; find main ideas and details in a short text; and more
