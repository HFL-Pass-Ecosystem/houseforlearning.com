Grade 7: skill rubrics

Our own format AFMT-SCH-06. Four levels for each strand; evidence to look for comes from the unit outcomes of this grade.

English

StrandEmergingDevelopingSecureMastering
Reading and understanding
Look for: answers with evidence from the text; finds main idea and meaning of new words		Needs a lot of help to read a text and answer questions about it. Many gaps; builds on guided practice.Can read a text and answer questions about it with some help. Some errors; improving with practice.Can read a text and answer questions about it accurately and on their own.Can read a text and answer questions about it accurately, in new situations, and explains the choices made.

Some errors; improving with practice.Can use correct grammar and a growing range of words accurately and on their own.Can use correct grammar and a growing range of words accurately, in new situations, and explains the choices made.
Speaking and listening
Look for: clear voice, takes turns, asks and answers questions		Needs a lot of help to speak clearly and listen to others. Many gaps; builds on guided practice.Can speak clearly and listen to others with some help. Some errors; improving with practice.Can speak clearly and listen to others accurately and on their own.Can speak clearly and listen to others accurately, in new situations, and explains the choices made.

Evidence this year: describe how setting and character shape a story; use continuous tenses, active and passive and reported speech; write an article with a clear opening and purpose; analyse rhythm, rhyme and imagery in a poem; write and format emails and formal letters; infer, summarise and use evidence; take part in a discussion and speak without notes for one minute
