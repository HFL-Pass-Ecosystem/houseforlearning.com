Data protection

Draft for lawyer review. We say aligned with, never certified or compliant, until a lawyer has confirmed each part.

Different countries protect personal data in different ways. Choose your country with the Data law box next to the Language button, or below. The page then shows the law we align with for you, the rights you have and the consent wording you will see.

How long we keep data

Kind of dataRetention
Enquiries and messagesWhile we answer and for the business that follows, then up to 24 months, then deleted.
Consent recordsFor as long as the consent is relied on, plus the period the law requires to show it.
Data-rights requests and our repliesUp to 3 years, to show we answered.
Accounts and learning recordsWhile the account is open, then up to 24 months unless the law needs longer.
Job applicationsUp to 12 months, or longer only with your agreement.
Cookie choicesUp to 12 months, then we ask again.

Cookies and consent record

Cookies other than the ones needed to run the site are used only if you agree. Saying no is as easy as saying yes. Your choice is recorded with its date and the wording shown.
