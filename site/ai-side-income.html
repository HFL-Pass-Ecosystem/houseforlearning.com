learn and earn

AI side-income path

Twelve weeks to build and sell AI solutions as a side income. Learn it, practise it, and earn from it, in the way you learn best.

Learning material, not financial, legal or tax advice, and no income is promised. Results depend on your work. Use practice data only. Check the rules where you live on contracts, tax and personal data before you sell anything.

Try lesson 1 freeHow to join

the plan

Twelve weeks in three parts

  • Build, 5 weeks. Ten lessons, two live sessions a week of about three hours.
  • Sell, 4 weeks. Four lessons, two live sessions a week of about three hours.
  • Hackathon, 1 week. One capstone lesson: build, pitch and show.
  • Live Q and A. Five a week, optional: three by chat and two with an open microphone.

phase one

Build

  • lesson 1

    Build an AI-powered website or product

    A first working product in one sitting: describe what you want, let an AI assistant draft it, then test and fix it yourself.

    Open lesson 1
  • lesson 2

    Create a content channel with AI, without showing your face

    Many people earn from channels that never show a face. You will plan a niche, make one short piece, and set a weekly rhythm.

    Open lesson 2
  • lesson 3

    Build a simple CRM and business tool with an AI coding assistant

    Small businesses pay for tidy tools. Learn to turn their messy list into a simple app with records, status and reminders.

    Open lesson 3
  • lesson 4

    Build an AI brain for a company

    An enterprise brain lets staff ask questions in plain words and get answers from the firm's own policies and files, with sources shown.

    Open lesson 4
  • lesson 5

    Build a team of personal AI agents

    One assistant for research, one for drafting, one for checking. Small agents with clear jobs are easier to trust than one that does everything.

    Open lesson 5
  • lesson 6

    Build a real-time voice agent

    Voice agents answer calls and bookings. You will script the conversation, test it and decide when it hands over to a person.

    Open lesson 6
  • lesson 7

    Think about markets with AI: learn and test on paper only

    This lesson teaches how to think, not what to buy. You test ideas on past data with pretend money and learn why most ideas fail.

    Open lesson 7
  • lesson 8

    Think about investing with AI: learn and compare on paper only

    AI can summarise reports and explain terms. It cannot know your goals or the future. You learn to ask good questions and check answers.

    Open lesson 8
  • lesson 9

    Automate invoicing and billing

    Automation saves hours every month. You will connect an order, an invoice and a payment reminder, with a human check.

    Open lesson 9
  • lesson 10

    Make marketing ads with AI that people want to share

    An ad that works says one clear thing to one clear person. AI helps you make versions quickly. You test and learn.

    Open lesson 10

phase two

Sell

  • lesson 11

    Find high-paying clients

    Good clients have a clear problem, a budget and a person who can say yes. You will find them and open a polite conversation.

    Open lesson 11
  • lesson 12

    Write proposals and price your work

    A proposal shows you understand the problem, what you will do, by when and for how much. Clear scope prevents arguments later.

    Open lesson 12
  • lesson 13

    Pitch, negotiate and close

    Calls are a skill you can practise. Listen first, show the result, handle price calmly and agree one next step.

    Open lesson 13
  • lesson 14

    Build your online brand and portfolio

    Clients look you up before they reply. A clear profile and two or three real examples build trust.

    Open lesson 14

phase three

Hackathon

  • lesson 15

    Capstone: build, pitch and show one solution

    One week to bring it together: choose a real problem, build a first version, pitch it, and take feedback.

    Open lesson 15

learn your way

For every kind of learner

  • Visual. Every lesson starts with a picture of the steps.
  • Aural. Say it aloud, listen on the audio version, and join live sessions.
  • Read and write. Short plain steps and five-line write-ups.
  • Kinesthetic. Every lesson has a task you do with your hands, then tick off.
  • Authoring, training and learning and earning. You build, teach one step to a peer, and earn from real work.

certificate

Your way to a Microsoft certificate

  1. Apply for the practice exam. You get access to it; prepare meanwhile.
  2. Pass the practice exam. It is built like the real one, with several attempts.
  3. Sit the main Microsoft exam. Your team books it and covers the exam cost.
  4. Become a certified professional once you pass.

Subjects: data reporting, presentations and spreadsheets. Learning material is in your course portal.
