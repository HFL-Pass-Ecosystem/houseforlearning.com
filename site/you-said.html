Your opinion

You said, we did

We listen. What is right and possible, we do, quickly. What we cannot do yet, we do not forget: it goes into our queue, and we grow to meet it.

This page shows both lists. It never shows names or personal details.

Write to us

How we handle what you tell us

  1. You tell us. Use "Tell us what you think" at the foot of any page, or write to us. Every opinion is optional.
  2. We read every message. Opinions from visitors and from signed-in members are counted separately, so both voices are heard.
  3. We act on what is right and possible. Small fixes go out quickly, and we list them below.
  4. The rest joins our queue. We keep it in view and come back to it as we grow.
