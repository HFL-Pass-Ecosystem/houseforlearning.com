  • Understand Explain who needs Online Sales and E-Commerce Operations and name one real first user, with evidence from your own market scan.
  • Apply Build and run the practice task: Launch a 10-product test shop; ship five real orders; track conversion and return rate.
  • Analyse Record every step, cost and problem, then compare the plan with what really happened.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Launch a 10-product test shop; ship five real orders; track conversion and return rate.

Condition

About 150 hours over 3-6 months. Own tools, free spreadsheet and form tools, and a real practice partner. Mentor and peer circle available. Currency written on every number.

Standard

Evidence reviewed by a mentor; at least 70% on the 10-question check; two written peer reviews given; every number carries its currency and unit.

Who it is for, requirements and materials

  • Who it is for: learners aged 16 and over, small-business starters, workers moving to a new field, and teams (see B2B, B2C, D2C).
  • Requirements: none; start here.
  • Materials included: lesson text, worked examples, case exercise, 10-question check, templates in My page.
  • Teaching team: a trainer from our network, a mentor who reviews your evidence, and a peer circle. Names are given at enrolment.

lesson by lesson

9 modules, in the order you do them

Module 1 Find a real need IdeaStudy · 5 hUnderstand

Search three online shops in a niche you like. List 10 products, price (with currency), delivery time and return terms. Find a gap: a size, a bundle, a fast delivery, clearer information.

Talk to 5 possible customers (ask what stops them buying online). Ask what they do now, how often, what they pay, and what annoys them. Write a one-page idea: offer, user, price you hope for.

Evidence you keep: the market table and the interview notes.

Module 2 Kick-off circle IdeaShare and mentoring · 7 hUnderstand
  1. Read your one-page idea aloud (3 minutes).
  2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
  3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course.

Evidence you keep: revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

Module 3 Build the first version ActionPractice · 35 hApply
  1. Choose 10 products you can truly supply. Write for each: title, 5 facts, honest photos, price with currency, delivery time.
  2. Build the test shop on a free or low-cost shop tool: catalogue, cart, checkout through our own pay page for tests, order email.
  3. Test the whole path yourself and with 3 friends: find, add, pay, receive confirmation. Fix every stumble.

Evidence you keep: the working documents, records and test results from these steps.

Module 4 Short modules ActionStudy · 5 hApply
  • Online consumer-protection basics: clear price including tax and fees, delivery times, right to return, contact details.
  • Payments: bank transfer and UPI style methods with reference-number matching; never store card numbers; refund steps.
  • Shipping options: weight bands, zones, packing, tracking, damaged parcel claims.
Module 5 Run the pilot ActionPractice · 35 hApply

Ship five real orders. For each, record the order time, packing time, courier, delivery date, cost and any complaint. Handle one return end to end.

Keep a daily log: date, what you did, cost, result, problem, fix.

Evidence you keep: the 30-day log.

Module 6 Costing and feedback circle ValueShare and mentoring · 15 hEvaluate
  1. Build the cost sheet from your log: product cost, packaging, shipping, payment fees, returns, time. Put the currency on every line.
  2. Cost per unit = total cost divided by units. Margin = (price - cost) divided by price.
  3. Present to the circle; a mentor from the trade tells you which cost or risk you missed.

Evidence you keep: cost sheet reviewed by a mentor.

Module 7 Fix and measure ValuePractice · 35 hEvaluate
  1. Fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
  2. Run one more week and compare the key numbers before and after.
  3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.

Evidence you keep: before/after table and decision note.

Module 8 Review room EmpowermentShare and mentoring · 8 hCreate

Present idea, method, safety or compliance notes, cost sheet, feedback and decision to a review room. Give two peers written feedback (one strength, one fix). Get a written note from one real user or buyer.

Module 9 Check and next step RegenerationStudy · 5 hCreate
  1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark 70% (draft).
  2. Write a 90-day plan and pick a follow-on course.
  3. Contribute back a template, checklist or worked example. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).

practice material and assessment

Worked examples, case, check and local law pointers

Check questions (draft, 10)
  1. What must a product page show?
  2. Why include tax and fees in the price?
  3. What is conversion rate and how do you compute it? (5 orders from 200 visits)
  4. What is a return rate?
  5. Why never store card numbers?
  6. How do you match a bank payment to an order?
  7. Name two ways to cut delivery cost.
  8. What do you do with a damaged parcel?
  9. Which metric will you watch weekly?
  10. What is your 90-day plan?

Rules for this course

No brand names or logos of other companies on any page, pack, slide or file. No fake reviews. No hidden fees. No personal customer data in shared files.
