how groups work

Six kinds of space

  • Learn: studios and assembly rooms.
  • Collaborate: project, brainstorm and meeting spaces.
  • Focus: silent study, quiet rooms and the library.
  • Work: desks and offices.
  • Care: food, rest, lockers and play.
  • Welcome and support: reception, stores and washrooms.
