Think about markets with AI: learn and test on paper only

This lesson teaches how to think, not what to buy. You test ideas on past data with pretend money and learn why most ideas fail.

Learning material, not financial, legal or tax advice, and no income is promised. Results depend on your work. Use practice data only. Check the rules where you live on contracts, tax and personal data before you sell anything.

your aim

What you will be able to do

Explain how to test a trading idea honestly on past data, using paper money only.

  • Task. Write one idea as rules and test it on past data with costs.
  • Condition. Past price data, a testing tool, paper money only.
  • Standard. Rules written, costs included, trade count and biggest fall reported, and a note on why it might fail.

before the live session

Pre-read: get ready in 10 to 15 minutes

Read this one or two days before the live session.

  • Read. The aim above and the three steps below. Note one thing you do not understand.
  • Prepare. Open the tool you will use for "think about markets with ai: learn and test on paper only" and check that it works.
  • Bring. One question to ask in the live session.

step 1

Write the idea as rules

State when to enter, when to leave, and how much to risk. If you cannot write it as rules, you cannot test it.

step 2

Test fairly

Use data the idea has not seen. Include costs and slippage. Count the trades: very few trades prove nothing. Watch the biggest fall, not only the gain.

step 3

Know the limits

Past results do not promise future ones. This is not advice. Do not use real money while learning, and know that dealing for others needs a licence where you live.

learn your way

See it, hear it, do it, write it

  • See. Sketch the steps of "think about markets with ai: learn and test on paper only" as a simple picture or flow before you start.
  • Hear. Say each step aloud to a partner, or record yourself and play it back.
  • Do. Work with your hands on the practice task, then tick each step as you finish it.
  • Read and write. Write your result in five lines in your own words.

after the live session

Post-read: your notes and recording

After the live session the join button changes to "watch recording", and the notes are added here.

  • Watch. The recording, if you missed any part.
  • Read. The notes for "think about markets with ai: learn and test on paper only" and tick off each step.
  • Share. Put your result on your showcase for feedback.

Draft. Recordings and notes are not working yet in this test.

check yourself

Three quick questions

Think of your answer first, then open the question.

Can past results promise the future?

No.

Why include costs?

They can turn a gain into a loss.

Do you use real money to learn?

No, paper money only.
