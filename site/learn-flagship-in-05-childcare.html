flagship course

Childcare Products and Services

Choose, test and offer safe products and services for babies and young children, and run a small childcare service with care and records.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L4 Practitioner8 modules · 37 h lessons plus practice

Choose, test and offer safe products and services for babies and young children, and run a small childcare service with care and records.

  • Define the stages of child development from birth to six.
  • Apply safety checks to toys, furniture and spaces.
  • Analyse a product for age fit and risk.
  • Evaluate a service against safeguarding rules.
  • Create a daily plan and a safety file.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Review a childcare product or run a small day plan for young children, with safety checks and records.

Condition

About 8 weeks part-time. A play space or crche visit, product samples, a supervisor.

Standard

All checks are written, risks are removed or reported, and the daily plan fits the age group.

Who it is for: Parents, carers, early-years assistants, small business owners. Requirements: None. Materials: lesson text, templates, rubric, check with answers.

lesson by lesson

8 modules with worked examples

Module 1 How young children grow 4 hDefine
  • Physical, language, social and thinking milestones.
  • Every child grows at their own pace; note, do not label.
  • Sleep, food and play needs by age.
Worked example. A 2-year-old uses about 50 words and joins two words; this is a typical stage.

Activity. Make a milestone chart for ages 0-6.

Standard to meet. Chart is accurate and says children differ.

Module 2 Safety at home and in care 5 hApply
  • Choking, falls, burns, poisoning and drowning.
  • Safe sleep: firm surface, back to sleep, nothing loose in the cot.
  • Check electrical points, stairs, windows and cleaning stores.
Worked example. A home check finds a loose cord near a cot and a medicine on a low shelf; both are fixed and noted.

Activity. Do a safety walk-through and list fixes.

Standard to meet. Every hazard has a fix and owner.

Module 3 Products: choose and test 5 hAnalyse
  • Age labels, small parts, sharp edges, paint and fabric safety.
  • Local standards and marks to look for.
  • Test: pull, drop, wash, and smell.
Worked example. A wooden toy with a small removable wheel fails the small-parts test for under threes.

Activity. Test three products and write pass or fail.

Standard to meet. Tests are recorded with reasons.

Module 4 Play and learning 5 hCreate
  • Play types: sensory, pretend, building, movement, stories.
  • Plan short activities of 10-15 minutes.
  • Use everyday items and local stories.
Worked example. A 15-minute water play with cups and a story about rain.

Activity. Plan five activities for one age group.

Standard to meet. Each has aim, materials and safety note.

Module 5 Health and food 4 hApply
  • Handwashing, nappy changing, illness rules.
  • Food allergy checks and safe feeding.
  • Record medicine only with written consent.
Worked example. A child with a nut allergy gets a plate checked by two adults and a note on the board.

Activity. Write an illness and allergy policy.

Standard to meet. Policy has steps and who to call.

Module 6 Safeguarding and families 5 hEvaluate
  • Signs of harm and what to report.
  • Who is allowed to collect a child.
  • Respect each family culture and language.
Worked example. A child is collected by an unfamiliar adult; staff call the parent before release.

Activity. Write a collection and reporting procedure.

Standard to meet. Procedure names who to tell and how.

Module 7 Running a small service 5 hCreate
  • Group sizes and adult ratios by age.
  • Daily plan, attendance and incident logs.
  • Costs and a simple price list in rupees.
Worked example. Ratios of 1 adult to 4 toddlers; logs kept daily; monthly costs listed.

Activity. Make a one-page service plan.

Standard to meet. Plan shows ratios, daily plan and logs.

Module 8 Capstone: day plan and safety file 4 hCreate
  • Choose an age group.
  • Prepare the daily plan, safety file and parent note.
  • Review with a supervisor.
  • Present.
Worked example. A full-day plan for 3-year-olds with meals, naps, outdoor time and the safety file.

Activity. Build and present.

Standard to meet. Plan fits age, file is complete.

assessment

How you are checked

Evidence: the work product from each module, kept in one folder with dates. A mentor and two peers mark it with this rubric. Pass: no criterion at 'Not yet'.

Ten-question check, with model answers

Q1. Why avoid labelling a child?

Model answer: Children grow at different paces.

Q2. Name three safe-sleep rules.

Model answer: Firm surface, back to sleep, nothing loose in the cot.

Q3. What is the small-parts test for?

Model answer: To avoid choking risks for young children.

Q4. How long is an activity for toddlers?

Model answer: About ten to fifteen minutes.

Q5. Who may give medicine?

Model answer: Only as the written consent allows.

Q6. What do you do on a concern?

Model answer: Record it and report to the named lead.

Q7. Why keep an incident log?

Model answer: To track events and learn from them.

Q8. What belongs in the safety file?

Model answer: Checks, incident log, consent forms, contacts.

Q9. Why check age labels?

Model answer: They show risks for younger children.

Q10. What is a ratio?

Model answer: Number of adults to children.

seven levels

Your ladder, L1 to L7

This course sits at L4 Practitioner. Each level adds to the one below.

  1. L1 Basic. Know the basic needs of a child.
  2. L2 Awareness. Describe stages and hazards.
  3. L3 Foundation. Help with care and play under supervision.
  4. L4 Practitioner. Plan and run a day alone.
  5. L5 Specialist. Lead a room and train assistants.
  6. L6 Advanced. Manage a centre and audits.
  7. L7 Super expert. Set policy and standards for a chain of centres.

ten locations and global

Your place

Local rules change licences, data handling, pricing words and names of bodies. Check them for your place before you act.

India (Bengaluru)

Language: English, Hindi, Kannada. Currency: INR. Data rule for what you publish: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Skills frame to cite: National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) and the national credit framework.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Language: Dutch, English. Currency: EUR. Data rule for what you publish: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Skills frame to cite: the Dutch qualification framework (NLQF) linked to the European Qualifications Framework.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Language: Arabic, English. Currency: AED. Data rule for what you publish: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection). Skills frame to cite: the emirate education and training regulators (for example KHDA in Dubai) and the national qualifications framework.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Language: Portuguese (Spanish and English support). Currency: BRL. Data rule for what you publish: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados). Skills frame to cite: the national technical-education catalogue and the S-system training bodies (SENAI, SENAC).

Canada (Toronto)

Language: English, French. Currency: CAD. Data rule for what you publish: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply. Skills frame to cite: the provincial colleges framework and the national credential-recognition guidance.

United States

Language: English, Spanish. Currency: USD. Data rule for what you publish: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13. Skills frame to cite: state and community-college credit systems.

Singapore

Language: English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil. Currency: SGD. Data rule for what you publish: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Skills frame to cite: SkillsFuture Singapore frameworks and the national credit system.

Australia

Language: English. Currency: AUD. Data rule for what you publish: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Skills frame to cite: the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) levels.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Language: Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English. Currency: HKD. Data rule for what you publish: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Skills frame to cite: the Hong Kong Qualifications Framework.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)

Language: Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic, English. Currency: GEL or ILS. Data rule for what you publish: the personal data law of the country where the learner lives. Skills frame to cite: the national qualifications framework of that country.

Cayman Islands (Caribbean)

Language: English. Currency: KYD. Data rule for what you publish: the Data Protection Act. Skills frame to cite: the local education and training framework.

Mexico (Central America)

Language: Spanish, English. Currency: MXN. Data rule for what you publish: the federal law on personal data held by private parties. Skills frame to cite: the national competency certification system and the education ministry levels.

Russian Federation

Russian Federation: HELD. A sanctions and licensing check by our lawyer must finish before any course is offered there. No localised version is published.
