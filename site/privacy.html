Privacy

Privacy and Cookie Notice

Your information is safe with us. This page says what we keep, why, and how you stay in control.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Introduction

This notice explains how House for Learning handles information about the people who use this website.

2. Who is responsible

House for Learning is responsible for the information you give us through this website. To reach us, use the contact form.

3. What we collect and why

When you send a message we keep what you type: your email, mobile number with country code, your message and, if you choose to give them, your name, organisation, role and country. We use it to reply to you and to route your message to the right team. You will see a reference number straight away.

4. If you do not give it

Browsing the site needs nothing from you. To send a message we need your email, mobile number, message and your agreement to be contacted.

5. How long we keep it

We keep a message for as long as it takes to answer you and to keep a record of the conversation. You can ask us to remove it.

6. Your rights

  • Ask to see what we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct it
  • Ask us to erase it or limit how we use it
  • Ask for a copy you can take elsewhere
  • Object to a use, or withdraw your agreement at any time

Use the contact form and say which right you want to use. We reply with a reference number.

7. Sharing

We share nothing with outside partners for advertising. Messages are seen by the House for Learning team that owns your topic.

8. Cookies

We set no advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps only your own choices, such as the cookie choice you make on the notice, your language choice and your reading settings, on your own device. Use “Privacy choices” in the footer to change them at any time.

9. Links

If you choose a language, the page opens in an automatic translation by an outside service. The English page is the official version.
