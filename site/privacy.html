Privacy notice

What House for Learning collects through this website, and why. Short, plain and in your favour.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us in a form or email: your name, email, organisation, role, country and your message. We do not ask for identity documents, passport details, biometric data, health data or bank details through this website.

Why we use it

To answer your message, to suggest courses, and to keep a record of our conversation. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Learning records (members area, opening soon)

When you join, you choose what we may learn about you, and every choice starts switched off. If you choose to help us learn, we record how you use our services so we can improve them for you. We do not use sensitive personal data, we never sell your data, and you can switch this off at any time in your settings.

Organisations see learning insights only at team level, for groups large enough that no one can be singled out, and only for learners who switched sharing on.

Learners under 16, or the age set by local law, need a parent or guardian’s consent before they sign up.

Cookies and storage

This site sets no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. It stores one item in your browser, and nothing else. See the full cookies page.

Who sees it

Only the staff who handle your message, and service providers that host our website and email under contract. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

As long as needed to answer you and for any learning that follows, and then as the law requires. After that we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your data, or withdraw your consent, at any time. We follow applicable data protection laws, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU and UK GDPR, the UAE data protection law and Canada’s PIPEDA, and give the same protection to visitors from other countries.
