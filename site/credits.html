step by step, planned

How credits stack

Every unit you pass is a small, real step. We plan for those steps to add up, so that short learning can grow into a longer pathway without starting again.

Where a step is not open yet, we say so. We make no accreditation claim.

the six steps

From one unit to a degree pathway

  1. One unit

    A short burst of 5 to 90 minutes with outcomes for head, heart and hands. Pass the check and it goes on your record.

  2. Micro-credit

    A small group of related units in one subject family.

  3. Short course

    Several micro-credits that together cover one job task from start to finish.

  4. Certificate stage

    A longer stage that mixes online study, practice and observed work.

  5. Diploma pathway

    Planned: a route that combines several certificate stages and real project work.

  6. Degree pathway

    Planned: a route towards degree study, only with a body entitled to award degrees.

what counts

Evidence that counts towards credits

  • Quick checks passed at 80%, which you can review and retry
  • Practical tasks observed by a faculty member or a qualified supervisor
  • Evidence from real project work, where your role allows it
  • Learning you did before joining, once recognising it is in place (planned)

your record

Your evidence stays yours

Every passed unit and every piece of evidence goes on your own learning record. The record saves after every step, so nothing restarts after an update.

You can see and download your record at any time, and you choose whether your organisation sees anything at all.

the honest position

Diplomas and degrees

We do not award degrees. A degree can only be awarded by a body entitled to award it. Any diploma or degree pathway will be named on this site only once an agreement is in place, and not before.

Until then, the units you pass are a real record of what you have learned and can do, and they are kept for you.
