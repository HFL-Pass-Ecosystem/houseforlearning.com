Grade 04: English

8 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L2Primary8 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Remember, Understand, Apply, Create
  • Understand Summarise a passage and find evidence for an answer.
  • Create Build and spell new words and say what they mean.
  • Apply Use adverbs and prepositions to add detail.
  • Apply Use simple, continuous and perfect tenses and match verbs to subjects.
  • Create Plan and write a short essay and an informal letter.
  • Remember Read and respond to a poem, noticing imagery and sound.
  • Apply Use context to work out new words.
  • Apply Present, listen and respond with a reason.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 04 English, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 8 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

8 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Prose reading and main ideas Weeks 1 to 5Understand

In this unit you work on 'Prose reading and main ideas', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: summarise a passage and find evidence for an answer. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Prose reading and main ideas' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: summarise a passage and find evidence for an answer, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: Main idea in one sentence. Character: what they say and do shows what they are like (she gave away her lunch: generous). Drama: names on the left, stage directions in brackets. Method: Model run for 'Prose reading and main ideas'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Summarise a passage and find evidence for an answer: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Unit 2 Prefixes and suffixes Weeks 6 to 10Create

In this unit you work on 'Prefixes and suffixes', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: build and spell new words and say what they mean. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Prefixes and suffixes' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: build and spell new words and say what they mean, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: Prefix: dis + appear = disappear. Origins: telephone = Greek tele (far) + phone (sound); biology = bios (life) + logos (study). Method: Model run for 'Prefixes and suffixes'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, warm up: say or read the example aloud twice, slowly. Cover it and say it again from memory.
  2. Step 2, find the pattern: underline the word, ending or sentence part that 'Prefixes and suffixes' is about. Say in your own words why it is there.
  3. Step 3, change one thing: swap one word, name or number in the example and say the new version aloud.
  4. Step 4, write two new lines of your own that do the same job. Keep them short and true to your life.
  5. Step 5, swap with a partner: read each other's lines, circle one thing to fix, and fix it.
  6. Step 6, own it: read your final lines to the class or teacher. Standard: build and spell new words and say what they mean.

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Build and spell new words and say what they mean: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)

Use CAD in money problems; deliver in English, French; personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

United States

Use USD in money problems; deliver in English, Spanish; personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Singapore

Use SGD in money problems; deliver in English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil; personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Australia

Use AUD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Use HKD in money problems; deliver in Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English; personal data rule: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)

Use GEL or ILS in money problems; deliver in Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic, English; personal data rule: the personal data law of the country where the learner lives; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Cayman Islands (Caribbean)

Use KYD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Data Protection Act; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Mexico (Central America)

Use MXN in money problems; deliver in Spanish, English; personal data rule: the federal law on personal data held by private parties; guardian contact for every learner under 18.
