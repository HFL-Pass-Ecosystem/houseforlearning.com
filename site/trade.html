Trade

Trade that is simple, safe and fair

We help buyers and makers find each other, check the details together and trade with confidence, in any direction: local, export, import or re-export.

Start a conversationChoose your sectors

Trade, in plain words

Learn trade, quality and safety, from the first lesson. Short courses for buyers, makers and teams on quotes, papers, quality records, delivery terms and safe handling.

For buyers

  • One quote that covers sourcing, test, packing and delivery.
  • Written test report with every lot, so you can see what you are buying.
  • Backup suppliers behind every line, so one delay does not stop your plan.
  • Margin bands shown openly. No hidden fees.
  • Help with papers, codes and delivery terms in plain words.

For makers and suppliers

  • New markets reached through our partner network.
  • Clear orders, prompt payment and honest feedback.
  • A growth path from first pilot order to a framework agreement.
  • Your intellectual property is safe with us: we work within clear guardrails and agree what is shared before it is shared.

How to introduce yourself

  1. Tell us what you need or offer

    Share the part, the quantity, the destination and the date that matters. A short note is enough.

  2. We check together

    We confirm who is involved, where the goods will be used and which standards apply, so everyone can proceed with confidence.

  3. Start with a pilot

    A first order with a written test report. We would rather earn your next order than ask for a bigger first one.

  4. Grow with us

    Repeat orders, framework agreements and shared planning once both sides are comfortable.

Where we work

In every region of the world. We begin where we already have partners and open new routes with you. Ask us about your country and your product.

Around every trade

  • Sourcing and supplier checks
  • Test, calibration reminders and quality records
  • Packing and kitting to your standard
  • Delivery, papers and tracking
  • Training for your team on trade, quality and safety

How we work

We reply promptly, say what we will do and do it, and ask early when something is unclear. We follow the task, the condition and the standard agreed with you. We treat every buyer, maker and colleague with courtesy, and we expect the same of ourselves.

We work with approved partners and name them only with their permission. Service levels shown on this site are targets until the relevant partners have signed.

Tell us what you need. We will reply promptly and courteously.
