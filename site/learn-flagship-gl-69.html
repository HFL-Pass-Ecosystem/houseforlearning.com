Boat and Helicopter Tourism

Plan a small boat or helicopter tour offering with safe operations, licences, weather rules, guest care and pricing.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L4 Practitioner31:00 h of lessons plus practice and sharingSelf-paced with peer circle
  • Understand Explain operator duties and passenger safety.
  • Apply Run briefings and weather checks.
  • Analyse Compute cost per hour and per guest.
  • Evaluate Judge risk and environment.
  • Create Write an operations plan.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Plan a tour product with route, safety briefing, weather rules, licences, price and incident plan, working with licensed operators.

Condition

A computer, a map, a spreadsheet, two peers and a licensed operator mentor. Learners do not operate vessels or aircraft.

Standard

Licences and insurance are checked with sources; weather go/no-go rules are written; briefing covers equipment and emergencies; cost per guest is correct; peers rate Meets or better.

Who it is for: Tour planners, guides, hotel desks, port staff. Requirements: none. Materials: lesson text, worked examples, templates, rubric, quiz, tracker.

lesson by lesson

8 modules with worked examples

Module 1 Product and route 3 hUnderstand

Design a tour that guests will value and operators can run safely.

  • Types: scenic, sunset, wildlife, transfer.
  • Duration, capacity, ports and landing sites.
  • Accessibility and comfort.
  • Seasonality.
Worked example. A 45-minute coastal route with a sunset slot and a 20-minute boarding window.

Activity. Design a route with times.

Standard to meet. Map and timings.

Module 2 Licences and insurance 5 hApply

Only licensed operators and crews run commercial trips.

  • Vessel or aircraft certificates, crew licences and operator permits.
  • Insurance for passengers.
  • Check expiry dates.
  • Keep copies.
Worked example. A certificate expires next month; bookings after that date are stopped until renewal.

Activity. Make a checklist.

Standard to meet. Sources and dates.

Module 3 Weather and go/no-go 5 hApply

Safety over schedule.

  • Wind, visibility, waves and ceiling.
  • Clear numbers and a named decision maker.
  • Refund policy for cancellations.
  • Record decisions.
Worked example. Wind above a set limit cancels flights and guests are offered a new date or refund.

Activity. Write rules.

Standard to meet. Numbers specified.

Module 4 Briefing and equipment 4 hApply

Guests must know what to do.

  • Life jackets, seat belts, exits and behaviour.
  • Language options and check understanding.
  • Equipment inspection log.
  • Accessibility.
Worked example. Guests repeat how to fasten a life jacket before boarding.

Activity. Write a briefing script.

Standard to meet. Under five minutes.

Module 5 Emergencies 4 hEvaluate

Plan and drill.

  • Man overboard, fire, engine failure and medical.
  • Contacts and roles.
  • Practice drills.
  • Review after drills.
Worked example. A drill finds the radio channel was wrong and the sheet is corrected.

Activity. Write a plan.

Standard to meet. Steps ordered.

Module 6 Cost and pricing 4 hAnalyse

Know the economics.

  • Hourly cost of vessel or aircraft is high.
  • Cost per guest = hourly cost x hours / guests.
  • Break-even load factor.
  • Seasonal pricing.
Worked example. Hourly cost 40,000 INR for 8 guests is 5,000 INR each.

Activity. Build a sheet.

Standard to meet. Currency shown.

Module 7 Environment and community 3 hEvaluate

Be a good neighbour.

  • Noise, wildlife and emissions.
  • Flight paths and speed rules.
  • Local jobs.
  • Respect for residents.
Worked example. Routes avoid nesting sites in season.

Activity. List controls.

Standard to meet. Controls clear.

Module 8 Operations plan 3 hCreate

Write and present.

  • Roles, schedule and records.
  • Review each season.
  • Guest feedback.
  • Training.
Worked example. The plan reaches 4.7 average guest score.

Activity. Present.

Standard to meet. Complete.

assessment

How you are checked

Your evidence: route plan, briefing script, go/no-go rules, price sheet. Two peers and one mentor mark it with this rubric. Pass: no criterion at 'Not yet'.

Ten-question check, with model answers

Q1. Who may operate?

Model answer: Licensed operators and crew. (Covered in module 1: Product and route. To pass that module: Map and timings.)

Q2. Go/no-go factors?

Model answer: Wind, visibility, waves or ceiling. (Covered in module 2: Licences and insurance. To pass that module: Sources and dates.)

Q3. Briefing items?

Model answer: Equipment, exits, behaviour. (Covered in module 3: Weather and go/no-go. To pass that module: Numbers specified.)

Q4. Cost per guest?

Model answer: Hourly cost x hours / guests. (Covered in module 4: Briefing and equipment. To pass that module: Under five minutes.)

Q5. Why drills?

Model answer: Find weaknesses. (Covered in module 5: Emergencies. To pass that module: Steps ordered.)

Q6. Insurance for?

Model answer: Passengers. (Covered in module 6: Cost and pricing. To pass that module: Currency shown.)

Q7. Why check expiry?

Model answer: Valid permits. (Covered in module 7: Environment and community. To pass that module: Controls clear.)

Q8. Environment control?

Model answer: Avoid nesting sites. (Covered in module 8: Operations plan. To pass that module: Complete.)

Q9. Cancel policy?

Model answer: Refund. (Covered in module 8: Operations plan. To pass that module: Complete.)

Q10. What are checks logged?

Model answer: Equipment inspection. (Covered in module 8: Operations plan. To pass that module: Complete.)

ten locations and global

Your place

Maritime and aviation law differ by place.

India (Bengaluru)

Language: English, Hindi, Kannada. Write numbers in INR. Personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Skills frame to name in your evidence: National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) and the national credit framework. Consumer rule: Consumer Protection Act 2019.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Language: Dutch, English. Write numbers in EUR. Personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Skills frame to name in your evidence: the Dutch qualification framework (NLQF) linked to the European Qualifications Framework. Consumer rule: EU and Dutch consumer rules (14-day cooling-off for online sales).

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Language: Arabic, English. Write numbers in AED. Personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection). Skills frame to name in your evidence: the emirate education and training regulators (for example KHDA in Dubai) and the national qualifications framework. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Language: Portuguese (Spanish and English support). Write numbers in BRL. Personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados). Skills frame to name in your evidence: the national technical-education catalogue and the S-system training bodies (SENAI, SENAC). Consumer rule: the Consumer Defence Code (CDC).

Canada (Toronto)

Language: English, French. Write numbers in CAD. Personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply. Skills frame to name in your evidence: the provincial colleges framework and the national credential-recognition guidance. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

United States

Language: English, Spanish. Write numbers in USD. Personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13. Skills frame to name in your evidence: state and community-college credit systems. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

Singapore

Language: English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil. Write numbers in SGD. Personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Skills frame to name in your evidence: SkillsFuture Singapore frameworks and the national credit system. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

Australia

Language: English. Write numbers in AUD. Personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Skills frame to name in your evidence: the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) levels. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

Russian Federation

Russian Federation: HELD. A sanctions and licensing check by our lawyer must finish before any course is offered there. No localised version is published.

Global

The global version uses plain English, metric units, and your own currency. Machine translation is available in every language; the English text is the official text. Before you act for real, check your local rules with an adviser. Currency: USD (or your own currency, written next to every number).
