The business opportunity

Five areas that work together, for people and organisations of every size.

  • Learning and earning

    People learn, earn credentials and find ways to earn from what they know.

    • Specialist communities
    • Deep learning content
    • Mentoring and social learning
    • Skilling, re-skilling and cross-skilling

  • Marketplace

    A place where credentials, badges and accreditations are earned against clear competency frameworks.

    • Authors
    • Trainers
    • Learners
    • Earners

  • Workplace

    A digital workplace where members work together on small and medium projects and turn skills into income.

    • Enquiry to quote
    • Quote to order
    • Order to cash
    • Care through the whole lifecycle

  • Work products

    The things people make and share.

    • Short articles and guides
    • Learning modules and courses
    • Earning and entrepreneurship modules
    • Co-working and consulting opportunities

  • Who it serves

    Learners and organisations at every stage, in every market.

    • Schools and universities
    • Vocational and technical education
    • Small and medium enterprises
    • Corporates, institutions and government

Five pathways, seven categories

Everything here is organised the same way as the picker: Professional, Academic, Vocational, Entrepreneurial and recreational pathways, and the categories Hardware, Software, Cloud, Data, Analytics, Others and Services.
