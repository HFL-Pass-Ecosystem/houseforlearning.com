how to read it

One number, one requirement

Find the division, then the section. The last column takes you to our requirement or to our element code. Quote the section number in every offer, submittal and question.

Section numbers and titles follow CSI MasterFormat, 2018 edition. Where a contract names another edition, that edition rules; the lead consultant confirms each number against the licensed copy before issue.

four formats

How the CSI formats fit our stages

CSI formats and where we use them
FormatWhat it organisesWhere we use it
MasterFormatWork results, by division and sectionSpecifications, prices and submittals from LOD 300 onward. This page.
UniFormatBuilding elements, by functionEarly design and cost plans at LOD 100 and 200. Our code groups line up: CS-SH with B Shell, CS-IN with C Interiors, CS-SV with D Services, CS-FU with E Equipment and Furnishings.
SectionFormatThree parts inside each sectionEvery section we issue is written in three parts: Part 1 General, Part 2 Products, Part 3 Execution.
OmniClassTables that classify everything in a modelClassification fields on model elements, so quantities and asset lists come straight from the model.

Planning values for teaching and early design. A licensed local engineer and the local authority confirm every real project.

Learning material, not a design or safety instruction. Real buildings are designed and signed off by licensed professionals under local rules.
