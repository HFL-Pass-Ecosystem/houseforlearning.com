Our care rules

  1. Say why we ask, before we ask.
  2. Ask for the least we need. No name for polls.
  3. Make it optional. Say what happens if you skip.
  4. Show who can see the answer.
  5. Keep it only as long as the table says, then delete.
  6. Never share a list of learners with another company unless the learner said yes for that purpose.
  7. Never use answers to rank or shame a learner.
  8. Children and young people: a parent or guardian agrees first, where the law asks for it.
  9. Anyone may ask to see, correct or delete their answers. Support replies within 24 hours.
  10. Test data only until the owner says live.

Reflection prompts

  1. Name one thing you can now do that you could not do before this session.
  2. What was hard? What would help?
  3. Whom could you teach this to this week?
  4. What will you do next, and by which day?
