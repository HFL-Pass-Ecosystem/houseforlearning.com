school course

Grade 10: Arabic

4 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L4Secondary4 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Understand, Apply, Analyse
  • Analyse Analyse a short text.
  • Apply Write a formal letter.
  • Understand Summarise a spoken text.
  • Apply Present on a topic.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 10 Arabic, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 4 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

4 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Literature and poetry Weeks 1 to 9Analyse

In this unit, 'Literature and poetry', you learn to analyse a short text. You work with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. You see it, say it, then do it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Literature and poetry' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: analyse a short text, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: حكمة: العلم نور والجهل ظلام. الكلمتان المتضادتان: نور وظلام. (Knowledge is light, ignorance is darkness; opposites: light and darkness.) Method: Model run for 'Literature and poetry'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Read the short text I wrote. أَنَا النَّخْلَةُ، جُذُورِي فِي التُّرَابِ وَرَأْسِي فِي السَّمَاءِ. تَهُزُّنِي الرِّيحُ فَلَا أَسْقُطُ، وَأُعْطِي التَّمْرَ لِمَنْ يَمُرُّ بِي. [I am the palm tree, my roots in the soil and my head in the sky. The wind shakes me but I do not fall, and I give dates to whoever passes by.]
  2. Step 2, Identify the theme. The palm stands for a person who is steadfast and generous. Write: يَرْمُزُ النَّصُّ بِالنَّخْلَةِ إِلَى الْإِنْسَانِ الصَّبُورِ الْكَرِيمِ. [The text uses the palm to symbolise the patient, generous person.] Support it with a quotation.
  3. Step 3, Name the devices. تَشْخِيصٌ [personification]: the palm speaks as أَنَا. طِبَاقٌ [antithesis]: التُّرَابِ and السَّمَاءِ, the low and the high. اسْتِعَارَةٌ [metaphor]: the palm as the human being. Each device adds to the meaning.
  4. Step 4, Write an analysis paragraph. يُبْرِزُ الْكَاتِبُ ثَبَاتَ الْإِنْسَانِ مِنْ خِلَالِ عِبَارَةِ «جُذُورِي فِي التُّرَابِ وَرَأْسِي فِي السَّمَاءِ»، فَالطِّبَاقُ يُصَوِّرُ التَّوَاضُعَ مَعَ الطُّمُوحِ. [The writer highlights steadfastness through the phrase, roots in the soil and head in the sky; the contrast depicts humility with ambition.]
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student only paraphrases: النَّخْلَةُ لَهَا جُذُورٌ وَتُعْطِي التَّمْرَ. Correction: analysis explains how and why: name the device, quote the words, and say what effect they create, such as showing generosity and strength.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: analyse the line تَهُزُّنِي الرِّيحُ فَلَا أَسْقُطُ. Model answer: تَدُلُّ الْجُمْلَةُ عَلَى الْمُثَابَرَةِ؛ فَالرِّيحُ رَمْزٌ لِلصُّعُوبَاتِ، وَالنَّخْلَةُ لَا تَسْقُطُ كَالْإِنْسَانِ الصَّبُورِ. [The sentence shows perseverance; the wind symbolises difficulties, and the palm does not fall, like a patient person.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Analyse a short text: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 2 Formal letters Weeks 10 to 18Apply

In this unit, 'Formal letters', you learn to write a formal letter. You work with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. You see it, say it, then do it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Formal letters' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: write a formal letter, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: الرسالة: التاريخ، المرسل إليه، التحية (السلام عليكم ورحمة الله)، الموضوع، المتن، الخاتمة مع الشكر، التوقيع. Method: Model run for 'Formal letters'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, A formal letter has a date, the addressee, a formal greeting, a body, a closing and a signature. Address: حَضْرَةِ الْأُسْتَاذِ مُدِيرِ الْمَدْرَسَةِ الْمُحْتَرَمِ. [To the respected school principal.] Greeting: تَحِيَّةً طَيِّبَةً وَبَعْدُ، [With best greetings, and then,].
  2. Step 2, Write the opening of the body. أَتَقَدَّمُ إِلَيْكُمْ بِطَلَبٍ لِإِقَامَةِ مَعْرِضٍ لِلْكِتَابِ فِي الْمَدْرَسَةِ يَوْمَ الْخَمِيسِ الْقَادِمِ. [I submit to you a request to hold a book fair at school next Thursday.] State the request in the first sentence.
  3. Step 3, Give reasons and details. يَهْدِفُ الْمَعْرِضُ إِلَى تَشْجِيعِ الطُّلَّابِ عَلَى الْقِرَاءَةِ، وَسَيُشَارِكُ فِيهِ أَوْلِيَاءُ الْأُمُورِ. لِذَا أَرْجُو التَّكَرُّمَ بِالْمُوَافَقَةِ. [The fair aims to encourage students to read, and parents will take part. I therefore ask you kindly to approve.]
  4. Step 4, Close and sign. وَتَفَضَّلُوا بِقَبُولِ فَائِقِ الِاحْتِرَامِ وَالتَّقْدِيرِ. [Please accept my highest respect and appreciation.] مُقَدِّمُ الطَّلَبِ: خَالِدٌ، الصَّفُّ الْعَاشِرُ. [Applicant: Khalid, grade ten.] Keep the language polite and the sentences short.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student starts the letter with مَرْحَبًا يَا مُدِيرُ [Hello, principal], uses slang, and forgets the date. Correction: open with the formal address, تَحِيَّةً طَيِّبَةً وَبَعْدُ، write standard Arabic, and put the date at the top.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write the body of a letter asking permission to organise a charity day. Model answer: أَتَقَدَّمُ إِلَيْكُمْ بِطَلَبٍ لِتَنْظِيمِ يَوْمٍ خَيْرِيٍّ لِجَمْعِ التَّبَرُّعَاتِ. أَرْجُو التَّكَرُّمَ بِالْمُوَافَقَةِ. وَتَفَضَّلُوا بِقَبُولِ الِاحْتِرَامِ. [I request permission to organise a charity day to collect donations. I kindly ask for approval. Please accept my respect.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Write a formal letter: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 3 Listening and summary Weeks 19 to 27Understand

In this unit, 'Listening and summary', you learn to summarise a spoken text. You work with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. You see it, say it, then do it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Listening and summary' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: summarise a spoken text, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: أبدأ بالتحية، ثم أعرض ثلاث أفكار مع مثال، وأختم بالشكر. للتلخيص: أكتب الفكرة الرئيسية في جملتين. Method: Model run for 'Listening and summary'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Listen to this announcement, which a partner reads once. تُعْلِنُ إِدَارَةُ الْمَدْرَسَةِ عَنْ إِقَامَةِ يَوْمٍ لِلنَّظَافَةِ يَوْمَ الْأَحَدِ الْقَادِمِ، وَسَيُشَارِكُ فِيهِ ثَلَاثُمِائَةِ طَالِبٍ. [The school administration announces a cleaning day next Sunday, with three hundred students taking part.]
  2. Step 2, Hear the second half. سَيَبْدَأُ الْعَمَلُ فِي السَّاعَةِ التَّاسِعَةِ صَبَاحًا وَيَنْتَهِي عِنْدَ الظُّهْرِ. عَلَى كُلِّ طَالِبٍ إِحْضَارُ قُفَّازَاتٍ وَكِيسٍ. [Work will start at nine in the morning and finish at noon. Each student must bring gloves and a bag.]
  3. Step 3, Take notes while listening. Write key words only: يَوْمُ نَظَافَةٍ، الْأَحَدُ، 300 طَالِبٍ، 9 صَبَاحًا – ظُهْرًا، قُفَّازَاتٌ وَكِيسٌ. [cleaning day, Sunday, 300 students, 9 am to noon, gloves and bag.] Notes use words and numbers, not full sentences.
  4. Step 4, Write the summary from your notes. أَعْلَنَتِ الْمَدْرَسَةُ عَنْ يَوْمِ نَظَافَةٍ يَوْمَ الْأَحَدِ، يُشَارِكُ فِيهِ ثَلَاثُمِائَةِ طَالِبٍ مِنَ التَّاسِعَةِ حَتَّى الظُّهْرِ، وَعَلَى كُلِّ طَالِبٍ إِحْضَارُ قُفَّازَاتٍ وَكِيسٍ. [The school announced a cleaning day on Sunday with three hundred students from nine until noon; each must bring gloves and a bag.]
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A student tries to write every word and loses the numbers and times. Correction: note who, what, when, how many and what to bring; leave out repeated words and add linking words only when writing the summary.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: summarise a different announcement about a trip next Friday at eight, with fifty students, bringing lunch. Model answer: سَتَنْظِمُ الْمَدْرَسَةُ رِحْلَةً يَوْمَ الْجُمُعَةِ الْقَادِمِ فِي السَّاعَةِ الثَّامِنَةِ، وَيُشَارِكُ فِيهَا خَمْسُونَ طَالِبًا، وَعَلَى كُلِّ طَالِبٍ إِحْضَارُ غَدَاءٍ. [The school will organise a trip next Friday at eight, with fifty students; each must bring lunch.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Summarise a spoken text: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Unit 4 Oral presentation Weeks 28 to 36Apply

In this unit, 'Oral presentation', you learn to present on a topic. You work with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. You see it, say it, then do it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Oral presentation' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: present on a topic, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: أبدأ بالتحية، ثم أعرض ثلاث أفكار مع مثال، وأختم بالشكر. للتلخيص: أكتب الفكرة الرئيسية في جملتين. Method: Model run for 'Oral presentation'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Open with a hook and a thesis. هَلْ فَكَّرْتُمْ يَوْمًا كَمْ لِتْرًا مِنَ الْمَاءِ نُهْدِرُ كُلَّ يَوْمٍ؟ [Have you ever thought how many litres of water we waste each day?] مَوْضُوعِي الْيَوْمَ هُوَ أَهَمِّيَّةُ الْحِفَاظِ عَلَى الْمَاءِ. [My topic today is the importance of saving water.]
  2. Step 2, Present the structure aloud. سَأَتَحَدَّثُ عَنْ ثَلَاثَةِ أُمُورٍ: أَوَّلًا أَسْبَابُ الْهَدْرِ، ثَانِيًا آثَارُهُ، وَثَالِثًا الْحُلُولُ. [I will talk about three things: first the causes of waste, second its effects, and third solutions.] Signposting helps listeners follow.
  3. Step 3, Develop each point with an example. أَوَّلًا، يُهْدِرُ بَعْضُنَا الْمَاءَ حِينَ يَتْرُكُ الصُّنْبُورَ مَفْتُوحًا. [First, some of us waste water by leaving the tap open.] ثَانِيًا، يُؤَدِّي ذَلِكَ إِلَى نَقْصِ الْمَاءِ لِلْأَجْيَالِ الْقَادِمَةِ. [Second, this leads to a shortage for coming generations.]
  4. Step 4, Give solutions and close. ثَالِثًا، يُمْكِنُنَا جَمْعُ مِيَاهِ الْأَمْطَارِ وَإِصْلَاحُ التَّسْرِيبِ. وَفِي الْخِتَامِ أَدْعُوكُمْ لِلْبَدْءِ الْيَوْمَ. شُكْرًا لِحُسْنِ اسْتِمَاعِكُمْ. [Third, we can collect rainwater and fix leaks. In conclusion I call on you to begin today. Thank you for your attention.]
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A speaker reads from the slides without looking up and gives no example. Correction: use only key words on the slides, speak to the audience, pause after each point, and add one example. Respond to questions with سُؤَالٌ وَجِيهٌ [a good question] before answering.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write a thirty-second introduction and thesis on screen time. Model answer: هَلْ تَعْرِفُونَ كَمْ سَاعَةً نَقْضِيهَا أَمَامَ الشَّاشَةِ؟ مَوْضُوعِي الْيَوْمَ أَثَرُ الْإِفْرَاطِ فِي اسْتِعْمَالِ الشَّاشَاتِ عَلَى صِحَّتِنَا. [Do you know how many hours we spend in front of the screen? My topic today is the effect of excessive screen use on our health.]

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Present on a topic: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

[The school will organise a trip next Friday at eight, with fifty students; each must bring lunch.] (Covered in unit 3: Listening and summary.)

Q4. Unit 4 (Oral presentation): Write a thirty-second introduction and thesis on screen time.

Model answer: هَلْ تَعْرِفُونَ كَمْ سَاعَةً نَقْضِيهَا أَمَامَ الشَّاشَةِ؟ مَوْضُوعِي الْيَوْمَ أَثَرُ الْإِفْرَاطِ فِي اسْتِعْمَالِ الشَّاشَاتِ عَلَى صِحَّتِنَا. Q6. Unit 2 (Formal letters): Write the opening of the body. Show how you do it and say what the result is.

Model answer: أَتَقَدَّمُ إِلَيْكُمْ بِطَلَبٍ لِإِقَامَةِ مَعْرِضٍ لِلْكِتَابِ فِي الْمَدْرَسَةِ يَوْمَ الْخَمِيسِ الْقَادِمِ. [I submit to you a request to hold a book fair at school next Thursday.] State the request in the first sentence. (Covered in unit 2: Formal letters.)

Q7. Unit 3 (Listening and summary): Hear the second half. Show how you do it and say what the result is.

Model answer: سَيَبْدَأُ الْعَمَلُ فِي السَّاعَةِ التَّاسِعَةِ صَبَاحًا وَيَنْتَهِي عِنْدَ الظُّهْرِ. عَلَى كُلِّ طَالِبٍ إِحْضَارُ قُفَّازَاتٍ وَكِيسٍ. [Work will start at nine in the morning and finish at noon. Each student must bring gloves and a bag.] (Covered in unit 3: Listening and summary.)

Q8. Unit 4 (Oral presentation): Present the structure aloud. Show how you do it and say what the result is.

Model answer: سَأَتَحَدَّثُ عَنْ ثَلَاثَةِ أُمُورٍ: أَوَّلًا أَسْبَابُ الْهَدْرِ، ثَانِيًا آثَارُهُ، وَثَالِثًا الْحُلُولُ. [I will talk about three things: first the causes of waste, second its effects, and third solutions.] Signposting helps listeners follow. (Covered in unit 4: Oral presentation.)

Q9. Unit 1 (Literature and poetry): Name the devices. Show how you do it and say what the result is.

Model answer: تَشْخِيصٌ [personification]: the palm speaks as أَنَا. طِبَاقٌ [antithesis]: التُّرَابِ and السَّمَاءِ, the low and the high. اسْتِعَارَةٌ [metaphor]: the palm as the human being. Each device adds to the meaning. (Covered in unit 1: Literature and poetry.)

Q10. Unit 2 (Formal letters): Give reasons and details. Show how you do it and say what the result is.

Model answer: يَهْدِفُ الْمَعْرِضُ إِلَى تَشْجِيعِ الطُّلَّابِ عَلَى الْقِرَاءَةِ، وَسَيُشَارِكُ فِيهِ أَوْلِيَاءُ الْأُمُورِ. لِذَا أَرْجُو التَّكَرُّمَ بِالْمُوَافَقَةِ. [The fair aims to encourage students to read, and parents will take part. I therefore ask you kindly to approve.] (Covered in unit 2: Formal letters.)

levels L1 to L7

Where this course sits on the ladder

Seven slides, one for each level. Step in at your level and move up; credits stack. A level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age.

Slide 1. L1 Basic: First sounds and greetings

On the slide: L1 Basic · First sounds and greetings

Notes: Say hello, give your name and name three everyday objects in Arabic. Notes: teach مرحبا (marhaba, hello) and كتاب (kitab, book) aloud before any writing; learners greet a partner and point to a book, a pen and a door while saying each word.

Slide 2. L2 Awareness: Letters and the right-to-left page

On the slide: L2 Awareness · Letters and the right-to-left page

Notes: Recognise that Arabic is written right to left and copy the shapes of the first eight letters. Notes: show that letters change form at the start, middle and end of a word; learners trace the letter for b, then find it in three printed words.

Slide 3. L3 Foundation: Simple sentences and a first reading

On the slide: L3 Foundation · Simple sentences and a first reading

Notes: Read and write short present-tense sentences about yourself, family and daily life with short vowels marked. Notes: use هذا كتاب (hadha kitab, this is a book) as the model; learners build five new sentences, swapping the noun each time, and read them aloud.

Slide 4. L4 Practitioner: Everyday conversation and roots (this course)

On the slide: L4 Practitioner · Everyday conversation and roots

Notes: Hold a two-minute conversation on school, food and travel and use the three-letter root idea to guess new words. Notes: show k-t-b giving kitab (book) and katib (writer); learners find the root in four given words, then order a meal in a role-play.

This course: Grade 10 Arabic is at this level. Its units include Literature and poetry; Formal letters; Listening and summary; Oral presentation.

Slide 5. L5 Specialist: Formal Arabic and dialect awareness

On the slide: L5 Specialist · Formal Arabic and dialect awareness

Notes: Compare Modern Standard Arabic with one spoken dialect and write a formal letter of about 150 words. Notes: take one phrase, such as how to say 'how are you', in both registers; learners list three differences and choose the right register for a news item and a chat.

Slide 6. L6 Advanced (degree level): Literary analysis and linguistics

On the slide: L6 Advanced · Literary analysis and linguistics

Notes: Analyse a classical or modern text for grammar, rhetoric and register, and write a reasoned essay in Arabic. Notes: choose a short poem of eight lines; learners mark case endings, name one image used, and explain in a 400-word essay how the form supports the meaning.

Slide 7. L7 Super expert (postgraduate/expert): Translation, research and teaching

On the slide: L7 Super expert · Translation, research and teaching

Notes: Design and defend a translation or research project that handles dialect variation, with a documented method. Notes: pick a 500-word article and translate it for two audiences; justify every choice about register, note untranslatable terms, and have a native reader check the result.

Notes: a level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age. Learners may move up or down a level by subject.

Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review.
