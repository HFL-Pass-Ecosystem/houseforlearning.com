school course

Grade 08: French

4 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L3Secondary4 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Understand, Apply
  • Apply Talk about past and future.
  • Understand Describe a day.
  • Apply Follow a longer dialogue.
  • Apply Present.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 08 French, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 4 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

4 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Past and future in simple form Weeks 1 to 9Apply

In this unit you work on 'Past and future in simple form', taught in a way that is with notes, a worked line and a self-mark. The target is clear: talk about past and future. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Past and future in simple form' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: talk about past and future, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Model run for 'Past and future in simple form'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Activity. About 30 to 40 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: a worked task and a corrected copy.

Standard to meet. Talk about past and future: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

assessment

How you are checked

Evidence for each unit is marked with this rubric by a teacher, and by a peer where the unit allows. Pass: no criterion at 'Not yet' after one retry.

Marking rubric (Not yet, Meets, Exceeds)
CriterionNot yetMeetsExceeds
AccuracyMany slipsA few slipsCorrect and self-corrected
UnderstandingNeeds help to followFollows the main ideaExplains the idea to someone else
Speaking or writingSingle wordsFull sentencesConnected sentences with variety
Listening and readingMisses key wordsGets the main pointGets main point and detail
Practice habitRarely practisesPractises weeklyPractises most days and keeps evidence

Ten-question check, with model answers

Q1. Unit 1 (Past and future in simple form): show that you can talk about past and future. What are the first and last step you used?

Model answer: Any correct example is fine. A good answer names the first step (see or say the idea) and the last step (check or reflect) from the model run, and meets the standard: at least 8 of 10 marks on a short task, with one line of reasoning for each answer.

Q4. Unit 4 (Speaking practice): show that you can present. What are the first and last step you used?

Q5. Which action verb starts the outcome of Unit 1 (Past and future in simple form), and at which Bloom level is it?

Model answer: 'talk', at the Apply level.

Q6. When do the formative checks FA1 to FA4 and the summative checks SA1 and SA2 happen?

Model answer: FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; SA1 in week 18; SA2 in week 36.

Q7. In which weeks does Unit 4 (Speaking practice) run?

Model answer: Weeks 28 to 36.

Q8. What evidence do you keep for each unit, and why?

Model answer: A worked task and a corrected copy. It shows what you can really do and lets a teacher check it against the standard.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)

Use CAD in money problems; deliver in English, French; personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

United States

Use USD in money problems; deliver in English, Spanish; personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Singapore

Use SGD in money problems; deliver in English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil; personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Australia

Use AUD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles; guardian contact for every learner under 18.
