How a pathway works

Every pathway has a ladder of roles. Each course is one step that qualifies you for the next rung.

  1. Choose a sector and pathway. Each pathway names the career it prepares you for.
  2. Follow the ladder. Each pathway shows its roles from entry level to leadership.
  3. Take the courses in order. Courses rise from Foundation to Professional, with hands-on practice.

The 1 to 7 level scale

HFL describes competence on seven levels, from Beginner to Guru. Use it to see how far a pathway takes you.

  • 1 · BeginnerBasic awareness of the field.
  • 2 · FoundationCarries out routine tasks with guidance.
  • 3 · PractitionerDoes the standard work of the role independently.
  • 4 · SpecialistHandles non-routine problems and supervises a small team.
  • 5 · ExpertDesigns solutions and leads projects and teams.
  • 6 · MasterSets direction for a department or business line.
  • 7 · GuruShapes the field and sets the standards others follow.
