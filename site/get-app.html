Get the House for Learning app

Put House for Learning on your phone, tablet or computer. It opens like any other app, with its own icon and window. It is free, needs no store account and no password, and always shows the latest version.

House for LearningChoose your device below.

The app is installed on this device. You can close this page and open the app from your Home screen, Start menu or Dock.

Windows computerYour device
  1. Open this site in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
  2. Look at the right end of the address bar. Click the small Install icon (a screen with a down arrow). Or press the Install now button above.
  3. Click Install in the small box that opens.
  4. The app opens in its own window and is added to your Start menu. Right-click its icon in the taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar to keep it handy.

No Install icon? In Edge, click the three dots at the top right, then Apps, then Install this site as an app. In Chrome, click the three dots, then Cast, save and share, then Install page as app.

Mac computerYour device
  1. Open this site in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge and click the Install icon at the right end of the address bar (or press Install now above). Then click Install.
  2. Using Safari? Click File in the top menu, then Add to Dock, then Add.
  3. The app opens in its own window and sits in your Dock and in Launchpad.
iPhone and iPadYour device
  1. Open this page in Safari. (This is the browser with the blue compass icon.)
  2. Tap the Share button: a square with an arrow pointing up. On iPhone it is at the bottom, on iPad at the top.
  3. Scroll the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. The app icon now sits on your Home Screen like any other app.

On iPhone and iPad the app must be added from Safari. If you opened this page inside another app, tap the Share button and choose Open in Safari first.

Android phone and tablet (Samsung Galaxy too)Your device
  1. Open this page in Chrome (on a Samsung you can also use Samsung Internet).
  2. Tap the Install now button above. If it is not shown, tap the three dots at the top right of Chrome (in Samsung Internet, the three lines at the bottom right).
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then tap Install.
  4. The app icon appears on your Home screen and in your app list. It opens full screen with no browser bars.

Windows, Mac, iPhone, iPad, Samsung Galaxy and other Android devices are all covered above. Using Firefox on a computer? Open this page in Edge or Chrome to install. To remove the app later, press and hold its icon (or right-click it) and choose Remove or Uninstall.
