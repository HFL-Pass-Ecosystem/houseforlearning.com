Campus builds

Six campus types, from a single mobile unit to a full flagship. Each is worked out from our own campus standards: spaces, seats, modules and a coded element list.

Try the campus builderCampus standardsBIM project briefs

six types

From one module to a flagship

  • S1 · satellite

    Mobile unit

    1 modules, 4 learners at one time, about 14 m2.

    Enrolment days, mentor meetings, a first presence at a partner site.

    Open Mobile unit
  • S2 · satellite

    Satellite pair

    2 modules, 14 learners at one time, about 28 m2.

    A first classroom in a village, a housing community or a workplace yard.

    Open Satellite pair
  • S4 · satellite

    Studio satellite

    4 modules, 30 learners at one time, about 56 m2.

    A full class in one room: the smallest campus that meets the 30-seat studio rule.

    Open Studio satellite
  • S6 · satellite

    Full satellite

    6 modules, 30 learners at one time, about 84 m2.

    A small town campus that stands on its own, with its own washrooms and pantry.

    Open Full satellite
  • BP · business park

    Business-park campus

    24 modules, 84 learners at one time, about 336 m2.

    A campus close to employers: core learning, project rooms and a coffee bar. Specialist labs are booked at the flagship.

    Open Business-park campus
  • FL · flagship

    Flagship campus

    194 modules, 403 learners at one time, about 2 716 m2.

    A township or large-city campus. All seven levels are taught on site, and the other campuses book its labs.

    Open Flagship campus

how a real campus is made

Type, climate annex, campus number

  • Pick the campus type that fits the number of learners.
  • Add the climate annex for the site.
  • Give the campus its number (C01, C02 and so on), which starts every file name.
  • Follow the LOD stage plan from brief to hand-over.
  • A local annex covers local rules. Where a local rule is stricter than ours, the local rule wins.

Climate annexes

  • climate annex A

    Hot and humid

    For coastal, monsoon and tropical sites: heat, heavy rain, damp air, salt near the sea and strong storms.

    Open the hot and humid annex

    Temperate

    For mild sites with warm summers, cool winters and rain through the year.

    Cold

    For sites with long winters, snow and frost.
