our promise

Skills, learning and market reach

Learn and earn: bloom where you are planted. Course and career pathways: your growth is our goal.

We help people and institutions across the world improve their skills, their learning and their reach into the market, using our digital enterprise platform, emerging technology and deep domain knowledge.

Our approach is open: it works with many systems, software and hardware, and many ways of doing business. It is aligned with data-protection law, the UN Sustainable Development Goals, OECD guidance, and each country and community’s own rules.

think digital

How our digital helpers work

Skills of people are joined with automated systems and resources to make solutions and run them to the task and standard.

  1. 01

    Reading

    Understands what a text means.

  2. 02

    Understanding

    Takes whole steps such as decisions and actions.

  3. 03

    Writing

    Turns the system data into plain human language to reply.

  4. 04

    Make and check

    The system makes the output; you check and approve it, or send it round again to improve it.

  5. 05

    Ask me anything coach

    Takes your questions and uses the libraries and public information you use most, so you get the best set of answers.

two markets

School and after school

We serve school education, from kindergarten to grade 12, and after school: undergraduate, postgraduate and professional learning. In both, learning is live or at your own pace, on computers, tablets and phones.

marketplace and workplace

One place to learn, work and trade

We believe in early use of new technology to meet big human problems in healthcare, climate and sustainability, and we give you a ready marketplace and workplace for building skills in new technology products and services.
