about us

How we help

Skills, learning and market reach, joined up in one place.

our promise

Skills, learning and market reach

We help people and institutions across the world improve their skills, their learning and their reach into the market, using our digital enterprise platform, emerging technology and deep domain knowledge.

Learn and earn: bloom where you are planted. Course and career pathways: your growth is our goal.

Our approach is open: it works with many systems, software and hardware, and many ways of doing business. Our teaching method, our processes and our growth method connect sectors and communities, and record social needs in line with data-protection law, the UN Sustainable Development Goals and OECD guidance, and each country and community's own rules.

think digital

How our digital helpers work

Skills of people are joined with automated systems and resources to make solutions and run them to the task and standard. Five helpers do the work:

  • Reading

    Understands what a text means.

  • Understanding

    Takes whole steps such as decisions and actions.

  • Writing

    Turns the system data into plain human language to reply.

  • Make and check

    The system makes the output; you check and approve it, or send it round again to improve it.

  • Ask me anything coach

    Takes your questions and uses the libraries and public information you use most, so you get the best set of answers.

marketplace and workplace

One place to learn, work and trade

We believe in early use of new technology to meet big human problems in healthcare, climate and sustainability. We give you a ready marketplace and workplace for building skills in many new technology products and services.

Investment, enterprise, development and management come with a full suite:

  • Connected-device services and people
  • Information and communication technology services and people
  • A full business stack (planning, customers, reporting, office tools) with no-code tools, built on our own interfaces and apps
  • An open-standards ecosystem of course and career pathways, joined to a marketplace and a workplace
  • Consultancy, channel, business-operations, support and full-service ecosystem
  • A partner, affiliate and value-provider network in four levels, hub and spoke
  • Artificial intelligence and machine learning
  • Big data and analytics
  • Career pathways
  • Authoring, training, marketplace and workplace

Experienced mentors and facilitators guide your professional, academic, trade, business and leisure goals. Our artificial intelligence and game-style learning keep you engaged and joined up, with paths you can trace back and forth, so you can move freely and grow skilled while staying delighted.
