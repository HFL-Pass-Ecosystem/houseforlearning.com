Grade 02: Hindi

4 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L1Primary4 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Remember
  • Remember Name family members.
  • Remember Name colours and numbers to 20.
  • Remember Name animals and birds.
  • Remember Read and write short sentences.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 02 Hindi, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 4 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

4 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Family and home words Weeks 1 to 9Remember

In this unit you work on 'Family and home words', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: name family members. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Family and home words' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: name family members, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: Maa (mother), pitaji (father), bhai (brother), behan (sister). 'Yeh mera ghar hai.' (This is my home.) Method: Model run for 'Family and home words'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Learn family words: माँ [mother], पिताजी [father], भाई [brother], बहन [sister], दादा [paternal grandfather], दादी [paternal grandmother], नाना [maternal grandfather], नानी [maternal grandmother], चाचा [paternal uncle]. Say each word and point to a family photo.
  2. Step 2, Use मेरा, मेरी, मेरे [my]. मेरा भाई [my brother], मेरी बहन [my sister], मेरी माँ [my mother], मेरे पिताजी [my father]. Match with the noun: मेरा for a masculine noun, मेरी for a feminine noun, मेरे for a respectful or plural one.
  3. Step 3, Introduce people. यह मेरी माँ हैं। [This is my mother.] ये मेरे दादाजी हैं। [This is my grandfather.] यह मेरा भाई है। [This is my brother.] वह मेरी बहन है। [That is my sister.] Elders take हैं as a mark of respect.
  4. Step 4, Ask and answer. तुम्हारे घर में कौन-कौन है? [Who is in your house?] मेरे घर में माँ, पिताजी, दादी और मैं हैं। [In my house there are mother, father, grandmother and I.] घर में चार लोग हैं। [There are four people in the house.]
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A child says मेरा बहन. Correction: मेरी बहन, because बहन is feminine. Another slip is मेरी भाई; say मेरा भाई. Look at the noun first, then pick मेरा or मेरी.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write three sentences introducing your mother, brother and grandmother. Model answer: यह मेरी माँ हैं। यह मेरा भाई है। ये मेरी दादी हैं। [This is my mother. This is my brother. This is my grandmother.]

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Name family members: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Unit 2 Colours and numbers Weeks 10 to 18Remember

In this unit you work on 'Colours and numbers', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: name colours and numbers to 20. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Colours and numbers' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: name colours and numbers to 20, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: lal (red), neela (blue), hara (green). ek, do, teen are 1, 2, 3. Method: Model run for 'Colours and numbers'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Learn colours: लाल [red], नीला [blue], हरा [green], पीला [yellow], सफ़ेद [white], काला [black], गुलाबी [pink], नारंगी [orange]. Hold up crayons and ask: यह कौन-सा रंग है? [Which colour is this?] Answer: यह लाल है। [This is red.]
  2. Step 2, Count from 1 to 10: एक, दो, तीन, चार, पाँच, छह, सात, आठ, नौ, दस. Then 11 to 20: ग्यारह, बारह, तेरह, चौदह, पंद्रह, सोलह, सत्रह, अठारह, उन्नीस, बीस. Count marbles, and say each number aloud.
  3. Step 3, Make sentences. मेरे पास पाँच लाल गुब्बारे हैं। [I have five red balloons.] टोकरी में बारह आम हैं। [There are twelve mangoes in the basket.] आसमान नीला है। [The sky is blue.] Count and describe objects around you.
  4. Step 4, Match colour words to the noun. हरा तोता [a green parrot], हरी मिर्च [a green chilli], हरे पत्ते [green leaves]. Colours ending in आ change: हरा for a masculine noun, हरी for a feminine noun, हरे for a plural masculine noun.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A child says हरा मिर्च. Correction: हरी मिर्च, because मिर्च is feminine. Also सत्रह is the number for 17 and अठारह is 18; keep the two apart by listening to the middle sound.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: say you have fifteen yellow flowers and a blue bag. Model answer: मेरे पास पंद्रह पीले फूल हैं। मेरा बस्ता नीला है। [I have fifteen yellow flowers. My bag is blue.] Notice पीले agrees with the plural फूल.

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Name colours and numbers to 20: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Unit 3 Animals and birds Weeks 19 to 27Remember

In this unit you work on 'Animals and birds', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: name animals and birds. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Animals and birds' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: name animals and birds, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: kutta (dog), billi (cat), tota (parrot), mor (peacock). 'Yeh tota hai.' (This is a parrot.) Method: Model run for 'Animals and birds'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Name animals: शेर [lion], हाथी [elephant], बंदर [monkey], गाय [cow], कुत्ता [dog], बिल्ली [cat]. Then birds: तोता [parrot], मोर [peacock], कौआ [crow], चिड़िया [sparrow], कबूतर [pigeon]. Say each word and imitate the creature.
  2. Step 2, Describe them in short sentences. हाथी बड़ा है। [The elephant is big.] तोता हरा है। [The parrot is green.] बिल्ली छोटी है। [The cat is small.] शेर दहाड़ता है। [The lion roars.] Notice बड़ा, छोटी change with the noun.
  3. Step 3, Say what animals do. गाय दूध देती है। [The cow gives milk.] कौआ काँव-काँव करता है। [The crow caws.] मोर नाचता है। [The peacock dances.] चिड़िया पेड़ पर बैठी है। [The sparrow is sitting on the tree.]
  4. Step 4, Ask and answer. तुम्हें कौन-सा जानवर पसंद है? [Which animal do you like?] मुझे हाथी पसंद है। [I like the elephant.] क्यों? [Why?] क्योंकि वह बहुत बड़ा है। [Because he is very big.]
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A child says तोता बैठी है. Correction: तोता बैठा है, because तोता is masculine. Likewise say चिड़िया बैठी है, because चिड़िया is feminine, not चिड़िया बैठा है.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write about a peacock and a cat. Model answer: मोर सुंदर है। वह नाचता है। बिल्ली छोटी है। वह दूध पीती है। [The peacock is beautiful. It dances. The cat is small. It drinks milk.]

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Name animals and birds: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Unit 4 Short sentences Weeks 28 to 36Remember

In this unit you work on 'Short sentences', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: read and write short sentences. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • Listen first: your teacher or a recording says the new words or sentences from 'Short sentences' twice. You repeat them aloud.
  • Notice the pattern: find where the new sound, word form or sentence rule appears in a short text and mark it.
  • Use it: read and write short sentences, first with help, then alone.
  • Check and fix: read your work aloud once more; fix one thing you hear is wrong.
Worked example. Solved: मेरा नाम अमन है। मैं स्कूल जाता हूँ। यह मेरी किताब है। शब्द: कलम, किताब, गेंद। Method: Model run for 'Short sentences'. Teacher text (3 lines about a daily activity). Step 1, read it aloud together. Step 2, mark every word or sentence that uses the new pattern. Step 3, a learner writes or says two new lines of their own that use the same pattern. Step 4, the learner reads them aloud and corrects one slip. What a good answer looks like: the pattern is used correctly in both lines, the lines make sense, and the learner can say which part is the pattern.

Step by step.

  1. Step 1, Learn the order of a Hindi sentence: who, what, action. मीना किताब पढ़ती है। [Meena reads a book.] Here मीना is the doer, किताब is the thing, पढ़ती है is the action. The action comes at the end.
  2. Step 2, Make more sentences. रवि फुटबॉल खेलता है। [Ravi plays football.] माँ रोटी बनाती है। [Mother makes roti.] मैं पानी पीता हूँ। [I drink water, said by a boy.] मैं पानी पीती हूँ। [I drink water, said by a girl.]
  3. Step 3, End sentences correctly. A full stop in Hindi is a vertical line। not a dot. Write: बच्चे स्कूल जाते हैं। [Children go to school.] Check that every sentence ends with the vertical line, and leave a space between words.
  4. Step 4, Describe a picture. चित्र में एक लड़की है। [In the picture there is a girl.] वह फूल तोड़ती है। [She picks a flower.] पास में एक कुत्ता है। [Nearby there is a dog.] वह भौंकता है। [He barks.] Look at each picture and say three sentences.
  5. Step 5, Fix an error. A child writes पढ़ती मीना किताब है. Correction: मीना किताब पढ़ती है। The action word should be at the end. Also check that the verb matches the doer: लड़का खेलता है, लड़की खेलती है.
  6. Step 6, Your turn: write two sentences about a boy who drinks milk and a girl who reads. Model answer: लड़का दूध पीता है। लड़की किताब पढ़ती है। [The boy drinks milk. The girl reads a book.]

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Read and write short sentences: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

levels L1 to L7

Where this course sits on the ladder

Seven slides, one for each level. Step in at your level and move up; credits stack. A level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age.

Slide 1. L1 Basic: Greetings and first words (this course)

On the slide: L1 Basic · Greetings and first words

Notes: Greet people politely, say your name and name five family members in Hindi. Notes: teach नमस्ते (namaste, hello) and मेरा नाम ... है (mera naam ... hai, my name is ...) by ear; learners greet a partner and point to family photos while saying each word.

This course: Grade 02 Hindi is at this level. Its units include Family and home words; Colours and numbers; Animals and birds; Short sentences.

Slide 2. L2 Awareness: The Devanagari script

On the slide: L2 Awareness · The Devanagari script

Notes: Recognise the vowels and first consonants of Devanagari and copy them neatly under the top line. Notes: show how letters hang from a horizontal line, then write क (ka) with correct stroke order; learners find it in three printed words and read each one aloud.

Slide 3. L3 Foundation: Simple sentences and postpositions

On the slide: L3 Foundation · Simple sentences and postpositions

Notes: Read and write short sentences with verb at the end and use postpositions such as में (in) and पर (on). Notes: model किताब मेज़ पर है (kitaab mez par hai, the book is on the table); learners make five sentences about a classroom and draw each one.

Slide 4. L4 Practitioner: Daily conversation and tenses

On the slide: L4 Practitioner · Daily conversation and tenses

Notes: Hold a short conversation about daily life using present, past and future tenses with correct gender agreement. Notes: contrast वह जाता है (he goes) with वह जाती है (she goes); learners ask a partner what they did yesterday, then write six sentences marking each verb ending.

Slide 5. L5 Specialist: Reading, writing and register

On the slide: L5 Specialist · Reading, writing and register

Notes: Read a news article and write a formal letter of about 200 words, choosing between polite and casual forms. Notes: compare आप, तुम and तू (you, three levels of respect) in one dialogue; learners rewrite it for a teacher, a friend and a child.

Slide 6. L6 Advanced (degree level): Literature and linguistics

On the slide: L6 Advanced · Literature and linguistics

Notes: Analyse a Hindi poem or story for theme, form and vocabulary origins, and write a critical essay in Hindi. Notes: use a short poem; learners sort ten words into Sanskrit-derived, Persian-derived and English-derived, then argue in 500 words how word choice shapes tone.

Slide 7. L7 Super expert (postgraduate/expert): Research, translation and teaching

On the slide: L7 Super expert · Research, translation and teaching

Notes: Design a research or translation project on Hindi language or literature, justify the method and present findings to peers. Notes: pick a topic such as code-switching in advertising; collect forty examples, classify them, discuss limits of the sample, and have a fluent reviewer check every claim.

Notes: a level is reached when you can meet its standard, not by age. Learners may move up or down a level by subject.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Use INR in money problems; deliver in English, Hindi, Kannada; personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Use EUR in money problems; deliver in Dutch, English; personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Use AED in money problems; deliver in Arabic, English; personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Use BRL in money problems; deliver in Portuguese (Spanish and English support); personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Canada (Toronto)

Use CAD in money problems; deliver in English, French; personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

United States

Use USD in money problems; deliver in English, Spanish; personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Singapore

Use SGD in money problems; deliver in English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil; personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA); guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Australia

Use AUD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Use HKD in money problems; deliver in Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English; personal data rule: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)

Use GEL or ILS in money problems; deliver in Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic, English; personal data rule: the personal data law of the country where the learner lives; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Cayman Islands (Caribbean)

Use KYD in money problems; deliver in English; personal data rule: the Data Protection Act; guardian contact for every learner under 18.

Mexico

Use MXN in money problems; deliver in Spanish, English; personal data rule: the federal law on personal data held by private parties; guardian contact for every learner under 18.
