Course & Career Pathways

Your growth, our goal.

EdTech can be considered to operate in two broad markets:

K-12, covering education from kindergarten to grade 12; and

Post-K-12, comprising undergraduate, postgraduate, and professional courses.

In both of these markets, HFL aims to deliver synchronous and asynchronous learning through computers, tablets, smartphones, and other portable electronic devices (PEDs).

The agnostic (in an HFL DEM & DAAP ecosystem context) refers to all the features so that it is interoperable among various systems. The term can refer to software & hardware, as well as business processes or practices, pedagogy, framework, CAP™ processes and GROWTH™ methodology, ushering in a new era of connected ecosystems where cross-sector and cross-community engagements are automated.

At HFL, we believe in the early adoption of new technologies to solve the challenges facing modern humanity in the fields of healthcare, climate change, sustainability, and the like.

HFL provides a conducive marketplace and workplace, an enabling business environment for skills development related to a variety of emerging technology products and services. The investment, enterprise, development and management activities come with a fully equipped suite of:

  • Internet of Things (IoT) Services & People
  • Information & Communications Technologies Services & People
  • ICTSP & IOTSP full stack with ERP, CRM, MIS, MS365, MSD, MSA, etc., with a no-code ecosystem powered by custom APIs and Apps
  • e2e Open Standards DEM & DAAP ecosystem with ACS, ATLE, PAVER, LAB & LEAP course and career pathways in a connected Marketplace & Workplace
  • Consultancy, Channel, GBOS, Support, Full-Service ecosystem
  • L1 to L4 partner, affiliate, value provider hub & spoke ecosystem
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning

Career Pathways Authoring, Training, Marketplace, Workplace and more…

We guide and facilitate your professional, academic and entrepreneurial goals, from your first unit to real work.

  • Authoring

    Turn what you know into short units and courses that others can learn from.

  • Training

    Blended learning: about 70% practice on real tasks, 20% digital study and 10% with a facilitator.

  • Marketplace

    Courses, tools and services from verified partners, in one connected place.

  • Workplace

    Use your new skills at real work, with support when you get stuck.
