    Build the habit and the care: real stories and reflection prompts.

    Practise the skill: guided tasks, simulators and supervised work.

blended learning

Mostly practice, with help when you need it

A blended course spends about 70% of your time on practice with real tasks, 20% on digital study and 10% with a facilitator.

You learn by doing, and a person is there when you get stuck.
