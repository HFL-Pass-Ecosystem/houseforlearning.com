Course & Career Pathways

Your growth, our goal.

We serve EdTech in two broad markets: K-12 (kindergarten to grade 12) and post-K-12 (undergraduate, postgraduate and professional courses). Learning is synchronous and asynchronous, on computers, tablets, smartphones and other portable devices.

Our ecosystem is “agnostic”: it is built on DEM and DAAP, interoperable and cross-sector. “Think Digital” skill sets are combined by automated systems, and five helpers guide you: NLP, NLU, NLG, M&C (Make and Check) and AMAC (Ask Me Anything Coach).
