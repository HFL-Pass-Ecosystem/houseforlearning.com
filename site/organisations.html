who we work with

Four kinds of partner

Employers, institutions, trainers and community groups can all use our units, ladders and live classes.

  1. 01

    Employers and small businesses

    Onboarding ladders and skill paths shaped around the roles in your team.

  2. 02

    Schools, colleges and academies

    Blended units and live classes alongside your own teaching.

  3. 03

    Trainers

    Train the trainer: learn to teach, coach and assess others with our units.

  4. 04

    Community groups

    Learning for people who are often left out, close to where they live.

how it works

From first call to first results

  1. Talk

    Tell us about your people, roles and goals.

  2. Plan

    We map your roles to units and an onboarding ladder.

  3. Run

    Online units, practice and live classes, mixed 70-20-10: seventy parts practice, twenty social and mentoring, ten formal learning.

  4. Review

    Every term we check results, feedback and safety, and adjust.

for employers

What employers get, and what we ask

  1. 01

    Ladders for your roles

    Onboarding steps 0 to 3 shaped around the jobs in your team.

  2. 02

    Evidence of skills

    Quizzes and observed tasks show what each person can do, on their own record.

  3. 03

    Safety first

    Safety units before hands-on work, and supervised practice on training rigs.

  4. 04

    Learn and earn

    Planned routes where your people study and work on real projects side by side.

In return we ask employers to protect study time, to supervise practical work safely, and to respect each learner’s privacy choices.

partner programme

Four tiers, checked every quarter

Partners join at one of four levels, from partner and affiliate to value provider, in a hub-and-spoke network. Each level has a memorandum checklist, and every partner is reviewed once a quarter.

tenders and proposals

Training tenders and requests for proposal

Organisations can invite us to bid for training, learning platforms or campus programmes. Send your tender pack through the contact form.

  1. Send

    The tender pack, the closing date and a contact person.

  2. Acknowledge

    We reply within one working day and name the person handling it.

  3. Clarify

    We ask our questions in writing, before the closing date.

  4. Propose

    A written proposal in your format, with prices, plan and how we measure results.

white-label editions

Our learning area, under your own name

An organisation can run a white-label edition of our learning area: the same units, ladders and live classes, shown in its own design and under its own name, with its own contact address.

Each edition is kept separate: its learners see only that organisation’s name and pages, and their records stay apart from every other edition. Questions about an edition go to the contact shown on that organisation’s site.

privacy

Insights for teams, never private answers

Organisations see learning insights at team level, and only for groups large enough that no one can be singled out. Private questions a learner asks stay private.

A learner shares insights with their organisation only if they switch that on.
