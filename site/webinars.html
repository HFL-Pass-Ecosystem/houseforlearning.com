live classes

Webinars and live classes

Live sessions bring a faculty member and a group of learners together. They are the facilitator part of a blended course, and they will be open to members once the members area opens.

what to expect

Every live session

  • your time

    Your own time zone

    Times will show in your own time zone, for India, the Gulf, the UK, Canada and more.

  • captions

    Captions on every session

    Every session will have captions in its own language, with more languages added as they are checked.

  • languages

    More than one language

    Sessions will run in English and Hindi first, with more languages to follow.

  • planned

    Read-along voice

    An assistant voice that can read along in your language is planned.

kinds of session

What we run live

  • Live Q&A for each series, such as Learning how to learn
  • Topic sessions, for example reading a single-line diagram
  • Choosing your pathway, for new learners
  • Classroom preparation for hands-on units

Dates, times and booking open with the members area. Members will see the calendar and save a seat in My Learning.

your next step

Hear first when dates open

Leave your details and choose “Webinars and live classes”.
