campus build · S6 · satellite tier

Full satellite

Six modules on one floor: a learning studio, a welcome module and a service module. It is the complete satellite campus of the standard.

Try the campus builderLOD stage plan

at a glance

Size and capacity

  • Tier: Satellite
  • Modules: 6
  • Floors: 1
  • Floor area: about 84 m2
  • Learners at one time: 30
  • Rooms: 4

Used for. A small town campus that stands on its own, with its own washrooms and pantry.

build order

How it is put together

  1. Level the base and lay the water and drain line to the service end.
  2. Place the four studio modules, then the welcome and service modules.
  3. Fit the glass wall, doors, ramp and movable wall. Waterproof the wet rooms up to 1.5 m.
  4. Connect and test power, cooling, water, drainage, data and the fire system.
  5. Place furniture and phone booths, walk both exit routes and record the hand-over.

model files

File names for this build

  • C01-B1-L1-ARC: architecture model, floor 1
  • C01-B1-L1-STR: structure model, floor 1
  • C01-B1-L1-MEP: services model, floor 1
  • Replace C01 with the number of the real campus. The element code follows when a file holds one element type, for example C01-B1-L1-ARC-CS-SH-05 for the glass wall.
