Online Car Dealership Operations

Business and Commerce

how it is taught

Practice, sharing and study

  • Practice (70%)

    Plan listings, inspection and handover steps for a sample dealership.

  • Share and mentoring (20%)

    Customer-journey circle.

  • Study (10%)

    Short modules: consumer rules, documentation, financing awareness.

The journey

  1. Idea
  2. Action
  3. Value
  4. Empowerment
  5. Regeneration

Length: Long Module, 1-3 months.

Experts and training partners are welcome to contribute to this course. Partner with us

lesson by lesson

The nine steps

Long Module, 1-3 months, about 60 hours: DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h. Tools: free spreadsheet, free form and survey tools, our own pay page for tests.

Lesson outline
StepStageKindHoursWhat you do
1IdeaSTUDY2Why this matters and who needs it: scan the local market and name one real user or customer.
2IdeaSHARE3Kick-off circle: each learner states a goal; mentor sets the evidence you will produce.
3ActionDO14Start the practice task: Plan listings, inspection and handover steps for a sample dealership.
4ActionSTUDY2Short modules: consumer rules, documentation, financing awareness.
5ActionDO14Continue and log every step, cost and problem in your record.
6ValueSHARE6Customer-journey circle.
7ValueDO14Finish the task, measure the result against your goal, write three lessons learned.
8EmpowermentSHARE3Present your evidence to a review room; give two peers written feedback.
9RegenerationSTUDY2Check (10 questions, aligned with recognised standards, not a certificate) and next-step plan.

Kept in My page: the task record, cost or result sheet, peer feedback, check result.

To pass (draft): evidence reviewed by a mentor, 70% on the check, two peer reviews given.

Limits: brand-neutral, no personal data, no medical, legal or investment advice; local rules checked with an adviser.
