Four quick games, one for each part of life. Each takes under two minutes and teaches one real idea. Every win adds points to your counter at the top.

Personal and life

You get 24 hours a day, like everyone. Slide each bar to build your ideal day. The total must reach exactly 24.

The idea: a good day is a trade. More of one thing always means less of another. Most people who plan their hours find time they did not know they had.

Work

Balance the load

A small workshop has a 10 kW supply. Switch machines on to finish the orders, but if the total goes past 10 kW the breaker trips. Aim for 8 to 10 kW, the efficient zone.

0 kW of 10 kW

The idea: power is shared. Engineers call this load management. Running everything at once is not clever; planning the order is. Real factories use the same thinking to cut their bills.
