The course record

Every live session or course run has a record with the same fields, so learners, hosts and partners find the same things in the same place.

Fields in a course record
FieldWhat it holdsFilled in by
Course codeCourse, country (two-letter code), language and date, joined, for example CRS-AE-EN-20261115.Set by the host
Description and objectivesWhat the course is and what you will be able to do. Pick one or more objectives.Host
Date and timeShown in two time zones, with a link to convert to your own.Host
LengthDays, hours and minutes.Host
HostThe organisation or person running the session.Host
Instructor teamLead, assistant, moderator, end-to-end administrator, logistics and experience facilitator, and a support contact.Host
Language and deliveryFace to face, web session or other; which video tool.Host
PrerequisitesWhat you should know or have done first.Host
AgendaThe steps of the session in order.Host
Course featuresWorks on web, tablet or mobile; or face to face only.Host
StandardsRules and standards that apply, the business case, and attached papers.Host
Where and limitsCountries it is open in and any restrictions.Host
Who it is forGovernments, companies, institutions, families or individuals, and the sector.Host
TypeCompliance, mandatory or recommended.Host
Ask for a repeatLearners can ask for up to five new dates.Learner
Tags, links, author, contactSo the course can be found and a person can be reached.Host

after the session

Recordings and certificates

  • Who can see a recording

    Only people who actually attended. Sign in with name, organisation, email and a second step.

  • View or download

    View only by default. A paid download is an option.

  • Certificate

    You can download your certificate after you finish.

  • Materials

    The host uploads slides and papers with the session.

  • How long we keep it

    60 months. In month 58 we write to the attendee and offer a paid download. After that the host decides whether to delete it.

Retention length and paid download price are draft and need a lawyer check before they are shown to learners.
