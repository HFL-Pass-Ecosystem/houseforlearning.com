Partners

Partner journey

Universities, industry and learners move through eight stages, and every stage carries a course and credential.

Choose your sectorsContact

Your journey with us, in eight stages

Learning leads: every other stage shows its course and credential.

  1. 1

    Guided discovery

    “Show me, step by step, the solutions that fit my needs and scenario”

    Guided discovery of offers by need and scenario.

    Course and credential: available at every stage.

    What you receive

    • Interest record
    • Suggested offers
    • Suggested learning

    Open: Honeycomb selector and offer catalogue

  2. 2

    Learning

    “Let me explore my own learning paths shaped to my business”

    Personal learning path per offer and role.

    Course and credential: available at every stage.

    What you receive

    • Learning path
    • Unit completion records
    • Credential when earned

    Open: Course and credential engine (every item carries a course)

  3. 3

    Ideas board

    “Let me comment on what I would like to see and shape what is built”

    Partners suggest, vote and shape new offers and improvements.

    Course and credential: available at every stage.

    What you receive

    • Idea record
    • Score
    • Decision with reason

    Open: Idea register feeding the work queue and the catalogue

  4. 4

    Project

    “Let me sketch my site or plant from a predefined solution relevant to my scenario”

    Start a project from a predefined package and configure it.

    Course and credential: available at every stage.

    What you receive

    • Project sketch
    • Configured scope
    • Risk review record (see risk-review rules)

    Open: Packages, tiers, project controls, risk review matrix

  5. 5

    People

    “Let me learn, design and configure my project with other people in the network”

    Collaborate with peers, experts and delivery partners.

    Course and credential: available at every stage.

    What you receive

    • Team and role bindings
    • Expert introductions
    • Shared workspace

    Open: Talent network, skills directory, communities of practice

  6. 6

    Marketplace

    “Let me buy my solution through a strong channel and see my orders without printing lists”

    Order through channels with group-secured payment.

    Course and credential: available at every stage.

    What you receive

    • Order list
    • Payment record
    • Delivery plan

    Open: Commerce and channel ecosystem; pay-as-you-go and subscription services

  7. 7

    Plant

    “When my plant or building is ready, let me see asset status, check warranty, scan a device and get its manuals and spares”

    Commissioned asset record, warranty and documentation.

    Course and credential: available at every stage.

    What you receive

    • Asset register entry
    • Warranty record
    • Manuals and spares list

    Open: Asset register, handover pack, scan-to-information

  8. 8

    Service

    “Show asset status in real time, predict maintenance, alert me to anomalies and let me schedule service”

    Live asset health, predictive maintenance and scheduled service.

    Course and credential: available at every stage.

    What you receive

    • Health score
    • Alerts
    • Service bookings
    • Experience report back to the knowledge platform

    Open: Lifecycle service family (ACT-SVC-*), condition monitoring, knowledge platform

How it works for you

  • Self-service first: the partner can move through each stage without waiting for a person
  • Every hand-off writes a log line (who, what, when, evidence, approver)
  • A partner can enter at any stage and can return to an earlier stage
  • Nothing is claimed that a partner has not evidenced (partner-first rule)

After Service the journey returns to Digital Talks, so each new need starts where the last one finished. You can join at any stage and go back to an earlier one.
