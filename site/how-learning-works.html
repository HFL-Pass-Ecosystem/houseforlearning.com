how we teach

How learning works here

Three ideas shape every unit: train the whole person, learn mostly by doing, and keep learning tied to real work.

1 · the whole person

Head, heart and hands

Every unit has at least one outcome for each: knowing (what you understand), feeling (why it matters to you and how you act) and doing (a skill someone can watch you use).

Outcomes are written as verb + what + how well, so you and your coach can both see when you have got there.

2 · the blend

70-20-10

  • 70%

    Practice

    Real tasks, fill-in workshop sheets, simulators and supervised work.

  • 20%

    Digital study

    Short online units, worked examples and quizzes.

  • 10%

    Facilitator

    Live classes, coaching and feedback from a person.

3 · the 3E loop

Enterprise, Education, Engagement

  1. Enterprise: a real job raises a problem
  2. Education: the problem becomes a lesson
  3. Engagement: the lesson becomes a tool people use at work

What people learn at work flows back into the next lesson, so courses stay close to real jobs.

4 · stacking up (planned)

From one unit to a pathway

  1. One unit
  2. Micro-credit
  3. Short course
  4. Certificate stage
  5. Diploma pathway
  6. Degree pathway

Units you pass are planned to stack, step by step, with your evidence kept at every step.

This is planned, and we make no accreditation claim. See how credits stack for the honest detail.

5 · learn and earn (planned)

Study and real work, side by side

Learn-and-earn routes are planned for people who want to study and work on real projects at the same time. Your units open project roles, and what you meet on the project becomes your next lesson: the 3E loop in practice.

  • Study time is protected and agreed in advance
  • Safety units and supervision come before any hands-on work
  • Project evidence goes on your own learning record
  • Details are shared with members when each route opens

6 · ATLE: four roles

Author, Trainer, Learner, Earner

Everyone here can grow through four roles. You can start as a learner and move into any of the others when you are ready.

  • A

    Author

    Writes units, cases and workshop sheets from real work, reviewed before they are published.

  • T

    Trainer

    Runs live classes and practice sessions, and coaches learners to the same quality checks.

  • L

    Learner

    Takes units at their own pace and keeps every result on their own learning record.

  • E

    Earner

    Puts proven skills to paid work on real projects (planned), with study time protected.

7 · P-A-V-E-R: five fields

Five fields of learning

Every unit sits in one or more of five fields, so you can see where it leads.

  • P

    Professional

    Skills and continuing development for people in a profession.

  • A

    Academic

    Subject knowledge that builds towards longer study.

  • V

    Vocational

    Hands-on trade and technical skills, practised safely.

  • E

    Employer, employee and entrepreneur

    Work skills for those who hire, those who are hired and those who start their own business.

  • R

    Resilience and recreation

    Well-being, rest and play: the skills that keep you going.

8 · Collate, Lab, Leap

Three steps from what you know to what you do

  1. Collate: gather what you already know, with your evidence
  2. Lab: practise and test it in a safe space, with feedback
  3. Leap: use it at real work, and record the result

Each step leaves evidence on your learning record, so the next step builds on the last.
