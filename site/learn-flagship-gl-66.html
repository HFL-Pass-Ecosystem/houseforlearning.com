flagship course

Vertical Farming

Plan and run a small indoor or stacked farm: crops, light, water, nutrients, climate, food safety and business case.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L4 Practitioner30:00 h of lessons plus practice and sharingSelf-paced with peer circle
  • Understand Explain how stacked growing works and what suits it.
  • Apply Set light, water and nutrient schedules.
  • Analyse Compute yield, energy and water per kilogram.
  • Evaluate Judge cost and risk.
  • Create Write a 90-day pilot plan.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Plan and run a small stacked growing pilot with schedules, logs, hygiene plan, harvest records and unit economics.

Condition

A small space or shelving kit, lights, seeds, a spreadsheet, two peers and a grower mentor.

Standard

Schedules are followed and logged; yield per tray recorded; hygiene plan applied; energy and water per kg calculated; cost per kg shown; peers rate Meets or better.

Who it is for: Growers, hospitality staff, school teachers, urban entrepreneurs. Requirements: none. Materials: lesson text, worked examples, templates, rubric, quiz, tracker.

lesson by lesson

8 modules with worked examples

Module 1 What suits vertical growing 3 hUnderstand

Not every crop suits stacked indoor growing.

  • Leafy greens, herbs and microgreens are fast, light and high-value.
  • Heavy crops such as grains and tubers are rarely economic indoors.
  • Weigh space, energy and market.
  • Check local demand.
Worked example. Microgreens are ready in about 8 days and sell at a high price per kg, suiting a small indoor unit.

Activity. Pick three crops and justify them.

Standard to meet. Reasons given with numbers.

Module 2 Systems 4 hUnderstand

Choose soil, hydroponic or aeroponic systems.

  • Hydroponics grows plants in nutrient water; aeroponics mists roots.
  • Parts: trays, pumps, channels, reservoirs, timers.
  • Failure risk if pumps stop.
  • Cleaning between cycles.
Worked example. A timer controls a flood and drain tray that waters lettuce every 30 minutes.

Activity. Draw a layout.

Standard to meet. Parts labelled.

Module 3 Light 4 hApply

Plants need the right light.

  • Intensity, spectrum and hours.
  • LEDs use less heat.
  • Distance matters.
  • Photoperiod varies by crop.
Worked example. Lettuce receives about 14 hours of light a day.

Activity. Set a light schedule.

Standard to meet. Settings logged.

Module 4 Water and nutrients 4 hApply

Balance matters.

  • pH and nutrient strength checks.
  • Keep water clean; change on schedule.
  • Record readings.
  • Adjust slowly.
Worked example. pH is kept between 5.8 and 6.2 using small adjustments.

Activity. Run daily checks.

Standard to meet. Log complete.

Module 5 Climate and hygiene 4 hEvaluate

Prevent disease.

  • Temperature, humidity and airflow.
  • Clean tools, hands and trays; keep pests out.
  • Separate harvest area.
  • Dispose of waste.
Worked example. Mould appears at high humidity; fans and a dehumidifier fix it.

Activity. Write a hygiene plan.

Standard to meet. Measures specific.

Module 6 Yield, energy and water 4 hAnalyse

Measure efficiency.

  • Yield per m2 per cycle.
  • kWh per kg and litres per kg.
  • Compare with field growing.
  • Track costs.
Worked example. A tray gives 3.5 kg per cycle using 9 kWh per kg.

Activity. Calculate for your pilot.

Standard to meet. Maths correct.

Module 7 Business case 4 hEvaluate

Does it pay?

  • Setup cost, running cost, price per kg.
  • Direct sales and contracts.
  • Break-even volume.
  • Risks.
Worked example. Price 250 INR per kg; cost 190 INR.

Activity. Build a cost sheet.

Standard to meet. Currency shown.

Module 8 Pilot plan 3 hCreate

Run 90 days.

  • Targets and weekly reviews.
  • Learn from losses.
  • Decide to scale or stop.
  • Share findings.
Worked example. The pilot produces 62 kg of herbs.

Activity. Present the plan.

Standard to meet. Peers follow.

assessment

How you are checked

Your evidence: plan, daily log, harvest record, cost sheet. Two peers and one mentor mark it with this rubric. Pass: no criterion at 'Not yet'.

Ten-question check, with model answers

Q1. Which crops suit?

Model answer: Leafy greens, herbs, microgreens. (Covered in module 1: What suits vertical growing. To pass that module: Reasons given.)

Q2. Why LEDs?

Model answer: Efficient, less heat. (Covered in module 2: Systems. To pass that module: Parts labelled.)

Q3. What pH range?

Model answer: About 5.8 to 6.2 for many crops. (Covered in module 3: Light. To pass that module: Settings logged.)

Q4. Why hygiene?

Model answer: Prevent disease. (Covered in module 4: Water and nutrients. To pass that module: Log complete.)

Q5. Yield measure?

Model answer: Per m2 per cycle. (Covered in module 5: Climate and hygiene. To pass that module: Measures specific.)

Q6. Energy per kg unit?

Model answer: kWh per kg. (Covered in module 6: Yield, energy and water. To pass that module: Maths correct.)

Q7. Biggest risk?

Model answer: Pump or power failure. (Covered in module 7: Business case. To pass that module: Currency shown.)

Q8. Why compare to field?

Model answer: See whether it pays. (Covered in module 8: Pilot plan. To pass that module: Peers follow it.)

Q9. What is break-even?

Model answer: Volume covering costs. (Covered in module 8: Pilot plan. To pass that module: Peers follow it.)

Q10. Why log readings?

Model answer: Spot trends. (Covered in module 8: Pilot plan. To pass that module: Peers follow it.)

ten locations and global

Your place

Food safety and water rules differ by place.

India (Bengaluru)

Language: English, Hindi, Kannada. Write numbers in INR. Personal data rule: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Skills frame to name in your evidence: National Skills Qualifications Framework (NSQF) and the national credit framework. Consumer rule: Consumer Protection Act 2019.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Language: Dutch, English. Write numbers in EUR. Personal data rule: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Skills frame to name in your evidence: the Dutch qualification framework (NLQF) linked to the European Qualifications Framework. Consumer rule: EU and Dutch consumer rules (14-day cooling-off for online sales).

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Language: Arabic, English. Write numbers in AED. Personal data rule: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection). Skills frame to name in your evidence: the emirate education and training regulators (for example KHDA in Dubai) and the national qualifications framework. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Language: Portuguese (Spanish and English support). Write numbers in BRL. Personal data rule: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados). Skills frame to name in your evidence: the national technical-education catalogue and the S-system training bodies (SENAI, SENAC). Consumer rule: the Consumer Defence Code (CDC).

Canada (Toronto)

Language: English, French. Write numbers in CAD. Personal data rule: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply. Skills frame to name in your evidence: the provincial colleges framework and the national credential-recognition guidance. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

United States

Language: English, Spanish. Write numbers in USD. Personal data rule: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13. Skills frame to name in your evidence: state and community-college credit systems. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

Singapore

Language: English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil. Write numbers in SGD. Personal data rule: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Skills frame to name in your evidence: SkillsFuture Singapore frameworks and the national credit system. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

Australia

Language: English. Write numbers in AUD. Personal data rule: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Skills frame to name in your evidence: the Australian Qualifications Framework (AQF) levels. Consumer rule: local consumer-protection law.

Russian Federation

Russian Federation: HELD. A sanctions and licensing check by our lawyer must finish before any course is offered there. No localised version is published.

Global

The global version uses plain English, metric units, and your own currency. Machine translation is available in every language; the English text is the official text. Before you act for real, check your local rules with an adviser. Currency: USD (or your own currency, written next to every number).
