Common questions

I signed in but everything looks locked. Why?

Usually only the booking amount is paid. Finish registration and the rest unlocks, with all bonuses. If you finished today, allow 24 hours.

How do I get my certificate?

Phase by phase, after you finish the phase lessons and its quiz.

Who do I ask about payment?

Our admissions team, through the contact page. Payment questions are never answered in a public chat.
