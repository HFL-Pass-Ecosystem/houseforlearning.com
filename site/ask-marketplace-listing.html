List something in the marketplace

How a course, service or skill gets listed, and what is checked first.

steps

From idea to listing

  1. Tell us what

    Course, service, tool or consulting.

  2. We check

    Who you are, your qualifications and a sample of your work.

  3. Agree

    Price, terms and how learners reach you.

  4. List

    Your page goes live with a mark that you were checked.

what we need

What we need from you

  • Your name or organisation and how to reach you
  • What you offer, who it is for and what they will be able to do
  • Your price and refund terms
  • Proof of your experience

status

Where this stands

The marketplace is planned. Until it opens you can register interest; nothing can be bought yet.
