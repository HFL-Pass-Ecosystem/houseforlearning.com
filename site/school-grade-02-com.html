Grade 02: Computing

3 units across the school year. Each has a worked example, an activity and a standard to meet.

about this course

What you will be able to do

L1Primary3 units36 teaching weeks36-week school yearBloom levels: Apply
  • Apply Open, type, save and name a simple document.
  • Apply Use shapes, fill and undo in a drawing tool.
  • Apply Write step-by-step instructions for a daily task.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Meet the outcome of every unit in Grade 02 Computing, and keep one piece of evidence for each.

Condition

A 36-week school year, 3 units, a teacher or guide, a learning book or device, and local examples in the local language first with English support.

Standard

Each unit: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school. Course pass: no unit at 'Not yet' after one retry, and a pass in SA1 and SA2.

Assessment plan

Formative checks FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; summative checks SA1 in week 18 and SA2 in week 36.

unit by unit

3 units with worked examples

Unit 1 Using a text editor Weeks 1 to 12Apply

In this unit you work on 'Using a text editor', taught in a way that is short, playful, with objects, pictures and talk. The target is clear: open, type, save and name a simple document. You move from seeing it, to saying it, to doing it alone.

  • See it: watch or try the skill in 'Using a text editor' on a teacher screen or a safe device.
  • Name the parts: write the key words and what each part does.
  • Use it: open, type, save and name a simple document. Save your work in a named file or folder.
  • Test and fix: run it, find one fault, correct it, and note what you changed.
Worked example. Solved: Type a title, select it, make it bold, add two lines, and save as notes.txt. Reopen to check. Method: Model run for 'Using a text editor'. Goal in one sentence. Plan: list the steps in order before touching the device. Do: follow the plan, saving after each step. Test: try normal input and one unusual input. Fix: change one thing and test again. Share: show a partner and explain it. What a good answer looks like: it works for normal input, steps match the plan, and the learner can explain each part. Online safety rules apply at all times.

Activity. About 20 to 30 minutes of class work, then one short practice task for home. Keep evidence: one page in your learning book.

Standard to meet. Open, type, save and name a simple document: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

How you are checked

Evidence for each unit is marked with this rubric by a teacher, and by a peer where the unit allows. Pass: no criterion at 'Not yet' after one retry.

Marking rubric (Not yet, Meets, Exceeds)
CriterionNot yetMeetsExceeds

Understanding of the partsCannot nameNames mostNames and explains
PlanningNoneA listA clear ordered plan

Ten-question check, with model answers

Q1. Unit 1 (Using a text editor): show that you can open, type, save and name a simple document. What are the first and last step you used?

Model answer: Any correct example is fine. A good answer names the first step (see or say the idea) and the last step (check or reflect) from the model run, and meets the standard: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Q2. Unit 2 (Drawing and colouring tools): show that you can use shapes, fill and undo in a drawing tool. What are the first and last step you used?

Model answer: Any correct example is fine. A good answer names the first step (see or say the idea) and the last step (check or reflect) from the model run, and meets the standard: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Q3. Unit 3 (Giving clear instructions): show that you can write step-by-step instructions for a daily task. What are the first and last step you used?

Model answer: Any correct example is fine. A good answer names the first step (see or say the idea) and the last step (check or reflect) from the model run, and meets the standard: at least 4 of 5 attempts correct, said or written, with one real example from home or school.

Q4. Which action verb starts the outcome of Unit 1 (Using a text editor), and at which Bloom level is it?

Model answer: 'open,', at the Apply level.

Q5. When do the formative checks FA1 to FA4 and the summative checks SA1 and SA2 happen?

Model answer: FA1 to FA4 around weeks 6, 12, 24 and 30; SA1 in week 18; SA2 in week 36.

Q6. In which weeks does Unit 3 (Giving clear instructions) run?

Model answer: Weeks 25 to 36.

Q7. What evidence do you keep for each unit, and why?

Model answer: One page in your learning book. It shows what you can really do and lets a teacher check it against the standard.

ten locations and global

Localised for your place

Teach in the local language first, with English support. Use local currency, units and examples in every worksheet. Local curriculum and ministry rules are checked before use in a school; Arabic, Islamic, Social and Moral content needs licensed educators and ministry review. Child-safety rules apply for every learner under 18.

India (Bengaluru)

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)
