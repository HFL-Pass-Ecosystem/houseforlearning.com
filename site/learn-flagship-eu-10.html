Agriculture and Fisheries

Plan a season on a small plot or model farm or fishery share, track inputs, yield and selling price, and decide the next season.

about this course

What you will be able to do

  • Understand Explain the need and the process for this field.
  • Analyse Break the work into steps, risks and costs.
  • Apply Carry out the practice task with measured data.
  • Evaluate Judge results against the standard.
  • Create Produce a final pack and a 90-day plan.

Task, condition and standard

Task

Plan a season on a small plot or model farm or fishery share, track inputs, yield and selling price, and decide the next season.

Condition

Real or model setting, partner or mentor, free tools, currency written on every number. This course teaches process and safety awareness only; local authorities set rules.

Standard

Evidence reviewed by a mentor; at least 70% on the check; rubric at Meets or better; two peer reviews given.

lesson by lesson

8 modules with worked examples

Module 1 Find your first buyer 6 hUnderstand

A shop, canteen or market trader wants steady, fresh, safely handled produce or catch.

  • Visit a market or landing site for one hour.
  • Note ten products, prices per kilo, who buys and how long goods wait.
  • Ask what buyers want: size, freshness, regularity.
  • Choose one crop or species suitable for your place and season.
Worked example. Tomatoes sell at 1.4 to 2.0 per kilo at the market; a canteen needs 30 kg twice a week.

Activity. Write a one-page idea with one buyer in mind.

Standard to meet. Ten products logged; buyer named.

Module 2 Sustainable practice 6 hAnalyse

Protect soil, water and stocks.

  • Rotate crops or rest ponds and grounds.
  • Use inputs only as needed.
  • Respect catch limits and closed seasons of your area.
  • Never apply chemicals without training and the label rules; check local authority.
Worked example. Rotation: tomatoes → beans → leafy greens keeps soil healthy.

Activity. Write a sustainability plan for your plot.

Standard to meet. Plan names local rules and sources.

Module 3 Season plan and inputs 35 hApply

Plan what, how much, when, with what.

  • Choose crop or species and a calendar.
  • List inputs: seed or fry, feed, compost, water, fuel, labour hours.
  • Put currency on each line.
  • Plan two selling routes.
Worked example. Plot 200 m²: 300 plants at 0.10 = 30; compost 40; water 25; labour 60 h at 3 = 180; total 275. Expected yield 600 kg.

Activity. Write the season plan and input list.

Standard to meet. Plan has dates; costs add up.

Module 4 Grow and record 10 hApply

Keep a simple daily or weekly log.

  • Record dates, weather, work, inputs, problems.
  • Check plants or stock for health.
  • Use only safe, legal practices.
  • Photos help.
Worked example. Week 4: pests on 10% of plants; removed by hand; no spray used.

Activity. Keep the log for at least four weeks (or a model).

Standard to meet. Log is dated and complete.

Module 5 Harvest and post-harvest handling 35 hAnalyse

Most losses happen after harvest.

  • Cool quickly; keep clean.
  • Keep raw and ready foods apart.
  • Record temperature and time.
  • Pack to protect.
Worked example. Fish iced within 30 minutes stays fresh longer; tomatoes stored at shade temperature last 5 days.

Activity. Write a handling plan with temperature checkpoints.

Standard to meet. Plan covers cooling, hygiene and records.

Module 6 Market linkages and price 14 hEvaluate

Sell where the buyer is.

  • Agree quantity, quality and price before harvest.
  • Keep a sales book.
  • Cost per kilo = total cost ÷ kilos sold.
  • Margin = (price − cost) ÷ price.
Worked example. Total cost 275 ÷ 540 kg sold = 0.51 per kilo; price 1.4; margin 63.6%.

Activity. Compute your cost per kilo and margin.

Standard to meet. Numbers correct with currency.

Module 7 Review the season and plan the next 28 hCreate

What worked?

  • Compare plan to actual.
  • List three lessons.
  • Change one thing next season.
  • Check training or support available locally.
Worked example. Yield 540 vs 600 plan: 10% lower due to pests. Next season: earlier planting and netting.

Activity. Write a season review.

Standard to meet. Three lessons with numbers.

Module 8 Review room and next step 8 hCreate

Present and check.

  • Show plan, log, handling, sales.
  • Peer feedback.
  • Take the check.
  • Next: Food Industry or Trading.
Worked example. Score sheet.

Activity. Present for 8 minutes.

Standard to meet. All rubric lines at Meets or better.

Ten-question check, with model answers

Q1. Why rotate crops?

Model answer: To keep soil healthy and reduce pests and disease.

Q2. Cost per kilo: cost 275, 540 kg sold?

Model answer: About 0.51.

Q3. Why agree prices before harvest?

Model answer: It reduces risk of unsold produce.

Q4. Where do most losses occur?

Model answer: After harvest.

Q5. What should you respect when fishing?

Model answer: Catch limits and closed seasons.

Q6. Name one cold-chain step.

Model answer: Cool or ice quickly and record temperature.

Q7. Why keep a sales book?

Model answer: To see prices, buyers and patterns.

Q8. Margin at price 1.4 and cost 0.51?

Model answer: About 63.6%.

Q9. Who decides chemical use rules?

Model answer: Your local authority and the product label; only trained people apply them.

Q10. Name one sustainable practice.

Model answer: Rest ponds or rotate crops.

ten locations and global

Your place

Use local units, rules and authorities.

India (Bengaluru)

Language: English, Hindi, Kannada. Currency: INR. Business set-up: Udyam (small business) or company registration with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs. Personal data: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. Product and food rules: FSSAI.

Netherlands (Eindhoven area)

Language: Dutch, English. Currency: EUR. Business set-up: registration with the Chamber of Commerce (KvK). Personal data: EU GDPR (Dutch: AVG), supervised by the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Product and food rules: NVWA.

UAE (Dubai and Abu Dhabi)

Language: Arabic, English. Currency: AED. Business set-up: a trade licence from the emirate economic department or a free-zone licence. Personal data: Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (personal data protection). Product and food rules: the municipality food-control team (for example Dubai Municipality).

Brazil (Sao Paulo)

Language: Portuguese (Spanish and English support). Currency: BRL. Business set-up: CNPJ registration, or MEI for micro-entrepreneurs. Personal data: LGPD (Lei Geral de Protecao de Dados). Product and food rules: ANVISA and MAPA.

Canada (Toronto)

Language: English, French. Currency: CAD. Business set-up: federal or Ontario business registration. Personal data: PIPEDA, with provincial rules where they apply. Product and food rules: the Canadian Food Inspection Agency (CFIA).

United States

Language: English, Spanish. Currency: USD. Business set-up: state registration (Secretary of State) and a federal tax ID. Personal data: state privacy laws (for example California), and COPPA for children under 13. Product and food rules: the FDA and the state health department.

Singapore

Language: English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil. Currency: SGD. Business set-up: registration with ACRA. Personal data: Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Product and food rules: the Singapore Food Agency (SFA).

Australia

Language: English. Currency: AUD. Business set-up: an ABN and, for companies, ASIC registration. Personal data: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles. Product and food rules: Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ) and the state health authority.

Hong Kong (Eastern Asia)

Language: Chinese (Cantonese, Mandarin), English. Currency: HKD. Business set-up: a business registration and, for companies, the Companies Registry. Personal data: the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance. Product and food rules: the Centre for Food Safety.

Georgia and Israel (Western Asia)

Language: Georgian, Hebrew, Arabic, English. Currency: GEL or ILS. Business set-up: local business registration in the country where you work. Personal data: the personal data law of the country where the learner lives. Product and food rules: the national food-safety authority.

Cayman Islands (Caribbean)

Language: English. Currency: KYD. Business set-up: a trade and business licence or company registration. Personal data: the Data Protection Act. Product and food rules: the public health authority.

Mexico (Central America)

Language: Spanish, English. Currency: MXN. Business set-up: tax registration (RFC) and company or sole-trader registration. Personal data: the federal law on personal data held by private parties. Product and food rules: the federal health-risk authority.

Russian Federation

Russian Federation: HELD. A sanctions and licensing check by our lawyer must finish before any course is offered there. No localised version is published.
