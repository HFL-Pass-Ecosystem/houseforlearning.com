Enterprise Software Courses

Full courses on business software, from level 1 to level 7.

enterprise software

Full courses

  • Customer Relationship Management Administration and Sales Process: Run a cloud CRM from first login to a governed, reported sales and service operation.
  • Creative and Document Suite Practice: Design, PDF and Brand Control: Produce, protect and manage brand-safe designs and documents with a professional creative suite.
  • Enterprise Database and Finance Applications Practice: Learn relational data, queries, and the core finance flows of a large business application suite.
  • ERP and CRM Business Applications: Sales, Operations and Finance: Run one connected business application for sales, stock, purchasing and finance, and tailor it with low-code tools.
  • Cloud Office Admin Centre: Users, Security and Compliance: Administer a cloud office suite: people, licences, mail, files, devices, security and reports.
  • Open-Source All-in-One ERP: Setup to Daily Operations: Set up and run an open-source business suite for sales, stock, buying, accounting and websites.
  • Small-Business Cloud Suite: CRM, Finance, Desk and Automation: Connect sales, books, support, projects and automation in one affordable cloud suite.
  • Learning Management System Administration: Set up, run and report on a learning platform: users, courses, tracking, certificates and integrations.
  • Earning and Workforce Management System Administration: Run the workplace side: roles, jobs, shifts, pay records, performance and earner payouts.
  • Business Platform Developer Track: Customising a CRM with Code and Low-Code: Extend a cloud CRM with formulas, flows, code, interfaces and tests, safely.
  • Business Analyst Track: From Business Need to Working Platform: Turn a business need into requirements, process maps, configuration, tests and training.
  • Solution Consultant Track: Selling, Designing and Delivering Platform Projects: Lead discovery, design a solution, scope and price it, and deliver it with the customer.
