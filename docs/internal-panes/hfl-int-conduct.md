# Code of conduct (draft)
1. Integrity and respect: own your actions, speak honestly and kindly, treat everyone with respect.
2. Accountability: deliver the value you promise.
3. Collaboration: work with colleagues, partners and learners to find the best answer.
4. Creativity and learning: learn from every job and try better ways.
5. Clarity: know the outcome you want and say it plainly.
6. Compliance: everyone follows this code; concerns are raised early and handled promptly.
