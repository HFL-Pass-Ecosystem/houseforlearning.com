# GL-19 Wallet Payment Solutions: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised payment-security, consumer-protection and reconciliation practice. This is not a licence or a certificate. Use a test environment or paper flow only. Never handle real customers' payment data. Our own platform pays by bank transfer and UPI pages only; this module teaches how payment flows work in general. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every money number.

Tools: a free diagramming tool, a free spreadsheet, a free test or sandbox environment where available.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Follow one payment from tap to bank
Goal: map a payment in plain words and pick one business flow.
1. Walk through a payment: customer chooses to pay, the wallet or bank app confirms, the provider checks and approves, money moves, the shop sees confirmation, the bank settles later.
2. Learn the payment methods in your market: wallets, bank transfer, instant bank payment systems, cash on delivery, cards. Note which your customers use.
3. Choose a business flow: a tailor selling online, a clinic taking a booking fee, a school collecting fees.
4. Write the aim: customers pay in under a minute, the business knows who paid what, and mistakes are fixable.
Evidence: payment walk-through and chosen flow.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their one-page plan aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this module: a payment flow map, a fee table, a failure-case list, a refund process and a reconciliation sheet.
Evidence: your revised plan and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Map the flow with fees and failures
1. Draw the happy path from order to money received, with time at each step.
2. List the fees: fixed, percentage, refund fee, payout fee, currency conversion. Calculate the cost on a sample sale of 100 (any currency).
3. List failure cases: payment declined, paid twice, paid but order not confirmed, customer disputes, network drop.
4. For each failure write what the customer sees and what the business does.
Evidence: flow map, fee table, failure list.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Payment methods, security and reconciliation
Short modules:
- Payment methods: pros and cons of each for customer and business; settlement times; limits.
- Security: never store card numbers or one-time codes, use the provider's own secure pages, strong sign-in for staff, least access, alerts on unusual payments.
- Reconciliation: match the shop's sales list to the provider report to the bank statement each day; investigate differences; keep records for the period the tax law requires.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the module.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Design refunds and reconciliation
1. Write the refund policy in plain words: when, how long, how the customer is told.
2. Build a reconciliation sheet with 30 invented sales, 3 refunds, 2 duplicates and 1 missing payout. Find and explain every difference.
3. Write the staff procedure for a disputed payment.
4. Compare the total cost of two methods for a month of 200 sales.
Evidence: refund policy, reconciliation sheet, dispute procedure, cost comparison.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Flow-review circle
1. Peers try to break your flow with a failure you missed.
2. Check that reconciliation differences add up to the exact gap.
3. A mentor with finance or payments experience reviews the refund policy and dispute steps.
Evidence: missed failures, checked reconciliation, reviewed policy.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Update the flow and policy.
2. Write a customer-facing help page for payment problems in plain words.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: which method to offer first and why.
Evidence: help page and decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: the flow, the fees, the failure cases, the reconciliation and your decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Ask one real user, customer or reviewer for a short written note.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, the written note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write your 90-day plan: what you will do next, how you will measure it, what you will learn next. Good follow-on courses: Online Currency Exchange Concepts; E-Commerce Growth Marketing; E-Commerce Inventory Management.
3. Contribute back: share your failure-case list, reconciliation template or help page wording so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Sample sale of 100. Fee 1.5% + 0.3 fixed = 1.8. Business receives 98.2. Effective fee 1.8%.
2. Month of 200 sales at 100 = 20,000. Fees = 200 x 1.8 = 360. Bank transfer fixed cost 0.5 each = 100. Difference 260 a month.
3. Reconciliation: 30 sales (3,000) minus 3 refunds (240) = 2,760. Provider fees 54. Expected payout 2,706. Bank shows 2,606: a missing 100 payout (one duplicate also needs checking).
4. Refund time: policy 7 working days; test refund took 4.

## Case exercise
A customer paid twice for one order and the second payment shows as pending. Questions: What does the customer see? What does the business do? How does reconciliation catch it? Write the procedure.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Describe the main steps of a payment. 2. Name three kinds of fee. 3. What is settlement? 4. Why never store card numbers? 5. What is reconciliation? 6. What do you do when a customer is charged twice? 7. Why keep payment records? 8. Name two methods and one difference between them. 9. Why alert on unusual payments? 10. What was your first-method decision and why?

## Rules for this module
No brand names or logos of other companies on any page, slide or file. No personal data of real people in shared files. No real card or account numbers anywhere. No pretending a test is live.
