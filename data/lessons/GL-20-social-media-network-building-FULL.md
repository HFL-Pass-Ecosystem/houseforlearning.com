# GL-20 Social Media Network Building: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised online-safety, content-moderation and ethical-engagement practice. This is not a licence or a certificate. Follow each platform's own rules; protect children; never buy followers or use fake accounts. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every money number.

Tools: a free account on a suitable platform, a free spreadsheet, a free scheduling tool within its free limits.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): A community is people, not a number
Goal: choose one topic and one group of people you can serve well.
1. Describe a community you like online. Why do people stay?
2. Choose a topic you know (a craft, a language, a local sport, a study skill) and the people who care about it.
3. Write the purpose in one line: who this is for, what they get, how they should treat each other.
4. Write the community rules in 5 lines: kindness, no hate, no spam, privacy, how to report.
Evidence: topic choice, purpose line and rules.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their one-page plan aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this module: a 4-week content plan, a moderation log, an engagement table and a safety review.
Evidence: your revised plan and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Set up and plan
1. Set up the space (a page, a group or a channel) with a clear name, description and rules pinned at the top.
2. Plan 4 weeks of posts: a mix of useful tips, questions to the group, member stories (with permission), and a weekly meet-up or live talk.
3. Invite the first 20 people personally: friends, colleagues, people who already care. Tell them why.
4. Define success: number of active members, replies per post, members who post themselves, number of reports handled.
Evidence: live space, 4-week plan, invitation list (no personal data shared).

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Moderation, safety and content policy
Short modules:
- Moderation: welcome new members, answer reports within a day, warn then remove, keep notes, be consistent, never moderate alone on serious issues.
- Safety: protect children, never ask for personal data, watch for scams and impersonation, teach members not to share private details, know the platform's report tools.
- Content policy: what is allowed, what is not, how to handle misinformation and arguments, how to credit others' work, copyright awareness.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the module.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Run the community for four weeks
1. Post to the plan and reply to every member in the first hour where you can.
2. Each week record: new members, active members, posts, replies, reports, actions taken.
3. Hold one live session or meet-up and note attendance.
4. Handle at least one difficult moment using your policy and record what you did and why.
Evidence: weekly engagement table and moderation log.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Community review circle
1. Show the numbers. Look at quality, not only quantity: helpful replies, members helping each other.
2. Peers review your moderation decisions: fair, consistent, kind?
3. A mentor with community-management experience reviews your safety steps.
Evidence: reviewed numbers and moderation notes.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Adjust the plan to what members valued most.
2. Ask 10 members for a short feedback note: what is useful, what to change.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: grow, hand over to member moderators, or close kindly.
Evidence: member feedback and decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: the community, the numbers, the moderation, the feedback and your decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Ask one real user, customer or reviewer for a short written note.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, the written note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write your 90-day plan: what you will do next, how you will measure it, what you will learn next. Good follow-on courses: Influencer Marketing; Digital Marketing; Podcasting.
3. Contribute back: share your rules template, content plan or moderation-decision examples (anonymised) so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates are placeholders until the owner fixes the revenue plan).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Week 1: 20 members, 6 active (30%). Week 4: 62 members, 21 active (33.9%).
2. Posts 5 a week, replies 40 in week 4: 8 replies per post.
3. Moderation log: 9 reports, 8 handled within a day (89%), 1 in 2 days.
4. Helpful-reply share: 12 of 40 replies came from members, not the host (30%).

## Case exercise
Two members start a heated argument and one posts a private phone number. Questions: What do you remove first? What do you tell each? What do you record? Write the steps under your policy.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Why is a community more than a follower count? 2. What goes in community rules? 3. How quickly should reports be handled? 4. Why never buy followers? 5. How do you protect children in a community? 6. What should you record in a moderation log? 7. Why measure helpful replies? 8. How do you credit others' work? 9. Why do members need to be told not to share private details? 10. What was your decision and why?

## Rules for this module
No brand names or logos of other companies on any page, slide or file. No personal data of real people in shared files. No fake accounts, no bought followers, no sharing of members' private details.
