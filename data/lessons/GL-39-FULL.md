# GL-39 Virtual Interior Design Consulting: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a person with one room they want to improve and a fixed budget.
1. Market scan: Look at 5 virtual design offers. Note price with currency, what the client sends, what the designer returns, and how many changes are included.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Pick one real room (a willing peer's) and write a brief with them: purpose, users, style words, budget with currency, must-keep items.
2. Collect room details: measurements, windows, doors, sockets, photos from each corner.
3. Draw a scaled floor plan with a free tool and place existing furniture.
4. Build a mood board with colours, materials and 3 reference images you are licensed to use.
Evidence: Brief, floor plan and mood board.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Space planning: leave walking room, keep the door swing clear, place furniture around the main activity, and plan light from windows and lamps.
- Tools: free floor-plan and mood-board tools work well. Keep files light so clients can open them on a phone.
- Client briefs: write down what the client wants in their words, the budget, the deadline, how many changes are included, and who owns the final files.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Produce two layout options and a shopping list with prices and links (no brand logos shown on the boards).
2. Present to the client over a call; record the questions and changes.
3. Deliver the final pack: layout, mood board, shopping list, simple timeline.
Evidence: Final design pack.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Show your pack to peers.
2. Peers give a critique: what works, what is unclear, what is unrealistic for the budget.
3. Revise the weakest page.
Evidence: Revised page and critique notes.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: client rating, share of budget used, number of changes asked, clarity of the shopping list.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Brief: a 3 m x 3.5 m bedroom, budget 600, style words 'calm, light, simple', keep the existing bed.
2. Plan: bed against the long wall, desk by the window, 80 cm walking space, door swing clear.
3. Mood board: soft white, sand, one green; natural textures; three licensed images.
4. Shopping list: desk 150, chair 70, lamp 35, curtain 60, shelf 55 = 370 under budget (same currency).

## Case exercise
The client wants to add a wardrobe and a bigger desk, and the budget stays at 600. Questions: Does it fit? What are the trade-offs? What is the new shopping list? What do you say about changes and extra fees? Write a one-page revised proposal.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. What goes in a brief? 2. Why measure the room carefully? 3. What is a scaled plan? 4. How many changes should be agreed? 5. Why leave walking room? 6. What should the shopping list show? 7. Why keep files light? 8. How do you stay within budget? 9. What did the critique change? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Image and software licences, client contracts, ownership of files and consumer rules for distance services.
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
Only licensed images on boards. No other company logos shown. No personal data of clients in shared files. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
