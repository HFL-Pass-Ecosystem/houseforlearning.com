# GL-26 Box Subscription Services: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a small group of 10 real people who might pay each month for a box.
1. Market scan: Look at 10 subscription boxes or repeat-order services near you (food, care, craft, books, plants). Note what is inside, the price per month with currency, how long people stay, and what the reviews complain about.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Pick one theme and one buyer. Write the promise in one sentence: what arrives, how often, why it is worth the price.
2. Build a unit-economics sheet for one box: goods, packing material, labour minutes, delivery, payment fees, returns. Put the currency on every line.
3. Make 5 sample boxes. Time the packing of each. Weigh each box and price its delivery.
4. Offer the box to 10 people for a one-month paid test on our own pay page (test mode).
Evidence: Unit-economics sheet and packing-time log.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Churn: the share of buyers who stop each month. If 100 start and 20 stop, monthly churn is 20%. Average stay is about 1 divided by churn, so 5 months. Lower churn is worth more than more sign-ups.
- Packaging: strong enough to travel, no bigger than needed, easy to recycle. Test by dropping a packed box from table height.
- Logistics: weight bands change delivery price. Pick the box size just under a price band. Plan for what happens when an address is wrong or a box is lost.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Ship the test boxes on the promised day. Log every late, damaged or wrong box.
2. After 2 weeks, ask each buyer 3 questions: what did you like, what would make you cancel, what would you add.
3. Update the sheet with real delivery cost and real returns.
Evidence: Shipping log and buyer answers.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Each learner describes their box in 60 seconds.
2. Peers play an unhappy buyer and ask why they should not cancel.
3. Write the top 3 reasons to cancel and one fix for each.
Evidence: Interview notes and fix list.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: monthly churn, cost per box, margin per box, share of boxes delivered on time.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Idea: a monthly tea-and-snack box for office workers. Promise: 4 small items, delivered on the first Monday, price 30.
2. Unit economics: goods 11, box and filler 3, packing 4 minutes at 20 per hour = 1.3, delivery 5, payment fee 1, returns allowance 1 = 22.3. Margin = (30 - 22.3) / 30 = about 26%.
3. Churn: 50 start, 10 stop in month 1 = 20%. Average stay about 5 months. Lifetime income per buyer about 5 x 7.7 = 38.5 margin (same currency).
4. Log: box 7 arrived dented; cause: no corner protection; fix: add a card insert; cost +0.4.

## Case exercise
A box costs 22.3 and sells for 30. After 3 months, churn is 25% and delivery rises to 7. Questions: New margin? Average stay? Is it still worth running? Name two changes that raise margin or cut churn, and write a one-page decision.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Define churn with an example. 2. Why is a low churn rate valuable? 3. Name 4 costs inside one box. 4. Margin: price 30, cost 21 (same currency). What is it? 5. Why drop-test a box? 6. What are weight bands? 7. What do you do with a lost box? 8. Name two ways to cut cost without cutting quality. 9. Why ask buyers why they would cancel? 10. What is your decision: scale, change or stop, and why?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Auto-renewal and cancellation rules for subscriptions, distance-selling and cooling-off rights, clear price and delivery information.
Personal data: in the EU the data-protection regulation applies. India's data-protection Rules are phased in: consent managers around Nov 2026 and the main duties around May 2027. UAE executive regulations are still uncertain: check the official portal. Collect little, ask consent clearly, protect it, delete it.
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
