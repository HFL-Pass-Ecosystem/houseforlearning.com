# GL-50 Skincare Spa and Clinic Management: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a client who wants a calm, clean and honest service at a fair price.
1. Market scan: Visit or phone 5 spas or skincare clinics. Note services, price per visit with currency, how hygiene is shown, and what the booking and aftercare process looks like.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Design a service menu of 6 services. For each, write time, price, products used (no brand names) and who may perform it under local rules.
2. Write a hygiene routine: hand washing, cleaning between clients, laundry, waste, tool sterilising as local rules require.
3. Build a weekly schedule with treatment time, cleaning time and breaks.
4. Calculate break-even: rent, staff, products, laundry, energy against price and number of clients.
Evidence: Menu, hygiene routine, schedule and break-even.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Hygiene standards: clean hands, clean tools, clean surfaces, clean linen, and records of cleaning. Follow your local health authority's rules.
- Licensing: many treatments need a licensed practitioner and a registered premises. Check what applies for each service and write down who told you.
- Client care: ask about skin conditions and allergies, explain what will happen, get consent, give clear aftercare, and refer to a doctor when a skin problem is outside your scope.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Run a mock day with peers as clients: booking, welcome, consultation form, service steps (without applying anything to skin), cleaning, aftercare note.
2. Time every step and adjust the schedule.
3. Run a hygiene audit using your own checklist and note every gap.
Evidence: Mock-day timings and audit sheet.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Swap audit sheets and audit each other's routine.
2. Peers list the three most serious gaps.
3. Fix them and re-audit.
Evidence: Fix list and re-audit.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: audit gaps closed, time per client, share of clients with consent form, break-even clients per week.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Menu: facial cleanse 45 minutes at 50; hand care 30 minutes at 30; and so on. Practitioner qualification written for each.
2. Hygiene routine: wash hands, clean tools, fresh linen, wipe surfaces, record time.
3. Break-even: costs 3,000 per month, income per client 60: 3,000 / 60 = 50 clients, about 12 per week.
4. Audit: 3 gaps found: no hand-wash sign, no waste bin lid, no cleaning record.

## Case exercise
A client has a skin reaction after a visit. Questions: What do you do first? Who do you refer to? What do you record? What do you change in the routine? Write a one-page response (mock exercise: no real client).

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. What is in a hygiene routine? 2. Why is a licence check needed per service? 3. What do you ask a new client? 4. When must you refer to a doctor? 5. Why plan cleaning time? 6. Break-even: costs 3,000 per month, income per client 60 (same currency). How many clients? 7. What belongs in aftercare? 8. What is a hygiene audit? 9. What did peers find? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Premises licence, practitioner qualification per treatment, hygiene inspection, advertising of results.
Personal data: in the EU the data-protection regulation applies. India's data-protection Rules are phased in: consent managers around Nov 2026 and the main duties around May 2027. UAE executive regulations are still uncertain: check the official portal. Collect little, ask consent clearly, protect it, delete it.
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
Mock service only: nothing is applied to anyone's skin in the course. No health or cosmetic claims. No client personal data in shared files. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
