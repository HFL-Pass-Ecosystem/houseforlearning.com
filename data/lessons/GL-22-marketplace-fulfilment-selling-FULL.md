# GL-22 Marketplace Fulfilment Selling: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised consumer-protection and marketplace-seller responsibility principles. This is not a licence or a certificate. Read each marketplace's current seller terms; they change. Do not buy stock you cannot afford to lose. Product safety and import rules apply. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every money number.

Tools: a free spreadsheet, a free seller account on a marketplace where one is available, free photo editing tools.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): What selling on a marketplace really costs
Goal: choose one product and list every cost between supplier and customer.
1. Explain in plain words: a marketplace lets many sellers list products to one large audience, for a share of each sale and sometimes extra fees.
2. Choose one product you can source legally in small quantity (for example a handmade or locally made item, or surplus stock you own).
3. List every cost: product, packaging, shipping to customer, marketplace commission, payment fee, storage or fulfilment fee, returns, tax, your time.
4. Write the target: net profit per unit after all fees, in your currency.
Evidence: product choice and cost list.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their one-page plan aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this module: a margin sheet, a live or draft listing, a sales and returns log and a decision.
Evidence: your revised plan and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Source and list
1. Get quotes from two suppliers for a small first order. Record price, minimum order, delivery time, quality samples.
2. Check product safety, labelling and any restricted-category rules for the marketplace and your country.
3. Photograph the product yourself on a plain background with several angles and scale.
4. Write the listing: honest title, key facts in order, size and weight, what is included, delivery time, returns.
Evidence: supplier quotes, rule checks, photos, listing text.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Marketplace rules, inventory and fees
Short modules:
- Marketplace rules: account standards, performance targets (late shipping, cancellations, complaints), restricted goods, review and listing policies; read the latest version.
- Inventory: reorder point, how long stock takes to arrive, do not run out, do not overbuy; if the marketplace stores and ships for you, learn the storage and handling fees.
- Fees: commission by category, payment fee, shipping subsidy or charge, return handling; add them all to the margin sheet.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the module.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Sell for four weeks
1. Publish the listing. Keep a daily log: views, orders, packed on time, shipped on time, questions, returns, reviews.
2. Reply to every question within 24 hours.
3. Update the margin sheet with real fees from the first five orders and recompute profit per unit.
4. Change one thing at a time (title, first photo, price) and record the effect.
Evidence: four-week sales log and updated margin sheet.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Margin review circle
1. Peers recompute your margin per unit from the raw numbers.
2. Discuss a bad review or a return and how you would handle it.
3. A mentor with online selling experience reviews your listing and policy compliance.
Evidence: recomputed margin and reviewed listing.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Fix price, listing or sourcing to reach the target margin.
2. Compare planned versus real: margin, shipping time, return rate.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, switch product, or stop.
Evidence: planned versus real table and decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: the product, the margin, the sales log and your decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Ask one real user, customer or reviewer for a short written note.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, the written note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write your 90-day plan: what you will do next, how you will measure it, what you will learn next. Good follow-on courses: E-Commerce Inventory Management; E-Commerce Growth Marketing; Wallet Payment Solutions.
3. Contribute back: share your margin-sheet template, listing checklist or supplier-quote form so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Item cost 200, packing 15, shipping to customer 40, commission 12% of price, payment 2% of price. Price 450.
2. Commission 54, payment 9. Total costs = 200 + 15 + 40 + 54 + 9 = 318. Profit = 450 - 318 = 132 per unit (29.3% of price).
3. Add return allowance 5% of price = 22.5. Profit 109.5. Add 2 hours of time at 30 = 60: net 49.5.
4. Reorder point: 6 sales a week, lead time 2 weeks = 12 plus safety 4 = 16 units.

## Case exercise
Your main photo is a supplier image and a buyer reviews it as 'not as shown'. Questions: What rule did you break? What do you do for the buyer? How do you avoid it next time? Write the reply and the fix.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Name five costs between supplier and customer. 2. What is a reorder point? 3. Why photograph the product yourself? 4. What marketplace performance measures matter? 5. How do you compute net profit per unit? 6. Why read the latest seller terms? 7. What do you do about a bad review? 8. Why change one thing at a time? 9. What is lead time? 10. What was your decision and why?

## Rules for this module
No brand names or logos of other companies on any page, slide or file. No personal data of real people in shared files. No counterfeit or restricted goods. No fake reviews. Honest listings only.
