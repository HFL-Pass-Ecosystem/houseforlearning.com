# EU-09 Construction and Built Environment: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Learning Course, about 150 hours (DO 105 h, SHARE 30 h, STUDY 15 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence or a certificate. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 5 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a small client such as a household or a small business that wants a clear price, a safe site and a finished job on time.
1. Market scan: Visit 3 small building jobs from the public footpath only, or talk to 3 local builders or owners. Write down what is being built, the main materials, the quoted price per square metre with currency, the time taken and any safety measures you can see.
2. Talk to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what do you pay, what would you change?
3. Write a one-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the one-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 7 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce: task record, cost or result sheet, peer feedback, check result.
Evidence: revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 35 h): Start the practice task
1. Choose a small real or model project, such as a 3 m x 4 m room extension or a boundary wall.
2. Read the drawing: dimensions, levels, materials, openings. Write a list of work items.
3. Measure quantities for each item and build a bill of quantities: item, unit, quantity, rate, amount, with currency.
4. Write a site safety plan: hazards, who is at risk, controls, protective equipment, first aid, emergency contact.
Evidence: Bill of quantities and a safety plan.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 5 h): Short modules
- Site safety: falls, falling objects, digging, electricity, lifting, dust and noise are the main causes of harm. Plan controls, use protective equipment, hold a short talk before work and stop work if unsafe.
- Reading drawings: scale, plan, section, elevation, symbols and notes. Check that dimensions add up and that the drawing shows what is being asked for.
- Cost estimating: quantity x rate. Add waste, labour, equipment, delivery, overhead and a contingency for the unknown. State what is not included.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 35 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Keep a site diary for the project or model: date, weather, people, work done, problems, deliveries, safety talks.
2. Update quantities against what was actually used and note waste.
3. Build a simple programme: tasks, durations, order, and which tasks wait for others.
Evidence: Site diary, updated quantities and programme.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 15 h): Review circle
1. Share your diary page of the week.
2. A mentor reviews the bill of quantities and finds one missed item.
3. Agree the three biggest risks to cost and time.
4. Swap records with a peer and write what you could not understand without asking.
Evidence: the circle notes and the agreed change.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 35 h): Finish and measure
1. Apply the agreed change and keep recording.
2. Measure the result against your goal: difference between estimate and actual cost, waste percent, days late or early, safety checks done.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 8 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one strong point, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 5 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day plan and pick a follow-on course.
3. Contribute back: share a template or worked example. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Wall 4 m long, 2.4 m high, 0.2 m thick: volume = 4 x 2.4 x 0.2 = 1.92 m3. If a unit of 0.2 x 0.2 x 0.4 m blocks is used, each block is 0.016 m3, so about 120 blocks, plus 5% waste = 126.
2. Cost: 126 blocks at 40 = 5,040; mortar 900; labour 3 days at 1,500 = 4,500; delivery 400; total 10,840; contingency 10% = 1,084; estimate 11,924 (same currency).
3. Safety: hazard = unstable scaffold; who = bricklayer; control = base plates, ties, daily check by a competent person.
4. Programme: foundation 2 days, wall 3 days, curing 7 days, plaster 2 days.

## Case exercise
A model extension quote is 120,000 for 12 m2. During the job the client adds a window (+6,500) and bad weather stops work for 4 days. Cement waste is 12% against 5% planned. Questions: Recalculate the cost. Which changes should have been agreed in writing? What would you add to the contingency next time? Write a one-page variation note to the client.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Name three site hazards. 2. What is a bill of quantities? 3. Why add waste and contingency? 4. Volume of a wall 5 x 2.4 x 0.2 m. 5. What is a section drawing? 6. Why record variations in writing? 7. Name two protective measures for working at height. 8. What does a site diary record? 9. What is a programme? 10. Your decision: what would you change next time?

## Rules for this course
Model or supervised projects only. No unsupervised work at height or with power tools. Follow local building and safety rules; ask an adviser. Work only with a qualified, competent person on any structural job. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files.
