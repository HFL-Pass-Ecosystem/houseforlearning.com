# EU-10 Agriculture and Fisheries: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Learning Course, about 150 hours (DO 105 h, SHARE 30 h, STUDY 15 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence or a certificate. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 5 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a local buyer such as a shop, canteen or market trader who wants steady, fresh, safely handled produce or catch.
1. Market scan: Visit a market, a landing site or a farm gate for one hour. Write down 10 products, the price per kilo with currency, who buys, how long the product waits before sale and how it is kept cool.
2. Talk to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what do you pay, what would you change?
3. Write a one-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the one-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 7 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce: task record, cost or result sheet, peer feedback, check result.
Evidence: revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 35 h): Start the practice task
1. Choose a small plot, a pond share, a boat share or a model farm. Write the season plan: what, how much, when to sow or fish, what you expect to harvest.
2. Write an input list with costs (seed or fry, feed, fertiliser or compost, water, fuel, labour hours) with currency on every line.
3. Agree selling routes in advance: at least two buyers, price range and delivery day.
4. Start the season record: dated entries of work done, inputs used, weather or water notes, losses.
Evidence: Season plan, input costs and a started record.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 5 h): Short modules
- Sustainable practice: protect soil and water, rotate crops or rest ponds and grounds, use inputs only as needed, respect catch limits and closed seasons of your area.
- Post-harvest handling and cold chain: most losses happen after harvest. Cool quickly, keep clean, keep raw and ready food apart, record temperature and time.
- Market linkages: sell where the buyer already is. Agree quantity, quality and price before harvest. Keep a simple sales book and write down what each buyer says.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 35 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Keep the record every working day or visit. Weigh every harvest or catch and note grade and price.
2. Handle product after harvest: clean, grade, cool, pack, label with date and place. Note losses at each step.
3. Compare plan with reality every two weeks and adjust one thing at a time.
Evidence: Season record with weights, losses and prices.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 15 h): Review circle
1. Bring your record to the exchange circle with farmers and fishers.
2. A visiting adviser reviews cost per kilo and loss rate.
3. Agree one change that cuts loss or raises price.
4. Swap records with a peer and write what you could not understand without asking.
Evidence: the circle notes and the agreed change.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 35 h): Finish and measure
1. Apply the agreed change and keep recording.
2. Measure the result against your goal: yield per area or per trip, cost per kilo, loss percent after harvest, average selling price.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 8 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one strong point, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 5 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day plan and pick a follow-on course.
3. Contribute back: share a template or worked example. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Market walk: mango 60 per kilo, tomato 40, fish 220 (same currency). Spot the gap: a buyer wants graded, washed produce delivered by 7 a.m.
2. Plan: 200 m2 of tomato, expected 3 kg per m2 = 600 kg. Cost 18,000. Break-even price = 18,000 / 600 = 30 per kilo before losses.
3. Loss: if 15% is lost after harvest, sellable weight is 510 kg, so break-even = 18,000 / 510 = about 35 per kilo. Cooling and shade cut the loss.
4. Sales book: day, buyer, kilo, price, paid or owed. Totals every Friday.

## Case exercise
A learner harvests 600 kg of tomatoes. 90 kg spoil because crates sit in the sun for 5 hours. Cost was 18,000. Two buyers offer 38 and 45 per kilo; the second pays in 10 days. Questions: What is the loss percent? What is the cost per sellable kilo? Which buyer is better and why? Which two changes cut the loss? Write a one-page decision.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Name two causes of post-harvest loss. 2. What is a closed season and why does it exist? 3. Cost per kilo: cost 18,000, sellable 510 kg (same currency). 4. Why cool product quickly? 5. Why agree prices before harvest? 6. What goes in a season record? 7. Name one sustainable practice for soil or water. 8. How do you compare two buyers? 9. How do you label a pack? 10. What is your next-season decision and why?

## Rules for this course
Follow local rules on land, water, fishing seasons, chemicals and food safety; ask an adviser. No health claims. No use of banned chemicals. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files.
