# GL-49 Cryobank Operations (Awareness Level): full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.
This is an AWARENESS course. You learn how things work, the rules and the ethics. You do not practise treatment, diagnosis or clinical work, and you use only public, made-up or simulated material.
Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a person who wants to understand how stored samples are kept safe, traceable and consented.
1. Market scan: Read public guidance from a health authority and an ethics body about storing human tissue or reproductive material. Note the rules on consent, time limits and records.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Draw a cold-chain flow: collection, labelling, transport, receipt, freezing, storage, retrieval, disposal.
2. At each step mark the record that must exist and who signs it.
3. Design a sample label that shows only a code, not a name, and explain where the code key is kept.
4. List risks: power failure, wrong label, lost record, expired consent. Add a control for each.
Evidence: Cold-chain flow, label design and risk table.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Cold chain: keep the temperature within range at every step, with alarms, backups and checks logged. An unbroken record is as important as the cold itself.
- Records: every sample links to consent, dates and handling steps through a code. Keep access limited and logged.
- Consent: people can agree to storage for a defined period and purpose and may withdraw. Rules differ by country; check them.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Run a paper exercise: a 'freezer failure' scenario with made-up samples. Write who is told, what is moved and what is recorded.
2. Run a paper exercise on withdrawn consent: how the sample is found and destroyed and how that is recorded.
3. Check that no real names or samples appear anywhere.
Evidence: Scenario records.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Discuss two made-up ethical dilemmas about long storage and consent.
2. Each learner states the rule that applies.
3. Agree three firm principles.
Evidence: Three principles.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: controls added to the risk table, completeness of the flow, scenario handled correctly, peer rating.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates are placeholders until the owner fixes the revenue plan).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Cold chain: collect > label code > transport at set temperature > receive and check > freeze > store > retrieve > dispose.
2. Record at each step: time, temperature, person, signature.
3. Label: code only; key in a separate locked place.
4. Scenario: freezer fails at night; alarm, backup unit, move samples, record, tell the lead.

## Case exercise
A donor withdraws consent. Questions: How do you find the sample from the code? What do you destroy and how? What do you record? Who is told? Write a one-page procedure using made-up records.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. What is a cold chain? 2. Why use a code on labels? 3. What must be recorded at each step? 4. What do you do if consent is withdrawn? 5. Name two risks and controls. 6. Why limit access? 7. What is outside this course? 8. How are rules different across countries? 9. What did the scenario show? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Rules on storing human tissue or reproductive material, consent and time limits, record retention.
Personal data: in the EU the data-protection regulation applies. India's data-protection Rules are phased in: consent managers around Nov 2026 and the main duties around May 2027. UAE executive regulations are still uncertain: check the official portal. Collect little, ask consent clearly, protect it, delete it.
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
Awareness only. No real samples, no personal data. Not legal or medical advice. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
