# GL-40 Online Car Dealership Operations: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a buyer who wants to know the true condition, the real price and the paperwork.
1. Market scan: Read 10 online used-car listings. Note which photos, facts and history details are shown, which are missing, and the price range with currency.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Pick 3 sample vehicles (use dummy or consented listings). Write the listing text: facts only, no hype.
2. Create an inspection checklist: body, tyres, lights, engine bay, interior, test drive, warning lights, service history.
3. Design the buyer journey: enquiry, video call, inspection report, price, paperwork, payment, handover.
4. Plan photos: 20 standard shots, same order, good light.
Evidence: Listing text, checklist and journey map.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Consumer rules: sellers must describe a vehicle truthfully and respect the cooling-off and returns rules that apply to your country. Ask an adviser which rules apply.
- Documentation: ownership papers, service records, identity checks as local law requires, a written sale agreement, and a handover form.
- Financing awareness: buyers may borrow. Explain only that loans cost interest and that they should compare offers from licensed lenders. Do not advise on a specific loan.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Inspect 3 real cars owned by willing peers using your checklist and record every finding.
2. Write an honest condition report for each, including faults.
3. Time each step of the journey and find where a buyer would wait or worry.
Evidence: Condition reports and journey timings.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Walk through a buyer's journey.
2. Peers play a nervous buyer and ask for proof.
3. Add the proof that is missing to your listing.
Evidence: Improved listing.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: share of listings with full facts, inspection items completed, journey time, buyer trust score.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates are placeholders until the owner fixes the revenue plan).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Listing for a sample car: facts only: year, mileage, service history, known faults (scratch on door, tyre wear), price 9,500 (same currency).
2. Checklist result: tyres 3 mm tread (legal minimum differs by country), brake pad wear noted, warning light off.
3. Journey timing: enquiry 1 day, video call 1 day, inspection report 2 days, paperwork 1 day.
4. Buyer trust fix: add a short walk-around video and the written inspection report.

## Case exercise
A buyer finds a fault that was not in your listing. Questions: What do you do? What does consumer law in your area require? How do you stop it repeating? Write a one-page response and update your checklist.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Why list faults honestly? 2. Name five items on an inspection checklist. 3. What documents are needed at sale? 4. Why use the same 20 photos? 5. What is a cooling-off right? 6. What should you say about loans? 7. Where might a buyer worry? 8. What proof would help a nervous buyer? 9. What changed in your listing? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Consumer protection for vehicle sales, odometer and history disclosure, registration and transfer papers, financing-offer rules.
Personal data: in the EU the data-protection regulation applies. India's data-protection Rules are phased in: consent managers around Nov 2026 and the main duties around May 2027. UAE executive regulations are still uncertain: check the official portal. Collect little, ask consent clearly, protect it, delete it.
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
No false claims about a vehicle. No personal data or number plates of real people in shared files. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
