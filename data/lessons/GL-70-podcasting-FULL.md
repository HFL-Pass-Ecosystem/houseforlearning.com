# GL-70 Podcasting: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised standards; this is not a licence or a certificate. This module makes three short audio episodes with free tools. Respect others' rights: use only your own words, your own music or properly licensed material, and get agreement before recording anyone. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number. Units are always shown too.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who listens and what show to make
Goal: choose a show idea and a listener.
1. Listen to three public podcasts in different styles. Note length, format, intro, music, ad breaks.
2. Choose a topic you know well and a listener (for example young farmers or small shop owners).
3. Write a show card: name, promise in one sentence, length, how often.
4. List 10 possible episode ideas.
Evidence: listening notes, show card and idea list.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
Goal: say your goal aloud and agree the evidence you will produce.
1. Each learner reads their one-page plan aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is the listener, why you, what is the first 30 seconds.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence list for this module: a show card, three recorded episodes, show notes, a rights list and listener feedback.
Evidence: your revised plan and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Plan and record episode one
Goal: make a clear first episode.
1. Write an outline: hook, three points, example, call to action, close.
2. Record in a quiet room with a phone or free recorder, held 10 to 15 cm from the mouth, with soft furnishings around to reduce echo.
3. Record a short test first and listen with headphones.
4. Do two takes. Note which is better and why.
Evidence: the outline and the raw recording.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Audio, rights and distribution
Short modules:
- Audio: keep levels steady, avoid clipping, cut long pauses, add a short intro and outro, remove noise lightly.
- Rights: your own voice and words are yours; for music, clips and guests you need written permission or a licence that allows the use. Free licences have conditions you must follow.
- Consent: guests agree to be recorded and say how their words will be used.
- Distribution: an audio host with a feed lets listeners follow your show; write titles and descriptions that say what the episode gives.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the module.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Edit and publish episodes one to three
Goal: finish three episodes.
1. Edit with a free audio editor: trim, balance volume, add intro and outro from properly licensed or self-made sound.
2. Export at a sensible size and write show notes with time marks.
3. Record episodes two and three with the same method and improve one thing each time.
4. Publish privately first and share the link with 5 test listeners.
Evidence: three finished episodes and show notes.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Listener-feedback circle
Goal: learn what listeners actually hear.
1. Play 3 minutes of each learner's episode.
2. Listeners say what they understood, where they got bored, and what they would share.
3. A mentor with audio experience gives one technical tip per learner.
4. Update your plan.
Evidence: listener notes and tips.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Measure and decide
Goal: see whether the show works.
1. Gather: number of listens, average listening time (if available), replies from test listeners.
2. Compare episodes one and three.
3. Write the show plan for the next 10 episodes with a topic, guest idea and listener goal.
4. Write three lessons learned and one decision: continue, change or stop.
Evidence: measure table and the next 10 episode plan.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
Goal: show your evidence and judge two peers fairly.
1. Present to a review room in 8 minutes: the show, three episodes, feedback and the next plan.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from a test listener.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, and the note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
Goal: close the module and plan what comes next.
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what you will do, where, with whom, and what you will learn next. Good follow-on courses: Virtual Event Planning, Online Tutoring Platform, Social Media Network Building.
3. Contribute back: share your show card or episode outline template so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Outline: hook 30 s + three points 4 min each (12 min) + example 3 min + call to action 1 min = 16.5 min.
2. Edit: raw recording 22 min; cut pauses and mistakes 5 min = 17 min final.
3. Feedback: 5 test listeners; 4 finished the episode = 80% completion.
4. Growth: episode 1 had 20 listens, episode 3 had 35 = 75% more.

## Case exercise
A farmer wants a weekly 15-minute show. Questions: Who is the listener? What is the hook? Which music is permitted? What do you do with a guest who withdraws consent? Write a plan for the next 10 episodes.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. What is a hook? 2. How far from the mouth do you hold the mic? 3. Why record a test first? 4. What is clipping? 5. When do you need written permission? 6. What does a guest consent cover? 7. Why write show notes? 8. Why publish privately first? 9. What do you compare across episodes? 10. What is your decision?

## Rules for this course
No brand names or logos of other companies on any page, file or slide. No personal data in shared files. Use only your own, properly licensed or permitted material. Get consent before recording anyone. No private data of guests in shared files.
