# GL-34 Online Tutoring Platform: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a learner and a parent or guardian looking for reliable help in one subject.
1. Market scan: Look at 5 tutoring offers. Note subjects, price per hour with currency, how they show tutor checks, and how they protect children.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Choose one subject and one level. Write who the tutor helps and the result they should expect.
2. Design a 45-minute lesson plan: goal, warm-up, teaching, practice, check, homework.
3. Prepare a safeguarding sheet: who is allowed in a session, where it takes place, how it is recorded with consent, who to tell if there is a concern.
4. Deliver session 1 to a willing learner (an adult peer if you cannot reach a learner) and note what worked.
Evidence: Lesson plan and safeguarding sheet.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Lesson design: one goal per lesson, short blocks, lots of practice, quick checks. If the learner cannot do it alone by the end, the plan needs changing.
- Safeguarding: protect the learner. Use approved tools, keep sessions open to a guardian where needed, never share private contact details, and report concerns through the proper route.
- Assessment: use simple pre and post checks of the same skill so progress is visible.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Deliver sessions 2 to 5. Start each with a quick recap and end with a 3-question check.
2. Record attendance, scores and one thing to change each time.
3. Collect feedback from the learner after session 3.
Evidence: Session log and feedback.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Watch a 10-minute clip or role-play of a peer's session.
2. Give two strengths and one change.
3. Revise your own plan.
Evidence: Observation notes.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: learner check score before and after, attendance, learner rating, number of sessions delivered.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Subject: reading at primary level. Goal: read a 100-word text with at most 3 errors.
2. Lesson plan 45 minutes: 5 recap, 10 teach one sound pattern, 20 practice, 5 check, 5 homework.
3. Pre-check: 9 errors in 100 words. After 5 sessions: 3 errors. Progress: 6 fewer errors.
4. Safeguarding: sessions in an approved tool, guardian present, no private contacts shared.

## Case exercise
A learner says something that worries you at the end of a session. Questions: What do you do in the next 10 minutes? Who do you tell? What do you write down and where? What do you not do? Then redesign your session close so every child knows who to speak to.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Why one goal per lesson? 2. Name two safeguarding rules. 3. Why use a pre and post check? 4. What would you do if a learner says something worrying? 5. How long should a recap be? 6. Why get feedback after session 3? 7. What makes a plan need changing? 8. What information should never be shared? 9. How do you show progress? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Child-safeguarding duties, background-check rules for people working with children, children's data, and recording consent.
Personal data: in the EU the data-protection regulation applies. India's data-protection Rules are phased in: consent managers around Nov 2026 and the main duties around May 2027. UAE executive regulations are still uncertain: check the official portal. Collect little, ask consent clearly, protect it, delete it. AI tools: if you use an AI tool in this course, the EU AI Act transparency duties (for example telling people they are dealing with AI, and marking AI-made content) have applied since 2 Aug 2026. Stand-alone high-risk AI systems are now due from 2 Dec 2027, and AI inside regulated products from 2 Aug 2028 (these dates were delayed; check the official source for any change).
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
Adults only unless a trained safeguarding lead is in charge. No recording without consent. No personal data in shared files. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
