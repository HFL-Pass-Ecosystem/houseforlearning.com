# GL-31 Affiliate Marketing: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a reader who wants an honest recommendation before buying.
1. Market scan: Read how 5 review pages explain that they earn a commission. Note which are clear and which hide it. Note typical commission types: per sale, per lead, per click.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Pick a topic you know well and a type of product (not your own). Write who you will help.
2. Write the disclosure sentence you will place above every recommendation, in plain words.
3. Create 3 pieces of honest content: a how-to, a comparison, and a list. Include one drawback of each product.
4. Set up tracking so you can tell which piece produced which visit. Use a free analytics tool.
Evidence: Three content pieces and the disclosure sentence.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Disclosure: say clearly and near the top that you may earn money if someone buys. Many countries have advertising rules about this; find yours and write the rule down.
- Tracking: use unique links and simple counts of visits, sign-ups and sales. Be open that tracking is used and respect consent rules for cookies.
- Payouts: know the threshold, the payment date and what happens when a sale is returned. Keep records for your tax adviser.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Share the pieces in your own channels for 2 weeks and track visits and sign-ups or sales.
2. Log time spent and any cost with currency.
3. Work out earnings per hour and per piece.
Evidence: Tracking sheet with earnings and costs.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Show your results, even if they are zero.
2. Peers say which piece is clearest and which feels pushy.
3. Rewrite one piece to be more honest and useful.
Evidence: Before and after of one piece.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: visits, click-through rate, conversion rate, earnings per hour.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Topic: budget cooking for students. Disclosure above every post: 'I may earn a small commission if you buy through my links.'
2. Piece 1: how-to. Piece 2: comparison of three pans with one drawback each. Piece 3: list of five pantry items.
3. Tracking: 300 visits, 12 clicks on product links, 3 sales at a commission of 2 each = 6; time spent 10 hours; earnings per hour 0.6 (same currency).
4. Rewrite: 'The best pan ever!' becomes 'A solid pan for the price, but heavy, so not for everyone.'

## Case exercise
After a month: 800 visits, 40 clicks, 5 sales, commission 3 each, 12 hours of work, plus a hosting cost of 4. Questions: Conversion from clicks? Earnings per hour after cost? Which piece worked best? What would make it more honest and useful? Write a one-page next-month plan.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. What is an affiliate commission? 2. Where must the disclosure appear? 3. Why include a drawback? 4. What is conversion rate? 5. Why track by piece? 6. Compute: 200 visits, 6 sales (same period). Conversion? 7. What happens to commission on a returned product? 8. Why keep records? 9. What makes a recommendation trustworthy? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Advertising disclosure rules for paid links and endorsements; cookie and tracking consent; tax on earnings.
Personal data: in the EU the data-protection regulation applies. India's data-protection Rules are phased in: consent managers around Nov 2026 and the main duties around May 2027. UAE executive regulations are still uncertain: check the official portal. Collect little, ask consent clearly, protect it, delete it.
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
No false claims. No fake reviews. Always show the disclosure. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
