# EU-07 Trading and Distribution: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Learning Course, about 150 hours (DO 105 h, SHARE 30 h, STUDY 15 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised practice; this is not a licence or a certificate. Check local rules with an adviser before you sell or serve anyone. Currency is always shown: write your own currency next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 5 h): Find a real need
Pick one product with a clear supplier and a clear buyer in different places. Find three supplier quotes and two buyer needs (price, quantity, time).
Talk to 2 buyers and 3 suppliers (quotes may be requested for training purposes). Ask what they do now, how often, what they pay, and what annoys them. Write a one-page idea: offer, user, price you hope for.
Evidence: the market table and the interview notes.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 7 h): Kick-off circle
1. Read your one-page idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course.
Evidence: revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 35 h): Build the first version
1. Build a mock trade file: buyer enquiry, supplier quote, your quote, order, invoice, packing list, shipping plan.
2. Choose the trade terms (for example who pays freight and who carries risk) and write them clearly.
3. Cost the deal end to end: product, packing, freight, insurance, duties and taxes, bank charges, your margin; show currency and exchange rate used.
Evidence: the working documents, records and test results from these steps.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 5 h): Short modules
- Trade documents: invoice, packing list, bill of transport, certificate of origin; what each one proves.
- Trade terms: the standard international terms and how they split cost and risk.
- Customs and payment basics: classification codes, duty, payment terms (advance, open account, bank guarantee), exchange-rate risk; sanctions and licence checks before any deal.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 35 h): Run the pilot
Run a small real or simulated deal: place a sample order, track it from order to delivery, and record every delay and cost difference against your plan.
Keep a daily log: date, what you did, cost, result, problem, fix.
Evidence: the 30-day log.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 15 h): Costing and feedback circle
1. Build the cost sheet from your log: goods, freight, insurance, duties, bank charges, storage, exchange-rate movement. Put the currency on every line.
2. Cost per unit = total cost divided by units. Margin = (price - cost) divided by price.
3. Present to the circle; a mentor from the trade tells you which cost or risk you missed.
Evidence: cost sheet reviewed by a mentor.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 35 h): Fix and measure
1. Fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Run one more week and compare the key numbers before and after.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: before/after table and decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 8 h): Review room
Present idea, method, safety or compliance notes, cost sheet, feedback and decision to a review room. Give two peers written feedback (one strength, one fix). Get a written note from one real user or buyer.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 5 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day plan and pick a follow-on course.
3. Contribute back a template, checklist or worked example. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).

## Check questions (draft, 10)
1. What does an invoice prove?
2. What is a packing list?
3. What do trade terms decide?
4. How does exchange-rate risk hurt a margin?
5. What is a classification code for?
6. Why check sanctions and licences first?
7. Name two payment terms and their risk.
8. What was your landed cost per unit?
9. What went wrong in your sample order?
10. What is your 90-day plan?

## Rules for this course
No brand names or logos of other companies on any page, pack, slide or file. No dealings in restricted goods or with restricted parties. Adviser and lawyer check before real trades. No bribes, ever.
