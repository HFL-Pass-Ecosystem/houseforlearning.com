# GL-41 Home Sharing and Short-Stay Hosting: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a guest who wants a clean, safe, honest stay at a fair price.
1. Market scan: Read 10 public listings for short stays in your area. Note price per night with currency, what is described, what guests praise and what they complain about.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Pick a real or sample room or home and list what is included: beds, bathroom, kitchen, Wi-Fi, rules.
2. Check local rules: does short-stay hosting need a registration, a permit, tax collection or permission from a building owner or neighbours? Write the answer and the source.
3. Write an honest listing: description, photos that match reality, house rules, check-in and check-out.
4. Write the guest-experience plan: arrival message, welcome sheet, cleaning routine, emergency contacts.
Evidence: Listing, rules summary and guest plan.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Local rules: some places limit nights per year or require a permit. Breaking the rules can mean fines. Check, write it down, and renew when rules change.
- Safety: working smoke alarm, safe wiring, clear exit, first-aid kit, locks, clear emergency numbers, and honest information about stairs or noise.
- Pricing: compare with similar places, adjust for season and weekday, and include cleaning and fees clearly. Do not hide extra charges.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Host a trial stay with a willing peer or run a walk-through with a peer as a guest.
2. Use a cleaning checklist and time the turnover between guests.
3. Collect written feedback and fix the three biggest issues.
Evidence: Turnover time and feedback.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Share your guest plan.
2. Peers read it as guests and say what is unclear.
3. Write the one improvement that matters most.
Evidence: Improvement list.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: turnover time, guest score, cost per stay, nights booked share (in a model).
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Listing: a private room with a shared bathroom; price 40 per night with cleaning fee 10 shown separately.
2. Rule check: ask the local authority whether a permit or tax registration is needed; write the answer and who gave it.
3. Turnover: clean 60 minutes using a checklist; laundry 30 minutes.
4. Safety sheet: smoke alarm tested, exit route shown, first-aid kit, emergency numbers.

## Case exercise
Neighbours complain about noise and your building rules limit short stays. Questions: What do you check first? Which parts of your guest plan reduce noise? Would you continue or stop? Write a one-page decision with reasons.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. Why check local rules first? 2. Name three safety items. 3. Why should photos match reality? 4. What is turnover time? 5. How do you price by season? 6. What fees must be shown up front? 7. What belongs on a welcome sheet? 8. What would a guest complain about? 9. What did you fix? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Short-stay permits or registration, tax collection, building and landlord rules, safety duties, guest registration rules.
Personal data: in the EU the data-protection regulation applies. India's data-protection Rules are phased in: consent managers around Nov 2026 and the main duties around May 2027. UAE executive regulations are still uncertain: check the official portal. Collect little, ask consent clearly, protect it, delete it.
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
No real guests are taken without permit and consent. No personal data in shared files. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
