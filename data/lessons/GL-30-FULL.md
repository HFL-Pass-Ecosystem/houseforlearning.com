# GL-30 Coworking Space Operations: full lesson text (DRAFT v0.1, 31 Oct 2026)

Long Module, about 60 hours (DO 42 h, SHARE 12 h, STUDY 6 h). Brand-neutral. Aligned with recognised good practice; this is not a licence, a certificate or professional advice. Check local rules with an adviser before you act for real. Currency is always shown: use your own currency and write it next to every number.

Journey: Idea > Action > Value > Empowerment > Regeneration.

## Lesson 1 - Idea (STUDY, 2 h): Who needs this, and who is your first user
Goal: name one real first user: a freelancer or small team looking for a desk by the day or month.
1. Market scan: Visit or call 5 shared workspaces. Note price per day and per month with currency, opening hours, desk count, extras, and how full they look.
2. Talk to 3 to 5 possible users or experts. Ask: what do you do now, what is hard, what would make you trust a new service?
3. Write a half-page idea: who, what you will do, what success looks like in numbers.
Evidence: the scan notes and the half-page idea.

## Lesson 2 - Idea (SHARE, 3 h): Kick-off circle
1. Each learner reads their idea aloud (3 minutes).
2. Peers ask three questions: who is it for, why you, what could go wrong.
3. The mentor agrees the evidence you will produce in this course (listed at the end of each lesson).
Evidence: your revised idea page and the agreed evidence list.

## Lesson 3 - Action (DO, 14 h): Start the practice task
Task steps:
1. Choose a real or sample space of 20 desks. Measure it or use a floor plan.
2. Build an occupancy model: desks, price per day and per month, expected fill rate by month, costs of rent, power, internet, cleaning, staff.
3. Find the fill rate where income equals cost (break-even).
4. Draft three price plans: day pass, monthly desk, private room.
Evidence: Occupancy model and price plans.

## Lesson 4 - Action (STUDY, 2 h): Short modules
- Membership models: pay per day, monthly, or by hours. Keep the choice simple. Compare what each plan earns at a fill rate of 50%, 70% and 90%.
- Safety: clear exits, fire equipment as local rules require, first-aid kit, safe wiring, visitor sign-in. Ask your local authority and your building owner what applies.
- Community: members stay when they meet people. Plan a weekly shared lunch or talk, and ask members what they need.
Check: 10 questions at the end of the course.

## Lesson 5 - Action (DO, 14 h): Continue and log
Keep a record of every step, cost (with currency) and problem.
1. Test the plans with 10 possible members; ask which plan they would choose and why.
2. Draw a layout with fire exits, quiet zone, call zone and shared area.
3. Write house rules in plain words and a one-page welcome sheet.
Evidence: Layout, house rules and survey results.

## Lesson 6 - Value (SHARE, 6 h): Review circle
1. Present your break-even fill rate.
2. A real operator (panel) answers questions about what you missed.
3. Add the top 3 missing costs.
Evidence: Operator Q&A notes.

## Lesson 7 - Value (DO, 14 h): Finish and measure
1. Finish the task and fix the biggest problem found in the circle.
2. Measure the result against your goal: break-even fill rate, average income per desk, member survey score, safety checklist items done.
3. Write three lessons learned and one decision: scale, change or stop.
Evidence: a before and after table and the decision note.

## Lesson 8 - Empowerment (SHARE, 3 h): Review room
1. Present to a review room: idea, task record, numbers, risks, decision.
2. Give two peers written feedback: one thing that is strong, one thing to fix.
3. Get a written note from one real user or expert where possible.
Evidence: slides or notes, two peer reviews, one outside note.

## Lesson 9 - Regeneration (STUDY, 2 h): Check and next step
1. Take the 10-question check. Pass mark is 70% (draft).
2. Write a 90-day next-step plan: what to do, where, how much, what to learn next. Pick a follow-on course from the catalogue.
3. Contribute back: share a template, checklist or worked example so the next learner starts higher. Accepted items earn credits (rates will be confirmed before opening).
Evidence: check result and the 90-day plan.

## Worked examples
1. Space: 20 desks; monthly price 400; day pass 25.
2. Costs per month: rent 3,000, power and internet 600, cleaning 400, host 1,500 = 5,500.
3. Break-even: 5,500 / 400 = 13.75, so 14 desks, or 69% full. At 90% (18 desks) income 7,200, profit 1,700 (same currency).
4. Plan test: 6 of 10 chose the monthly desk, 3 the day pass, 1 the private room.

## Case exercise
A space of 20 desks is 55% full. Rent rises by 500. Questions: New break-even fill rate? What price or plan change reaches it? Which safety item is the most urgent in a full room? Write a one-page plan for the owner.

## Check questions (draft set, 10)
1. What is a fill rate? 2. How do you find break-even? 3. Name five costs of running a space. 4. Why are three plans enough? 5. Name two safety items. 6. What rules would you write first? 7. How can you build community cheaply? 8. Income per desk: 400 per month, 20 desks, 70% full (same currency). Monthly income? 9. What did the operator say you missed? 10. Scale, change or stop: what is your decision?

## Law check (DRAFT, not legal advice)
Points to check for this course: Building use, fire safety, occupancy limits and first-aid requirements of your area; business registration.
Rules differ by country and change. Write down the rule you rely on, the official source (use the official portal where the rules are unclear) and the date you checked. Ask an adviser before acting for real. Everything in the course stays test, made-up or consented material.

## Rules for this course
Check local building, fire and business rules with an adviser before opening to the public. No brand names or logos of other companies on any page or slide. No personal data in shared files. No medical, legal or investment advice.
