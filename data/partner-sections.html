how we work with partners

One programme, many local champions

House for Learning runs each programme through a simple yearly cycle that partners and learners can see from the start.

  1. Name a champion.Each country or region names a training champion who is the first point of contact for that place.
  2. Agree the classes.Classes are set up and the way each class will be assessed is agreed up front.
  3. Bring in the modules.New modules are submitted and reviewed. Each is checked for whether it needs local adaptation or translation before launch approval, then passed to the online team.
  4. Enrol and monitor partners.Partners enrol, progress is tracked, and a partner is assigned the platform access and licences they need.
  5. Measure by country.Results are looked at in numbers first, then in words: where the gaps are, why, and what to do. A quarterly review ends with an action plan and sign-off.
  6. Recognise good work.Partners and learners who perform well can be recommended and can add their comments in the tool, which feeds the HFL Chartered Practitioner role. Certified records move to the record-keeping platform for later proof.

We update the process every quarter, with changes tracked, and adapt to local standards wherever they apply.
