# Flagship detail: Accountancy, Finance and insurance (DRAFT)

Read with `00-STANDARD.md`. Minutes add up to the course length. Practice files are invented. No fee is set.

## CPD-ACC-001 Reconcile accounts to a month-end standard (about 210 min)
**Modules:** 1 Why reconcile and what "done" means (20). 2 Reading a bank list and a ledger list (30). 3 Matching and timing differences (45). 4 The reconciliation statement layout (35). 5 Unusual items: spotting and explaining (30). 6 Full practice file (40). 7 Assessment (10).
**Case card 2:** Bank shows 14,820.00; ledger shows 15,240.00. A cheque of 600.00 is unpresented and a fee of 180.00 is unrecorded. Learners find the remaining gap and write the statement.
**More checks:** 1 What is a timing difference? *An item in one record this month and the other next month.* 2 What do you do with an unexplained 5.00? *Check against the set limit; record it and note it.* 3 Why keep the statement signed and dated? *It proves who checked and when.* 4 Name one unusual item. *A round-sum payment, a duplicate, a payment on a weekend.* 5 Who approves a write-off? *The person named in the approval card, never the preparer.*
**Glossary:** reconciliation, unpresented cheque, timing difference, write-off, approval card.

## CPD-ACC-002 Analyse ratios for a board (about 180 min)
**Modules:** 1 What a board wants in one page (20). 2 Six ratios and their formulas (45). 3 Comparing with last year (30). 4 Explaining a change in plain words (30). 5 Building the one-page summary (35). 6 Assessment (20).
**Case card 2:** Current ratio fell from 1.8 to 1.2 while sales rose 10 percent. Learners give two possible reasons and the first question for the finance lead.
**More checks:** 1 Formula for the current ratio? *Current assets divided by current liabilities.* 2 Why compare with last year, not only a target? *Shows direction and pace.* 3 What if a ratio looks good but cash is low? *Check timing of receivables; a ratio alone can hide it.* 4 How many numbers on the board page? *Few; each with a one-line meaning.* 5 Who sees the working file? *Finance and the reviewer, with the source noted.*
**Glossary:** ratio, liquidity, margin, trend, source note.

## CPD-ACC-003 Explain a tax change to a client (about 100 min)
**Modules:** 1 Find and check the rule: source and date (20). 2 The 100-word summary (25). 3 A worked example with a sample client (25). 4 Citing and dating the note (15). 5 Practice and feedback (10). 6 Check (5).
**Case card 2:** A new rule shifts a filing date by 30 days. Learners write the client note and say what they verified first.
**More checks:** 1 Why state the date of the rule? *Rules change; the date shows what applied.* 2 Can you advise without checking the source? *No.* 3 Why use one worked example? *Clients understand numbers better than clauses.* 4 What if the rule is unclear? *Say so, and refer to the senior.* 5 Where is the note stored? *In the client file with the source.*
**Glossary:** rule, source, worked example, client note, refer.

## CPD-FIN-001 Identify conflicts of interest (about 100 min)
**Modules:** 1 What a conflict is, with everyday examples (20). 2 Eight short cases (30). 3 Disclosure wording (20). 4 Recording the decision and approver (15). 5 Practice (10). 6 Check (5).
**Case card 2:** You are asked to recommend a fund managed by a company your cousin runs. Learners decide, disclose, and record.
**More checks:** 1 Is a conflict always wrong? *No; it must be spotted, disclosed and managed.* 2 When disclose? *Before advice, in writing.* 3 Who decides? *The named approver, not the adviser.* 4 What if unsure? *Treat as a conflict and ask.* 5 Where is it recorded? *The conflicts register.*
**Glossary:** conflict, disclosure, approver, register, client interest.

## CPD-FIN-002 Record a suitability review (about 200 min)
**Modules:** 1 Why a review exists (20). 2 Facts to collect from the client card (40). 3 Matching needs to a product type (45). 4 Writing the reason in plain words (30). 5 The advice record checklist (30). 6 Practice file (25). 7 Assessment (10).
**Case card 2:** A client aged 62 wants growth but needs the money in 3 years. Learners record facts, match, and explain the reason.
**More checks:** 1 Name three facts always needed. *Goal, time horizon, ability to bear loss.* 2 What if the client declines to answer? *Record it and limit the advice.* 3 Why write a reason? *So another person can see why this fit.* 4 How long are records kept? *Per local retention rule.* 5 Who reviews the record? *A second checker as per policy.*
**Glossary:** suitability, horizon, risk tolerance, advice record, checker.

## CPD-FIN-003 Report suspicious activity (about 100 min)
**Modules:** 1 What warning signs look like (20). 2 Six sample transactions (30). 3 The local route card: who, how fast (20). 4 Not telling the customer (15). 5 Practice (10). 6 Check (5).
**Case card 2:** A customer deposits many small amounts just under a limit, then asks to move it abroad. Learners report using the card.
**More checks:** 1 Who do you tell? *The named officer on the route card.* 2 May you warn the customer? *No.* 3 What if you are wrong? *Reporting in good faith is expected.* 4 What do you record? *Facts you saw, with date and time.* 5 When report? *At once, within the card's time.*
**Glossary:** warning sign, route card, reporting officer, tipping off, good faith.
