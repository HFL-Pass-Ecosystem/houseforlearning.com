# Accountancy and Financial and insurance services: course detail (DRAFT)

Method and recording only. No tax, legal or investment advice. Figures invented. Rules differ by country and body; always check your own. Expert review before publish. No fees.

## ACC-001 Reconcile accounts to a month-end standard
**Worked example:** Bank balance 10,000; ledger cash balance 9,700. Items: cheque issued, not yet cleared (400); deposit made, not yet shown by the bank (100). Check: 10,000 - 400 + 100 = 9,700; ledger 9,700. Difference after reconciling: 0.
**Layout:** balance per bank; add/less timing items; adjusted balance; balance per ledger; add/less ledger items; adjusted balance; difference.
**Case card:** a practice file of 12 bank lines and 12 ledger lines with 3 planted differences.
**Checks:** 1 Why reconcile monthly? *To catch errors and fraud early.* 2 A cheque issued but not cleared affects which balance? *Bank balance side (deduct it).* 3 What do you do with an unexplained 50? *Investigate, record it and escalate if above the limit.* 4 Who reviews? *A second person, per your policy.*

## ACC-002 Analyse ratios for a board
**Worked example:** Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities = 600 / 400 = 1.5. Last year 1.8. Plain-words note: "Liquidity fell because supplier balances rose faster than cash. Action: review payment timing."
**Six ratios:** current, quick, gross margin, net margin, debtor days, creditor days.
**Case card:** two years of invented statements; learners compute 6 ratios and write a one-page summary.
**Checks:** 1 Formula for gross margin? *(Revenue - cost of sales) / revenue.* 2 A falling current ratio means? *Less cover for short-term debts; look at why.* 3 Why compare to last year? *A single number means little without a reference.* 4 Name one limit of ratios. *They depend on accounting choices and one-off items.*

## ACC-003 Explain a tax change to a client
**Worked example (about 100 words):** "From the next period the rule on X changes so that Y. For you, using last year's figures, this would mean about Z more/less. The date it starts is D. Please keep records of Q. Source: [official notice, date]."
**Case card:** a short invented rule change and client profile.
**Checks:** 1 What must the note cite? *The official source and its date.* 2 Why give a worked example? *It shows the effect in the client's terms.* 3 What if you are unsure? *Say so, check the source, escalate.* 4 Do you give advice beyond your role? *No; refer to a qualified adviser.*

## FIN-001 Identify conflicts of interest
**Worked example:** an adviser earns extra if a client picks product P. Conflict: personal gain could influence advice. Disclosure wording (approved by your firm) is given before advice; decision and approver are recorded.
**Case card:** 8 short cases; learners mark conflict/not conflict and the action.
**Checks:** 1 Define a conflict of interest. *When personal or firm gain could affect your duty to the client.* 2 First step? *Declare it per policy.* 3 What do you record? *The conflict, the action and the approver.* 4 Is a conflict always a breach? *No; unmanaged or undisclosed ones are the problem.*

## FIN-002 Record a suitability review
**Worked example record:** client goals; risk comfort in the client's words; facts (income band, time horizon); need matched to a product type with a written reason; alternatives considered; client confirmation.
**Case card:** an invented client card; learners complete the record to the checklist.
**Checks:** 1 Why record the reason? *To show the match was thought through.* 2 What facts are essential? *Goals, time horizon, comfort with loss, financial position.* 3 What if facts are missing? *Do not proceed until gathered.* 4 Who signs? *The adviser and, where required, the client.*

## FIN-003 Report suspicious activity
**Worked example warning signs:** cash deposits just under a reporting limit; sudden unexplained wealth; reluctance to give identity details.
**Rule:** follow your internal route; do not tell the customer ("tipping off" is an offence in many places; check yours).
**Case card:** 6 invented transactions; learners mark concern/no concern and write a report in four lines.
**Checks:** 1 Who do you report to? *Your named reporting officer or route.* 2 May you tell the customer? *No.* 3 What goes in the report? *Who, what, when, why it worries you.* 4 Do you need proof? *No; reasonable suspicion is enough to report.*
