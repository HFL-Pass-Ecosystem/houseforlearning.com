# Flagship detail: Medical and healthcare, Social care, Teaching (DRAFT)

Read with `00-STANDARD.md`. Minutes add up to the course length. Module lists below follow the course data.

## CPD-MED-001 Plain English for ward handovers (180 min)
**Modules:** 1 Why handovers fail, with two invented near-miss stories (25). 2 The SBAR order, speaking it in 60 seconds (35). 3 Repeat-back and clarifying questions (25). 4 Plain words and approved abbreviations (30). 5 Practice role-plays with feedback (30). 6 Assessment and reflection (15). 7 Second worked case (10). 8 Teach-back and peer check (10).
**Case card 2:** Bed 3, Patient L, going for a scan at 2 pm, needs a porter, wears a hearing aid, daughter phoned twice. Learners hand over to a colleague in under 2 minutes.
**More checks:** 1 What belongs in "Background"? *Only history needed for this handover.* 2 Why keep it under two minutes? *Attention drops; focus on what must change.* 3 What do you do if the listener looks confused? *Slow down, rephrase, ask a repeat-back.* 4 Name a risk of unapproved abbreviations. *Two readers may take different meanings.* 5 Who confirms an open task? *You name the person; they confirm out loud.*
**Glossary:** handover, SBAR, repeat-back, open task, near miss.

## CPD-MED-002 Recording care notes to the standard (90 min)
**Modules:** 1 What good notes do and why (15). 2 Facts, time, signature (20). 3 Correcting errors and late entries (15). 4 Privacy and need-to-know (15). 5 Write-and-score practice (10). 6 Check and reflection (5). 7 Second worked case: the refused meal (5). 8 Peer review and portfolio (5).
**Case card 2:** A resident refused a meal at 12:30 and said, "I'm not hungry." Staff offered again at 13:15; accepted soup. Write the note.
**More checks:** 1 What is a late entry? *A note added after the event; mark it as late with the real time of writing.* 2 May you write for someone else? *Only with a clear policy; normally each author records their own.* 3 Why avoid "appeared to"? *It mixes opinion with fact unless backed by what you saw.* 4 How long do notes stay? *Per local retention rules.* 5 What if you spot a colleague's error? *Tell them and the lead; do not alter their entry.*
**Glossary:** fact, opinion, late entry, retention, need-to-know.

## CPD-MED-003 Talking with patients and families in a second language (240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Respect, names and greetings (30). 2 Explaining a step in plain words (40). 3 Worry and feelings: Listen, Acknowledge, Explain, Check (40). 4 Interpreters and when to use them (25). 5 Escalating a concern in writing and by voice (30). 6 Role-play practice (45). 7 Assessment and reflection (20). 8 Second worked case: the closed question (10).
**Case card 2:** A son asks you to "tell him everything" about his mother's condition; policy says only the nurse in charge shares details. Learners respond and escalate.
**More checks:** 1 When use an interpreter? *When understanding is not secure or the topic is sensitive; use an approved service.* 2 Is it fine to use a child to translate? *No.* 3 How do you handle an accent you cannot follow? *Ask politely to repeat, then check back.* 4 What is a closed question? *One that gets yes/no; use open ones to learn more.* 5 How do you end the talk? *Summarize, ask for questions, say what happens next.*
**Glossary:** interpreter, plain words, escalate, consent to share, closed question.

## SOC-001 Assess risk in a home visit (240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Purpose and limits of a visit (25). 2 Seeing: signs of risk (45). 3 Rating on the 3-level scale (30). 4 Talking with the person respectfully (30). 5 Recording the visit (30). 6 Practice with case cards (35). 7 Assessment (15). 8 Second worked case and lone working (30).
**Case card 2:** Person M, 76, smells of damp, heating off, says "I'm fine." Learners list signs, rate, name the first action.
**More checks:** 1 Why start with the person's view? *They know their life best.* 2 Who else do you tell about a High rating? *The manager the same day.* 3 What is a protective factor? *Something that lowers risk, e.g. a daily visitor.* 4 What if you feel unsafe? *Leave and report per lone-working policy.* 5 How long after a visit do you write the note? *As soon as possible, the same day.*
**Glossary:** risk, protective factor, rating, lone working, capacity.

## SOC-002 Document a safeguarding concern (180 min)
**Modules:** 1 What counts as a concern (15). 2 Writing facts only (25). 3 Who to tell and how fast (20). 4 Confidentiality and sharing (15). 5 Practice (10). 6 Check (5). 7 Second worked case: the child and the bruise (45). 8 Peer review and reflection (45).
**Case card 2:** A child at a day centre says a bruise came "from Dad's belt". Learners write the report exactly and name the route.
**More checks:** 1 Do you question further? *No; listen, record, report.* 2 Use the person's words? *Yes, in quotes.* 3 Is a delay acceptable? *No; follow the stated time.* 4 What if the lead is the concern? *Use the alternative route in policy.* 5 Do you tell the person what happens next? *Yes, as far as is safe.*
**Glossary:** safeguarding, disclosure, quote, escalation, confidentiality.

## SOC-003 Plan a person-centred review (180 min)
**Modules:** 1 What person-centred means (25). 2 Preparing and inviting (25). 3 Running the review: person first and last (40). 4 Setting goals with actions, owners, dates (30). 5 Recording and sharing (20). 6 Practice (20). 7 Assessment (10). 8 Second worked case and reflection (10).
**Case card 2:** Person N wants to go to a weekly choir but transport is a barrier. Learners plan agenda and three actions.
**More checks:** 1 Who owns an action? *A named person.* 2 What if the family disagree with the person? *Record both views; the person's wishes lead unless capacity rules apply.* 3 How do you make the room welcoming? *Seating, time, quiet, support person.* 4 When is the next review set? *Before the meeting ends.* 5 How to record outcomes? *Plain language in the person's words.*
**Glossary:** person-centred, goal, action, advocate, review.

## TCH-001 Design a lesson with measurable outcomes (180 min)
**Modules:** 1 Outcomes and Bloom verbs (30). 2 Alignment: outcome, activity, check (35). 3 Designing a 5-item check (30). 4 Timing and flow (25). 5 Practice: draft your lesson (30). 6 Peer feedback and assessment (10). 7 Second worked case (10). 8 Portfolio and reflection (10).
**Case card 2:** A Year 7 science topic, "states of matter". Write 3 outcomes and one check each.
**More checks:** 1 Rewrite "learn about volcanoes" as an outcome. *"Describe three causes of eruptions in 3 sentences using terms from the word list."* 2 Why only one verb per outcome? *Keeps measurement clear.* 3 What is misalignment? *An activity that does not lead to the outcome.* 4 How many outcomes per lesson? *Usually 2 to 4.* 5 What to do if few learners meet the outcome? *Re-teach and re-check.*
**Glossary:** outcome, alignment, Bloom verb, exit check, re-teach.

## TCH-002 Evaluate learning with short checks (180 min)
**Modules:** 1 Why short checks (15). 2 Choosing a check type (25). 3 Reading results quickly (20). 4 Planning a re-teach (20). 5 Practice (5). 6 Check (5). 7 Second worked case: the results table (45). 8 Peer check and class tracker (45).
**Case card 2:** A results table: Q1 95, Q2 60, Q3 55, Q4 90. Learners pick the weakest two and plan the re-teach.
**More checks:** 1 One quick check with no equipment. *Hands-up or finger votes.* 2 What if 100 percent get it right? *Move on or extend.* 3 Why avoid reading out marks? *Protects dignity.* 4 How soon re-check? *Within a week.* 5 Where to record patterns? *A simple class tracker.*
**Glossary:** formative check, exit slip, pattern, re-teach, tracker.

## TCH-003 Adapt a lesson for mixed ability (180 min)
**Modules:** 1 Same outcome, different routes (25). 2 Scaffolds: prompts, word banks, pictures (35). 3 Extension for fast learners (25). 4 Time and environment (25). 5 Practice: adapt your worksheet (30). 6 Assessment (20). 7 Second worked case: the reading task (10). 8 Teach-back and portfolio (10).
**Case card 2:** A reading task; one learner is new to the language, another reads fluently. Create three versions.
**More checks:** 1 What is scaffolding? *Temporary support that is removed as skill grows.* 2 Name two ways to extend. *Open question, ask them to teach or create a problem.* 3 Does extra time change the standard? *No.* 4 How do you record the plan? *A short note linked to the learner need.* 5 Who agrees adaptations? *Teacher with the support lead, per policy.*
**Glossary:** scaffold, extension, differentiation, support plan, standard.
