# Flagship detail: IT and telecom, Legal, Scientists, Beauty and therapies (DRAFT)

Read with `00-STANDARD.md`. Minutes add up to the course length. All cases are invented. No fee is set. Beauty items follow local law and are not medical advice.

## CPD-ITC-001 Configure a secure network (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Zones: staff, guest, devices (30). 2 Drawing a small network plan (40). 3 Standard settings in the lab (50). 4 Passing the check list (35). 5 Writing the repeat-it guide (40). 6 Assessment (25). 7 Second worked case: a cafe till kept apart (10). 8 Teach-back and peer check (10).
**Case card 2:** A café wants guest Wi-Fi that cannot reach the till. Learners design the zones and list settings.
**More checks:** 1 Why separate guests? *A guest device cannot reach staff systems.* 2 What is a default password risk? *Known to attackers; change at install.* 3 What to log? *Changes and failed sign-ins.* 4 Who approves a rule change? *The owner or change approver.* 5 Why write the guide? *Another person can repeat it.*
**Glossary:** zone, firewall rule, default setting, log, repeatable build.

**Case card 2:** A staff laptop sends data to an unknown address at 3 am. Learners triage, contain, and report.
**More checks:** 1 First step? *Confirm and contain; do not delete evidence.* 2 Who is told? *The incident lead per playbook.* 3 Severity based on what? *Data affected and spread.* 4 What goes in the report? *Time, what happened, action, next step.* 5 When close? *After recovery and review.*
**Glossary:** alert, triage, containment, playbook, evidence.

## CPD-LEG-001 Draft a clear client letter (about 180 min)
**Modules:** 1 Who reads it and what they need (15). 2 Plain words under one page (30). 3 Readability test (20). 4 Next step and date (20). 5 Practice (10). 6 Check (5). 7 Second case: a moved hearing (45). 8 Teach-back and portfolio (35).
**Case card 2:** Explain that a hearing is moved by two weeks and what the client must bring. Learners write the letter.
**More checks:** 1 Why one page? *Readers stop after a page.* 2 Replace "pursuant to". *"Under".* 3 What always ends the letter? *Next step and date.* 4 Do you include legal Latin? *Avoid it; if you must, explain it in plain words.* 5 Who reviews? *A second person per firm policy.*
**Glossary:** plain words, readability, next step, deadline, review.

## CPD-LEG-002 Review a contract clause by clause (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Contract parts (25). 2 One-line summaries (40). 3 Spotting risk clauses (45). 4 Reasons and priority (30). 5 Safer wording (40). 6 Assessment (30). 7 Second case: a price-change clause (15). 8 Peer check and reflection (15).
**Case card 2:** A clause lets the supplier raise price at any time. Learners flag, explain, and propose wording.
**More checks:** 1 What is a risk clause? *One that exposes you to cost or loss.* 2 Name two. *Unlimited liability, auto-renewal.* 3 How propose safer wording? *Add a cap, notice period or consent.* 4 Who decides? *The client, with advice from a qualified lawyer.* 5 What if you are unsure of the law? *Refer to the senior.*
**Glossary:** clause, liability, indemnity, notice, renewal.

## CPD-SCI-001 Design a repeatable test (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Question and hypothesis (25). 2 Controls and measures (40). 3 How many repeats (40). 4 Writing the method (45). 5 Peer check (35). 6 Assessment (25). 7 Second case: a coating and corrosion (20). 8 Teach-back and reflection (10).
**Case card 2:** Does a coating cut corrosion? Learners define control, measure, repeats and write steps.
**More checks:** 1 What is a control? *The comparison without the change.* 2 Why repeats? *To see natural variation.* 3 How detailed is a method? *Another lab can repeat it exactly.* 4 What if one sample is odd? *Record it; do not remove without a stated rule.* 5 Name one bias. *Choosing samples by hand.*
**Glossary:** hypothesis, control, repeat, variable, bias.

## CPD-SCI-002 Analyse results with error bars (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Means and spread (40). 2 Plotting with labels (35). 3 Error bars: what they show (35). 4 Is the difference real? (40). 5 Writing the reason (25). 6 Assessment (25). 7 Second case: overlapping results (25). 8 Teach-back and raw data check (15).
**Case card 2:** Two groups: 12.1 ± 0.4 and 12.6 ± 0.5. Learners judge overlap and say what to do next.
**More checks:** 1 What does an error bar show? *Spread or uncertainty, stated in the caption.* 2 Do overlapping bars prove no difference? *Not alone; use a stated test.* 3 Axis label needs? *Name and unit.* 4 Why keep raw data? *To re-check.* 5 What if n is small? *Say results are tentative.*
**Glossary:** mean, spread, error bar, uncertainty, sample size.
