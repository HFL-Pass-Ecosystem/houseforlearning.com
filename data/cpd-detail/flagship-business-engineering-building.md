# Flagship detail: Business, Engineering, Building (DRAFT)

Read with `00-STANDARD.md`. Minutes add up to the course length. All cases are invented. No fee is set.

## CPD-BUS-001 Lead a team through change (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Why change fails (25). 2 Mapping who is affected (35). 3 Three messages: time and sender (40). 4 Handling worry and resistance (35). 5 Three simple measures (30). 6 Case practice (25). 7 Assessment (10). 8 Second case: a shift change for 40 staff (40).
**Case card 2:** A shift pattern changes for 40 staff in 3 weeks. Learners list groups affected and write the first message.
**More checks:** 1 Who hears bad news first? *The most affected, from their own manager.* 2 Why a sender for each message? *People trust a known voice.* 3 Name two measures. *Attendance at briefings, questions answered, tasks done on time.* 4 What if resistance stays high? *Ask what is behind it; adjust the plan.* 5 When to stop measuring? *When the change is routine.*
**Glossary:** stakeholder, resistance, message plan, measure, sponsor.

## CPD-BUS-002 Negotiate a supplier contract (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Target, limit, walk-away (30). 2 Three trades you can offer (35). 3 Listening and questions (30). 4 Role-play 1 (35). 5 Role-play 2 with a twist (40). 6 One-page summary for sign-off (30). 7 Assessment (10). 8 Second case: an early-payment offer (30).
**Case card 2:** The supplier offers a 5 percent discount if you pay in 10 days instead of 45. Learners decide using cash cost.
**More checks:** 1 What is a limit? *The worst deal you accept.* 2 Name a trade. *Longer term for lower price.* 3 Why summarize in writing? *Avoids later disputes.* 4 What if you hit your limit? *Pause or leave; do not cross it.* 5 Who signs? *The person with authority, per policy.*
**Glossary:** target, limit, trade, walk-away, sign-off.

## CPD-BUS-003 Write a one-page business case (about 180 min)
**Modules:** 1 The one-page shape (15). 2 Problem and options (25). 3 Costs and benefits with sources (25). 4 Recommendation and main risk (20). 5 Practice and feedback (10). 6 Check (5). 7 Second case: replace 12 laptops now or later (45). 8 Teach-back and peer check (35).
**Case card 2:** Replace 12 old laptops now or in a year. Learners write the page.
**More checks:** 1 Why name sources? *So the reader can check them.* 2 How many options? *Usually three, including do nothing.* 3 What belongs under risk? *The biggest threat and how to reduce it.* 4 How long is the page? *One page.* 5 What if numbers are guesses? *Mark them as estimates.*
**Glossary:** option, cost, benefit, estimate, recommendation.

## CPD-ENG-001 Select a cable to the standard (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Load, current and the safety margin (35). 2 Voltage drop and tolerance (40). 3 Reading the sample table (35). 4 Choosing size and type, citing the clause (40). 5 Calculation sheet (30). 6 Three practice loads (40). 7 Assessment (10). 8 Teach-back and peer check (10).
**Case card 2:** A 22 kW load 60 m from the board, voltage drop limit stated on the sheet. Learners calculate and choose.
**More checks:** 1 Why a margin on current? *Heat, grouping and ageing reduce safe capacity.* 2 What if voltage drop is over the limit? *Use a larger size or shorten the run.* 3 Where is the choice recorded? *On the calculation sheet with the clause.* 4 Who checks the sheet? *A second competent person.* 5 Can you copy last year's sheet? *Only after re-checking the inputs.*
**Glossary:** load, ampacity, voltage drop, clause, derating.

## CPD-ENG-002 Check a protection setting (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 One-line diagram basics (30). 2 Device order from source to load (30). 3 Coordination curves (45). 4 Checking a setting (35). 5 Reporting a mismatch and next check (25). 6 Assessment (15). 7 Second worked case: crossed curves (30). 8 Teach-back and peer check (30).
**Case card 2:** A downstream breaker trips later than the upstream. Learners mark the curves, state the mismatch and next step.
**More checks:** 1 What is coordination? *Only the device nearest the fault trips.* 2 Who may change a setting? *Only an authorised person, with a change record.* 3 What do you report? *Setting, expected value, difference, location.* 4 Why list devices in order? *To trace which one should act first.* 5 What if you cannot verify? *Say so; do not guess.*
**Glossary:** one-line diagram, coordination, trip curve, setting, change record.

## CPD-ENG-003 Prepare a site test record (about 180 min)
**Modules:** 1 Which tests and why (20). 2 Filling the test sheet (30). 3 Pass or fail against limits (20). 4 Signing and storing (15). 5 Practice (10). 6 Check (5). 7 Second worked case: a reading just under the limit (40). 8 Teach-back and peer check (40).
**Case card 2:** An insulation reading is just under the limit. Learners judge and record.
**More checks:** 1 Can a field stay blank? *No; write "not applicable" with reason.* 2 What if a reading fails? *Record it, flag it, do not hide it.* 3 Who signs? *The tester and the checker.* 4 Why record the instrument? *Calibration and traceability.* 5 How long are records kept? *Per project and legal rule.*
**Glossary:** test sheet, limit, calibration, traceability, sign-off.

## CPD-BLD-001 Inspect a site for safety (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Hazard, risk, control (30). 2 Spotting hazards in photos (45). 3 Ranking by risk with a reason (35). 4 Action list: owner and date (30). 5 Walk-through practice (50). 6 Assessment (20). 7 Second worked case: open edge, cables and bricks (15). 8 Teach-back and peer check (15).
**Case card 2:** Photos show an open edge, loose cables, and stacked bricks near a path. Learners rank and assign actions.
**More checks:** 1 What is the best control? *Remove the hazard first; protective equipment is the last resort.* 2 Who gets the action list? *The site lead and each owner.* 3 When re-inspect? *At the date set or after any change.* 4 What do you do for imminent danger? *Stop work and tell the lead now.* 5 Why photograph? *Evidence of what was found.*
**Glossary:** hazard, risk, control, imminent danger, action owner.

## CPD-BLD-002 Estimate quantities (about 240 min)
**Modules:** 1 Reading scale and units (30). 2 Measuring areas and lengths (45). 3 Waste allowance (25). 4 Quantity calculations (40). 5 Bill of quantities layout (35). 6 Assessment (25). 7 Second worked case: paint for the walls (25). 8 Teach-back and peer check (15).
**Case card 2:** A 6 m by 4.5 m floor needs tiles of 0.3 m by 0.3 m, 8 percent waste. Learners compute the number of tiles and boxes of 10.
**More checks:** 1 Floor area of 6 m by 4.5 m? *27 square metres.* 2 Why add waste? *Cuts, breakage, mistakes.* 3 What is a bill of quantities? *A list of items, units and amounts.* 4 What if the drawing and note disagree? *Query, do not assume.* 5 Rounding rule? *Round up to full units you can buy.*
**Glossary:** scale, area, waste allowance, bill of quantities, query.

## CPD-BLD-003 Prepare a handover pack (about 180 min)
**Modules:** 1 What clients need to keep (20). 2 Checking for gaps (30). 3 Building the index (20). 4 Walk-through with the client (15). 5 Practice (10). 6 Check (5). 7 Second worked case: missing warranty and test record (40). 8 Teach-back and peer check (40).
**Case card 2:** The pack lacks warranty papers and one test record. Learners find gaps and list the chase.
**More checks:** 1 Name three documents. *Drawings, warranties, test records.* 2 Why an index? *So the client can find items later.* 3 Who signs the handover? *The client or their representative signs to accept; the project lead hands over.* 4 What if a document is late? *List it as outstanding with a date.* 5 Where stored? *Both client copy and project archive.*
**Glossary:** handover, warranty, as-built, outstanding item, index.
