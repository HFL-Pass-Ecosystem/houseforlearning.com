Millions of books, magazines, audio and video are free to read or borrow at the Internet Archive (archive.org). No payment and no sign-in are needed to look around.

How to find and borrow a book

  1. Open the archive

    Tap the link above. It opens in a new tab.

  2. Search

    Type the book name, the author or a topic in the search box at the top. Tap the search button.

  3. Open a result

    If you see "Read online" or "Download", it is free to use at once.

  4. Borrow

    If you see "Borrow", make a free account on the Internet Archive. Tap "Borrow" and read it in your browser. Your loan returns by itself when the time ends.

  5. Read

    Tap the full-screen button to read. Use the arrows to turn pages.

Please respect copyright. Borrow or read only what the Internet Archive offers to you. Do not copy, share or sell books you borrow. Follow the rules shown on each book page and the laws of your country.

Please note: The Internet Archive is a separate, free, non-profit library. We do not own it, run it or charge for it, and we are not linked to it. It is free for everyone. Our subscription covers only our own services, never the Internet Archive.
